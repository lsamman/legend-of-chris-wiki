    Mic Q

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Does not exist. Never will.

    Mic Q
    ExistenceNone, in any universe
    Comparable toDwayne “The Rock” Johnson
    Being searched for byMedea
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Characters

    Mic Q is the object of the loser's punishment in the Donkey Kong Racing Duel. The book clarifies, in parentheses, that Mic Q is something “which never has, never will, and cannot, exist in any universe much like dwane the rock johnson.”

    This makes Mic Q one of the few entities in the canon whose non-existence is confirmed in all universes, including those in the Shadow Realm. Medea was sent to find him anyway and was never seen again.

    Trivia

    • Whether Mic Q is a person, a microphone, or a letter of the alphabet is unknown. This wiki uses “him” provisionally.
