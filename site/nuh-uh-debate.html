    The Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate is the climax of the Siege of the Galactic Senate and the longest exchange of dialogue in The Legend of Chris. After RFK declared "It's over Bon Jovi I have 4 Red Bulls and a lightsaber", "Bon Jovi looked around and proclaimed 'Nuh Uh'", and the debate took up nearly a full page.

    Contents
    1. Structure
    2. Resolution
    3. Legacy
    4. Trivia

    Structure

    The debate opens with "Nuh Uh" "Yuh huh", and then settles into a strict pattern of two "Nuh Uh"s for every one "Yuh huh". By this wiki's count there are 202 "Nuh Uh"s and 101 "Yuh huh"s, an exact 2:1 ratio, for 303 utterances in total.

    The 2:1 ratio has led to analysis. Since the book does not attribute the lines, the most common reading is that Bon Jovi is "Nuh Uh" and RFK is "Yuh huh". This means Bon Jovi spoke twice as often as RFK, and lost anyway.

    Resolution

    The debate is not concluded on the page. Chapter 2 simply ends mid-argument, and Chapter Triangle begins: "Bon Jovi Died at the hands of RFK." Whether the debate itself killed Bon Jovi, or whether RFK used his lightsaber or his 4 Red Bulls, is one of the canon's open questions.

    Legacy

    • TON 618, the Projects' flagship robot, stands for "Terrible Offensive Nuhuh". Fans consider this evidence that the "Nuh Uh" is a weapon.
    • It is the clearest case of RFK's Jedi battle tactics in action, since the Jedi are also known for long conversations.

    Trivia

    • The page count of the debate is greater than the page count of the Attack on Moscow, the Fall of Constantinople and the cloning of Chris combined (the wiki has not measured this precisely).
