    Constantinople is a city held by the Barack Empire and the target of The Goober Gang's only known mission. It fell at 14:74pm Unc Standard Time in the Fall of Constantinople.

    Contents
    1. The plan
    2. Geography

    The plan

    Mr. Bean laid out the plan in England:

    • Enter Constantinople and "plant several nukes around it".
    • "break the main oil lines to the city causing flooding of the city".
    • "once the city is one big oil pool we lure Obamna into the center and light the nukes".
    • Escape within 6.8 seconds "via a wormhole that theroreticly could open there".

    The canon does not say whether the oil part was ever done.

    Geography

    The city has a "nadir side", where two old abandoned Walmarts served as nuke drop points. One was guarded by the local mafia, and the other had become a Popeyes. On the outskirts are "the target parks", where the road "had really deteriorated", the grass was dying, and electricity "refused to exist here bc this place was mid at best". 8aller Jump Street served as the extraction point. North of DaBaby's last known location was the escape wormhole.

    Inhabitants

    • Vladimir Putin, who was near the center and drinking coffee
    • The local mafia
    • A Popeyes cashier
    • DaBaby, vaporized, in a suspended animation cell

    Trivia

    • In real history, Constantinople (now Istanbul) famously fell in 1453. The canon's fall took place at a time that does not exist.
    • "Nadir" means the lowest point, so it is not clear how a city has a nadir side.
