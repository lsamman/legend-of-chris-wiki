    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Keynote reality.

    Reality 42-B
    TypeReality

    Notable eventThe iPhone 3G Keynote
    First appearanceThe Flashback Chapter
    Locations

    Reality 42-B is the reality in which Steve Jobs held the iPhone 3G Keynote. The full address is "Reality 42-B, Shadow-Filtered Theater, Cupertino, California, United Multiverses."

    Notes

    • The address implies that the multiverse has been politically unified as the "United Multiverses", which contrasts with the Multiverse Civil War of the origin story.
    • 42 is famously "the answer to life, the universe and everything" in The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy. Whether a Reality 42-A exists is unknown.
