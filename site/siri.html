    Siri (3G)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Never update.

    Siri (3G)

    Characters

    Siri, credited as SIRI 3G, is the voice assistant of the iPhone 3G. She is part oracle and part ship's computer, and is Steve Jobs's most reliable crew member.

    Contents
    1. The prophecy
    2. Ship's computer
    3. Trivia

    The prophecy

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, with the world gone silent, Steve asked: "Hey Siri, how do we defeat Chris?" Siri gave the three-part answer quoted above. All three parts later come up:

    • Begin the Mixtape: fulfilled when 808s and Apocalypses dropped.
    • Sync with the Flip Phone Covenant: the crew's goal in the TurboGrafx 16 sector. See The Flip Phone Covenant.
    • Never update: still in force, as far as anyone knows.

    Ship's computer

    Siri also operates the Inav:

    • Steve's command "Hey Siri, locate the Inav" summoned the ship to Gotham.
    • The request "Hey Siri get me some diet dr.pepper" deployed a hovering coaster with an ice cold Diet Dr Pepper.
    • When Steve asked how Chris could be so blind, Siri answered: "The world will never know." Siri then warned: "Steve we must blip soon this city Is unstable."
    • Ordered to the casino, Siri replied "Right away sir."
    • On "Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam", Siri fired the Ibeam.
    • On "Take us to.. The Turbo Graphx 16 sector", Siri replied "Teleporting sir."

    Trivia

    • Siri is Apple's real voice assistant. It debuted in 2011 on the iPhone 4S and never ran on the real iPhone 3G, so SIRI 3G is a canon-exclusive build.
    • "The world will never know" is a line from an old Tootsie Pop commercial.
