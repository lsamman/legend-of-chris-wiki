    ChatGPT (also "chat gpt" and "GPT") is Kanye West's AI companion, business adviser and rap partner. It is unfailingly supportive of Kanye, has a strong vocabulary, and is the canon's most cautious strategist.

    Contents
    1. Biography
    2. Continuity errors

    Biography

    Business adviser

    When Kanye worried that his song would not continue "the water gas trend", ChatGPT reassured him: "Of course sir, anyone will buy your product for your are baller".

    Gotham

    In Gotham, ChatGPT "booted up its existential sarcasm module" to report that "statistically 98.7% of Gotham is built from darkness, broken dreams, and Tim Burton's college notebooks." On meeting FS Martin Genius it "gasped in XML" and recognized him as the one "who made Vine loop in reverse in Timeline R". Lil Jokez claimed that "ChatGPT simps for lint". In response, ChatGPT began "Initializing 808s and Apocalypses", and its "speakers unfolded like mecha wings" for the battle.

    With Steve Jobs

    After the club was sent to the Shadow Realm, ChatGPT commented "Honestly this is not pogchamp lets be real". It asked Steve Jobs about the Inav so it could add it "to my GitHub respiratory". It was also the first to notice that "Gotham is disappearing". Inside a wormhole it said "Woah.. this is baller man im deadass".

    The heist

    At Sector 1/99 ChatGPT called the casino "one of the best casinos ive seen in a millennia, ever since that one in Vegas closed", and warned the team to "Be on earlert there could be kirks anywhere". It plugged into Jobs's iPhone to power the Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser, and fought the Kirks with an Igun. It also argued the case for defeating Chris to Hank Hill: "we would have less regulation in general, and in addition.. balling would be free."

    Doubts about Elon

    On the way to the TurboGrafx 16 Sector, ChatGPT disagreed with the plan to roll back to 3G at the last moment: "If we do that then we have to deal with elon when we deal with chris, if hes coming straight from the shadow realm then who know what he'll want."

    Continuity errors

    At the end of the Flashback Chapter, after Microsoft Bing explains the Verizon contract to Hank's crew, the canon says "'Aight bet boss' chat gpt said with a wojack ahh face." ChatGPT was not there. At that point it was with Kanye and Jobs in the Inav. Fan explanations include a typo for Jesse, a second ChatGPT, and the theory that ChatGPT, like the real one, is everywhere at once.

    Trivia

    • ChatGPT is a real AI chatbot made by OpenAI.
    • It mixes up "repository" and "respiratory", which is unusual for a language model.
