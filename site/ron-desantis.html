    Ron DeSantis, spelled “Ron destantis” and “Ron De Santis” in the same paragraph, is the man who killed Chris shortly after his birth and the protagonist of the opening of Chapter 2.

    Contents
    1. The stabbing
    2. Quest and destiny
    3. The Julian affair and the war
    4. Continuity notes

    The stabbing

    Chapter 2 opens: “Then he was stabbed to death by Ron destantis claiming he was gay because he was a homo-sapien.” This is a pun on homo sapiens, and it is the full extent of Ron's stated motive. See The Stabbing of Chris.

    Quest and destiny

    “Ron De Santis then started his quest to find the 73 Taco bell locations.” He “was destined for glory by the hands of trumpy the red,” a third party advocating Truth Social. Neither the quest nor the destiny is ever resolved.

    The Julian affair and the war

    After a redditor left a 0.5 on IMDb and Joeius Bidenias review-bombed it, “Joe then forced Ron to make Julian the native bird of the island of Logic and Retoric. Ron said no and resorted to Mark zuckerberg and elon musk to take back the byzantine empire of mexico.” The war was “long and hard,” featured an ethereal green dot, and was eventually interrupted by Medea.

    Continuity notes

    • Ron's paragraph appears twice in the book. Its first appearance is leaked into the middle of the Cycle of Chris in the Chapter Before Chapter 2, cutting off just before Medea's arrival, after which the litany restarts.
    • The book never connects “Ron said no” to the Byzantine war. Refusing to name a bird is apparently a sufficient cause for war.
