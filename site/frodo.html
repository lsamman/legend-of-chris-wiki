    Frodo Baggins

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Conqueror of Andromeda. Ate the universe.

    Frodo Baggins
    CampaignGalactic takeover of the Andromeda Galaxy
    ToolMaster Chef
    MealThe universe (sirloin steak with lobster tail)
    Baller statusRevoked forever
    Former residenceSector 1/99
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Characters

    Frodo Baggins is the hobbit who ate the universe. After Zuckerberg's Snap, “frodo appeared deep in the universe and started a galactic take over of the andromeda galaxy he used master chef to cook the universe into a sirloin steak with lobster tail. Frodo then ate it and left the face of the universe never to be baller again.”

    Contents
    1. Aftermath
    2. Later mention
    3. Trivia

    Aftermath

    Frodo's meal caused the Great Famine, during which Elon Mush died. See The Cooking of the Universe.

    Later mention

    In Chapter the Maybe Seventh, Steve Jobs names “Frodo Baggins” among the great minds who once called Sector 1/99 home. This is the only time his surname is given, and it confirms that “never to be baller again” did not mean never to gamble again.

    Trivia

    • In The Lord of the Rings, Frodo's loyal companion is Samwise Gamgee. In this canon, Samwise appears in the very next sentences as a fusion of two tech bros. Fans are divided on whether this is a coincidence.
