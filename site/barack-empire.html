    The Barack Empire

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Constantinople's management (former).

    The Barack Empire
    LeaderBarack Obama
    TerritoryConstantinople
    RivalsBernie Sanders, The Goober Gang
    StatusLost Constantinople at 14:74pm UST
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Factions & Organizations

    The Barack Empire is the power that held Constantinople before the Goober Gang's mission. Mr. Bean described the objective as "take Constantinople away from the Barack Empire and Restore Bernie Sander's control over that area", which implies the Empire took the city from Bernie Sanders at some earlier point.

    The Empire is named once. Its only known assets are the city and the 7 Chaos Emeralds held by its ruler. After the Fall of Constantinople its status is unknown.
