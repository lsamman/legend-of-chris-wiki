    The EU

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations

    The EU
    TypePolitical union
    Demanded byOsama Bin Bom'in
    Rejected byMF Vroom
    ReasonWrong continent
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Factions & Organizations

    The EU is a political union that Osama Bin Bom'in and his frat wanted O'Block to join. MF Vroom said no because “the Projects are in the americas not Europe”, which was the immediate cause of the O'Block Coup.

    After the coup, O'Block never formally joined. Under Osama it was instead “scaled to be a EU level nation” in the O Block 2 era. This suggests Osama wanted membership less than he wanted the size.

    Trivia

    • MF Vroom's objection is one of the canon's rare arguments based on real geography, in a setting where Moscow is Italian and Constantinople is ruled by the Barack Empire.
    • The EU's response to the demand is not recorded.
