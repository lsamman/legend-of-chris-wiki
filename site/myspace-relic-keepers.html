    Keepers of the MySpace Relics

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Guardians of a dead website. Heavily armed.

    Keepers of the MySpace Relics

    Notable operationThe Attack on Moscow
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Factions & Organizations

    The Keepers of the MySpace Relics is an order mentioned in Chapter 2. Its three known members are Osama Bin Bom'in, Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire. "Being keepers of the MySpace relics they wasnt cool with this C tier beef", meaning the DQ–McDonald's MySpace Beef, and they planned the Attack on Moscow to end it.

    The relics

    The book never says what the MySpace relics are. However, MySpace is one of the platforms whose "secret teachings" Chris studied in the origin litany, and "MySpace v1.3.3" is one of the sacred apps needed to defeat Chris in the Prophecy. If the Keepers hold an early version of MySpace, they may be one of the most important factions in the canon without anyone in the book realizing it.

    Fan theories
