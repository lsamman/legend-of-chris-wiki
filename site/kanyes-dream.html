    Kanye's Dream
    DreamerKanye West
    Visitor“A mysterious figure known as Travis Scott”
    Request“Squad wins for the boys tonight”
    Told toSteve Jobs, aboard the iNav
    First appearanceChapter Number New Jersey
    Events

    Kanye's dream is told at the start of Chapter Number New Jersey. Walking through the cabin of the iNav “redbull-less and sleep deprived”, Kanye explains “that in his dream last night a mysterious figure known as Travis Scott wanted him to get them squad wins for the boys tonight”.

    The morning after

    Steve asks, “How did you sleep Ye”. Kanye: “Felt like a victory royal in Fortnite season 2.” He then gets “his weed infused coffee to make him boogie this morning” and sees the two Tesla 2028 roadsters docked outside.

    Notes

    • The chapter's very first sentence is about someone else: Plankton wakes on hearing Ioannus Paranukus say he wants a career in accounting, “as a distant voice in his ear”. The next sentence moves to Kanye.
    • Chris was last traced to the Reformed Travis Scott Sector. The book does not say whether the dream is connected.
    • An earlier version of the chapter gave the dream to Plankton.
