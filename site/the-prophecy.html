    The Prophecy

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Six apps, one contract, one court, still you.

    The Prophecy
    TypeLegend / prophecy
    Told byMF Vroom

    Key itemVerizon contract

    First toldChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Concepts & Lore

    The Prophecy is the legend, told by MF Vroom in Chapter 4+.1+.1…, that sets out exactly what it takes to defeat Chris. It names a phone loaded with six sacred apps and the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text, promises a team filled with [DETERMINATION], and adds one condition: that “on the court, its still you.”

    Text

    MF Vroom recites it in full, sitting in Son's Accord as TON 618 wakes up:

    “Your here to work on defeating Chris right, the power of the projects will help you but according to legend If one has a phone capable of containing MySpace v1.3.3 Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition, Facebook (Pre-Cringe) Twitter Bluebird Variant (with no limits), Apple Podcasts 2, Settings (Full access), as well as the “The 3 year contract plan from Verizon for unlimited talk and text” You will be able to beat Chris on the court, your team will be filled with [DETERMINATION] and lebron james and elon musk and mark zuckerberg and Chris will fall, but only if despite everything, on the court, its still you.”

    Requirements

    The phone must contain:

    • MySpace v1.3.3
    • Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition
    • Facebook (Pre-Cringe)
    • Twitter Bluebird Variant (with no limits)
    • Apple Podcasts 2
    • Settings (Full access)
    • The 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text

    If these are met, the bearer will beat Chris on the court, and LeBron James, Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg and Chris will fall. These are the same four who, in the origin, balled themselves into the sky together.

    Progress

    • Verizon contract: obtained in the Casino Heist and now inside Kanye's Yeezy jacket and then the Inav.
    • Facebook (Pre-Cringe): located in the Projects National Archives on a 67GB microSD card. This is why, the heroes believe, Osama has released the TONs: “If he knows our end goal already and he has connections with Chris.. That has to be why.”
    • The phone: most likely the iPhone 3G, which Steve Jobs says is “the only phone capable of running” four of the apps. Steve also plans to “sync with the flip phone covenant, and then we start with the social media apps”.
    • The other apps: not yet located.

    Earlier versions

    In Chapter Triangle, Plankton and RFK already describe a similar requirement. The only person able to battle Chris is “one who has met with the tech bros and has downloaded the sacred social media apps onto there nokia I-4500 flip phone... and 3 year contract plan from version for unlimited talk and text with there cutting edge 3g network.” The Prophecy brings the list up to date and drops the Nokia. Fans disagree about whether the Nokia I-4500 or the iPhone 3G is the true vessel.

    Continuity errors

    • The iPhone 3G keynote lists four sacred apps. The Prophecy adds Apple Podcasts 2 and Settings (Full access) without explaining where they came from.
    • Apple Podcasts was founded by the four figures the Prophecy says will fall, and Settings was created by Elon Mush. The Prophecy therefore requires the enemy's own apps.
    • It is not clear whether the Verizon contract counts as an app.

    Behind the scenes

    The final clause, “despite everything, its still you”, quotes Undertale, where a mirror tells the player “Despite everything, it's still you.” [DETERMINATION] is also an Undertale idea. Both fit with the Deltarune references from Spamton earlier in the chapter.
