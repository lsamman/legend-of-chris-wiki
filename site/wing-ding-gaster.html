    Captain Wing Ding Gaster commands the S.S. Vessel. The narrator calls him "the man who speaks in hands". His mission is to find The Great Double Whopper with Cheese so that he can rule the Kanye System.

    Contents
    1. Chapter 2
    2. Trivia
    3. On the spin-off wiki

    Chapter 2

    Gaster is screaming at FRIEND when Vinny B. James and Tyrone wake aboard. A line of his speech appears as strange symbols. He accepts Vinny's help finding the Great Double Whopper with Cheese, leads shanties, and gets a "little oogie oogie boogie shiggle wiggle" from his rum. He cries "LAND HO" at the sight of an island, then yells that the land "CHEATED ON ME". Through his Captain Gaster's Spyglass he "spots Epstein's Temple on the island" and orders the ship back. He steers into the Little Saint James Sea on a theory that the island's secret was the Great Double Whopper with Cheese (The Little Saint James Detour).

    When the Roaring Knight knocks Vinny overboard, Gaster's ship "speeds off yet again".

    Trivia

    • His name comes from a character in the video game Undertale. In this story he is a loud pirate captain.
    • Five of his lines are printed twice: once in the narration and once in a black text box with his green face beside it.
    • His line of symbols is printed in the Wingdings typeface. One of its characters shows up as an empty box in the PDF itself.
