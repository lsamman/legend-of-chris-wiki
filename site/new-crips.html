    The New Crips is the gang army that RFK and Plankton plan to raise by putting MF Vroom back in power in the Projects. The narrator calls MF Vroom "the first member of the New Crips, Everyone's last hope of universalizing Balling".

    Origins

    While looking for allies against Chris, RFK briefly considers Mark Zuckerberg (whose "baller finger just was chill like that"), then decides: "nah bro screw that we're gonna enlist MF Vroom." His reasoning is that restoring Vroom would let them "harness the power of the gang army, more powerful than gang wars fueled by a new parks of nikes". Plankton finds this "a enticing idea". Because Vroom was last seen being exiled into the COD lobbies, the search leads to Call of Duty HQ deep inside Dunder Mifflin.

    Plankton approves the plan with the canon's most famous two words: "Aight Bet".

    Membership

    The group is never formally sworn in, but these figures are generally counted as members or allies:

    • MF Vroom, first member and rightful heir of the Projects Royal Family
    • RFK and Plankton, founders and recruiters
    • Ioannus Paranukus, a Jedi native to the projects who joins at COD HQ, "I wish to free my king!"
    • Son S. Sonionring, MF Vroom's right-hand man, who resurfaces at the Projects National Spaceport

    When Plankton meets Ioannus, he asks: "do you bid us harm or are you a desirable crip". This is the closest thing to a recruitment test on record.

    Opposition

    The New Crips are opposed by Osama Bin Bom'in, who exiled MF Vroom and took control of O'Block, his ambassador Tung Tung Tung Sahur, the Police Kirks, and the giant robot TON 618. By the end of the current canon the gang is heading for the palace to retrieve Facebook (Pre-Cringe).

    Trivia

    • The phrase "new parks of nikes" is probably a typo for "new pair of Nikes". The wiki keeps it as written.
    • Kanye West claims, separately, that "all the crips will be buying their gas" from his corner store. It is not known whether he means the New Crips.
    • At the casino, the guards "did the crip walk slowly down the stairs". No membership is implied.
