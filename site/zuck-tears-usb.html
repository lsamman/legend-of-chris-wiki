    The Zuck Tears USB is a USB stick labelled 'Zuck Tears (Do Not Install)', handed to Kanye West by FS Martin Genius in the alley outside Club Shadowban, with the words "This might help you inside."

    Contents
    1. Use
    2. Aftermath
    3. Trivia

    Use

    FS Martin Genius instructed Kanye to "Run it on stage," because "Jokez's autotune can't survive legacy formats." The text never actually shows Kanye plugging it in. During the Battle of Club Shadowban, Lil Jokez "morphed into a 2007 JPEG of Heath Ledger", which may be the legacy format taking effect.

    Aftermath

    After the battle, the club was found empty, dusty and seemingly abandoned for years. Steve Jobs concluded: "It seems like something happened with that usb drive, this room must've been sent to the shadow realm…" The USB thus has the distinction of being the only item in the canon to do exactly what its label warned against.

    Trivia

    • "Zuck" is Mark Zuckerberg, whose tears were also on display in FS Martin Genius's "ancient gif of Zuck crying in a Chili's". It is unknown whether the USB contains that gif.
    • Mark Zuckerberg is otherwise the one who sent all the universes to the Shadow Realm in Zuckerberg's Snap, so it is fitting that his tears do the same.
