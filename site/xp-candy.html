    XP Candy

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    XP Candy
    TypeCandy (snortable)
    Distributed byAlpha Mogger Beta
    Sold forKromer
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    XP candy was the product behind Alpha Mogger Beta's campus business at Darkworld State University. Pledges, MF Vroom among them, were hazed by being made to package it for the higher-ups. The business made “so much [kromer]”, and MF Vroom adds that “sometimes we even got to snort some ourselves.”

    What XP candy actually does is never explained. The name suggests it gives experience points.

    Behind the scenes

    • Pokémon has “Exp. Candy” items that give a Pokémon experience. Fans think the name comes from there. This is a fan theory.
    • XP candy is one of several substances the canon handles casually, next to galaxy gas, Mountain Dew and solidified purple juice.
