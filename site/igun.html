    The Igun

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Apple's take on sidearms.

    The Igun
    TypeFirearm
    ManufacturerPresumably Apple
    UsersSteve Jobs, Kanye West, ChatGPT
    Used againstCharlie Kirks
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Items & Technology

    The Igun is a weapon used during the Casino Heist. When the crew came upon 3-4 Charlie Kirks guarding the lower chambers of the casino, Steve Jobs announced "its time to count gang violence", leapt at the first one with his Igun and incapacitated him. The text specifies 37 shots.

    Notes

    • Kanye and ChatGPT incapacitated the other two "with their newly acquired Iguns." When and where they acquired them is never shown. Since ChatGPT has no stated hands, how it holds one is also unclear.
    • The count of "3-4" Kirks followed by "the other two" leaves the fate of the possible fourth Kirk unresolved.
    • Part of Apple's "i" product line in the canon, alongside the Ibeam, Inav and Ipouch/Ibag/Ipocket.
