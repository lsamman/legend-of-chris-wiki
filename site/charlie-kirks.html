    Charlie Kirks (plural; individually a Kirk) are "trained warriors" found defending the verison gift card in the lower chambers of The 6 7 Casino. They appear to exist in large numbers, as ChatGPT warns that "there could be kirks anywhere".

    Contents
    1. Casino encounter
    2. Tactics
    3. Trivia

    Casino encounter

    During the Casino Heist, Kanye West opened the door at the bottom of the stairwell and found "3-4 Charlie kirks" in the hallway. The Boys "deduced" that they "must be here to defend the verison gift card".

    Steve Jobs leapt onto the first Kirk with his Igun and incapacitated him. In response, "The other kirks quickly took out their T1 phones and opened their truth social accounts and started talking about the problem with abortion in democrats rans states." While they were posting, Kanye and ChatGPT incapacitated the other two with their newly acquired Iguns. The fight set off the casino alarms.

    More Kirks later arrived through the stairwell as the gang escaped into the Inav. Hank Hill noticed "dead kirks to the left hallway".

    Tactics

    • Kirks respond to combat by opening Truth Social on T1 phones. This is not effective.
    • They appear in groups and are capable of hearing fights from elsewhere in the building.
    • A variant, the Police Kirks, can transform into police cars.

    Trivia

    • Named after the American political commentator Charlie Kirk.
    • "Count gang violence" is how Steve Jobs announces the fight; ChatGPT's "earlert" is not a real word.
