    The 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text is an ancient relic and the central MacGuffin of the later canon. It is one of the components named in the Prophecy as necessary to defeat Chris on the court.

    In the book its name is always set apart, printed centered on its own lines in bold italics, in the form:

    "The 3 year contract plan from Verizon for unlimited talk and text"

    This typographical reverence is extended to no other object, including the iPhone 3G.

    Names

    The relic is referred to by several names:

    • "3 year contract plan from version for unlimited talk and text" (sic), in Chapter Triangle
    • "the gift card" and, in a Steve Jobs aside, possibly "19 dollar fornite card, code: who wants it"
    • "This ancient redeemable card", by Microsoft Bing
    • "the verison gift card" (sic), by the narration and by Kanye
    • "the relic" and "the artifact"

    Whether the relic is a contract, a phone plan, or a gift card is the subject of ongoing editorial debate. The canon treats all three as the same thing.

    History

    First mention

    In Chapter Triangle, Plankton explains that the only person capable of battling Chris is one who has downloaded the sacred social media apps onto their Nokia I-4500 flip phone "with android wireless technology and 3 year contract plan from version for unlimited talk and text with there cutting edge 3g network".

    The casino

    The relic was "recovered from the ruins of the LA Walmart" and became "the key to" The 6 7 Casino, as it is "one of the few in the universe that can create a field of gambling around any building that it is contained in." Microsoft Bing hired Hank Hill's crew to guard it, warning "there will be hunters tonight".

    Liberation

    In the The Flashback Chapter, Steve Jobs orders Siri to take the Inav to the Casino: "its time to liberate the gift card". In Chapter the Maybe Seventh he tells the gangity gang that the relic "Resides here" and that with it "we have a shot at defeating Chris".

    During the Casino Heist, the Ibeam blasted open the Vault, revealing the card "Glowing with a radiant golden light". Kanye West "grabbed the gift card and put it into his Yeezy jacket", and it was loaded into the Inav "to keep it safe for now".

    Activation

    On arrival in the TurboGrafx 16 Sector, Steve Jobs "took out his iPhone and started entering the gift card code", as part of a plan to sync with the Flip Phone Covenant without triggering 5G.

    The Prophecy

    In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, MF Vroom recites the Prophecy, which lists the relic alongside the Sacred Apps, Apple Podcasts 2 and Settings (Full access) as what a phone must contain to beat Chris.

    Powers

    • Generates a Field of Gambling around any building it is contained in.
    • Glows with a radiant golden light.
    • Is guarded by Charlie Kirks, who are "trained warriors" deployed specifically to defend it.
    • Has a redeemable code.

    Continuity errors

    • "Verizon" is spelled "version" in Chapter Triangle and "verison" in Chapter the Maybe Seventh.
    • Because the centered name already begins with "The", the book reads "the most remarkable, the / The 3 year contract plan..." and "Inside the vault they saw it.. the / The 3 year contract plan...", giving the relic two definite articles.
    • In Chapter Triangle the plan comes with a Nokia flip phone, but by Chapter the Maybe Seventh it is being entered into an iPhone 3G.
    • The "19 dollar fornite card" may or may not be the same object.

    Trivia

    • At the time of writing, no 3 year Verizon plan was known to generate gambling fields in the real world.
    • Free "$10 Fortnite cards" were advertised in the skies of England in Chapter Triangle, suggesting a thriving gift-card economy across the multiverse.
