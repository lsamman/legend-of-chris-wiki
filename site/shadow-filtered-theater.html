    The Shadow-Filtered Theater in Cupertino, California, Reality 42-B, was the venue of Steve Jobs's iPhone 3G Keynote. It had a levitating stage, an audience of people and sentient holograms, and at least one MySpace server, which cried.

    Fate

    At the end of the keynote, "The stage collapses into a pool of Mountain Dew Baja Blast." It is unclear whether the rest of the theater survived.

    The name suggests it filters shadows. Given how often things in the canon are sent to the Shadow Realm, this may have been a sensible safety feature.
