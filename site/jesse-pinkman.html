    Jesse Pinkman

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Yo.

    Jesse Pinkman
    AliasesJesse pink-man
    AffiliationCrew of the crack van

    Characters

    Jesse Pinkman (written "Jesse pink-man") is one half of "the wrath of Jesse and white". With Walter White he crews the crack van, and he is the main supply runner for Hank Hill's group.

    Biography

    The rescue

    Jesse and White brought the crack van out of hyperspace beside the twelve towers to rescue Hank from the DOD: "Quick! Hank get on yo!"

    Dusty Depot

    While stranded in Dusty Depot, Jesse "used a port-a-potty to go to a quick-check to score some Doritos", and came back "with 2 mountain dews and a pack of zyns". The clerk told him about an oil casino nearby, and Jesse came up with the plan: "we could nap some, anyone got some nft's on their phone?" He then "ripped open the porta-potty door" to reveal a portal of "swirls and particle effects".

    The casino

    In the 6 7 Casino Jesse said "Woah mr.white.. we could lowkey go gambling in here yo." When Microsoft Bing made his offer, Jesse was the skeptic: "Ancient relic? So your asking us to protect it? Yo?" During the heist he recognized Kanye ("Ye??"). Hank's decision to join Kanye was made with a nod to Jesse.

    Trivia

    • Jesse Pinkman is a main character of Breaking Bad, where his catchphrase is "yo". The canon uses it faithfully.
    • He is the only character known to have used the port-a-potty for actual shopping.
