    DJ Khaled (spelled "DJ kaled" in the canon) is a combatant at The 6 7 Casino who appears during the Casino Heist, apparently as its security guard.

    Contents
    1. Role in the story
    2. Continuity errors
    3. Trivia

    Role in the story

    As Mr. White ran toward the gunfire, "a hand was put on Mr.whites shoulder" and someone said "Hank let the security guard deal with this.. BRING OUT THE LOBSTER!!" DJ kaled then "jumped out from behind him with his dj set and laid out a sick beat with plenty of doros bass."

    "Without hesitation Kanye West jumped at DJ kaled, but DJ kaled dodged it no problem." The fight then stopped when Jesse recognised Kanye.

    When Steve Jobs fired the Ibeam, there was "a giant flash" and "a huge massive low taper fade got put on DJ KALED". His status after the haircut is unknown.

    Continuity errors

    • The hand is on Mr. White's shoulder, but the speaker addresses Hank.
    • It is never said who speaks the lobster line. Readers are split between DJ Khaled announcing himself in the third person and an unseen hype man.

    Trivia

    • DJ Khaled is a real-world DJ and record producer known for shouting on his own tracks.
    • "Doros bass" is believed to relate to Doritos ("doros").
    • "Huge massive low taper fade" is a reference to the low taper fade meme.
