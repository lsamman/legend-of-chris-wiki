    The Secret Teachings of Social Media
    CurriculumTwitter, MySpace, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook
    Studied byChris and the tech bros
    VenueThe sky
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Concepts & Lore

    The Secret Teachings of Social Media are the hidden doctrines that Chris, LeBron James, Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg balled into the sky to learn: “the secret teachings of twitter, myspace, instagram, snapchat and, facebook.” (The comma after “and” is canonical.)

    Legacy

    The teachings resulted in Apple Podcasts. Later, they are reframed as weapons. RFK says Chris can only be fought by one who has “downloaded the sacred social media apps,” and Steve Jobs presents the Sacred Apps (MySpace v1.3.3, Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition, Facebook (Pre-Cringe), and Twitter Bluebird Variant) as running only on the iPhone 3G.

    Trivia

    • Instagram is the only one of the five original teachings that does not appear in the Sacred Apps or the Prophecy. Its fate is unknown.
