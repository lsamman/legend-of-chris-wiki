    Mercedes Benz System Cruiser

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Underpowered, cramped, no USB port. Beloved.

    The Mercedes Benz System Cruiser is the starship that RFK and Plankton use to reach Dunder Mifflin, rescue MF Vroom, and return to the Projects. It is described as "newly bought" and "extremely Underpowered for the galaxy To galaxy trips that would be required by it", and it is the New Crips' main means of transport.

    Specifications and modifications

    The stock cruiser could not handle intergalactic travel. Thanks to "a modification of the liquid deuterium and gaseous methane propellant the engines had 40% more performance, this modification was due to the addition to galaxy gas, graciously gifted to them by litl Wayne of the litl empire." Even modified, the trip to Dunder Mifflin took a month.

    Other known features:

    • Hyperspace capability: "Preparing to exit hyper space!"
    • A cramped cockpit, which seats at least four once MF Vroom and Ioannus Paranukus come aboard.
    • No USB port. MF Vroom asks "Bro where's the usb port" on boarding, "And they never did find it as the ship took off.."
    • Atmospheric flight: it can cruise through the treetops of Adirondack Park and make "a slow long turn, as fast as you could turn at mach 3".

    Service history

    Dunder Mifflin infiltration

    The cruiser passes through the Paper Belt, hugs the wires of the Giant LED Sign, browses the Lobby File Cabinet, squeezes through the crack in a vending machine door into the Labyrinth, and lands on the storm-battered landing platform of Call of Duty HQ.

    Escape

    After MF Vroom is rescued, the gang finds the ship "unweathered in their endeavors in the base and was ready to cruise onto the next destination". Plankton calls it their "Goofy Ahh ship" and asks why it is "built like a 2008 Honda accord Zyn weed addition".

    Return to the Projects

    It comes out of hyperspace "With a goose on the windshield and leaves on their wings", turns over the Adirondacks to line up with the Projects National Spaceport, and lands after "a hour or two of practicing mogging". When Tung Tung Tung Sahur tries to arrest MF Vroom, Plankton and RFK run back into the cruiser "to grab their modded nerf guns". The gang then escapes in Son's 2005 Honda Accord instead, and the cruiser is left at the spaceport.

    Continuity errors

    • Plankton compares the cruiser to a 2008 Honda Accord. Shortly afterwards the gang rides in a real 2005 Honda Accord, which TON 618 then searches for as "the Camry". The canon's car problems go back to the Toyota Corolla Question.
    • "Zyn weed addition" is probably a typo for "edition". Zyn is a nicotine pouch brand.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: Mercedes-Benz makes cars and vans, not starships. Galaxy Gas is a real brand (see its article).
    • It is one of three main vehicles in the canon, along with the Inav and the Crack Van. It is the only one without a USB port.
