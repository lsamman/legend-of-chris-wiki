    Chris, known in his full and most feared form as Chris: All Balled Up Deluxe Edition, is the title character of The Legend of Chris and the central figure of its entire cosmology. He is at once a baby, a baller, a corpse, the ball itself, a founding member of Apple Podcasts, a war criminal, and the being whose defeat on the court is foretold by the Prophecy.

    According to the opening incantation, everything that is believed is ultimately “a matter of Chris.”

    Origins

    Chris's biography is recorded in the Cycle of Chris, the origin litany that makes up the Chapter Before Chapter 2:

    Chris was a baby he was baller and then he died and then he balled from the grave and then he became the ball and balled himself into the sky together with lebron james and elon musk and mark zuckerberg

    In the sky he studied the secret teachings of Twitter / X, myspace, instagram, snapchat and facebook alongside LeBron James, Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg. Together they founded Apple Podcasts and “spread the word of chris to all the middle aged men to roam the earth” (see the Middle-Aged Men).

    The litany then runs through the entire history of the Multiverse, ending with the merging of Musk and LeBron into Samwise, who “then created chris and then he was born in newark.” Chris is therefore both the beginning and the end of his own origin story, which is why the litany repeats itself eight times.

    Death (again)

    Immediately after his birth in Newark, Chris “was stabbed to death by Ron destantis claiming he was gay because he was a homo-sapien.” See The Stabbing of Chris. Scholars note this is at least Chris's second recorded death, the first being the one he balled out of the grave from.

    War crimes and the cloning

    In Chapter Triangle, Plankton briefs RFK on Chris's later history: “after his warcrimes in the galactic sentate his body was thought to be clones and thrown into a pit thought to have no end to it.” Because the body was cloned, his soul “remained in the first one because our all knowing overseers declared it the less wack one.” (See Chris's War Crimes & the Cloning and the Pit With No End.)

    After the crash of the housing market in 2007/2008 Chris's location was lost. The most recent intelligence places him in the Reformed Travis Scott Sector, on an unknown planet, buying a McDouble. RFK reacts to the name by noting he has not heard it “since operation desert storm aired on cartoon network.”

    Balling monopoly

    Chris's chief offense against the living is economic. According to MF Vroom, “back in the day I used to be a baller on the court, I played Wemby, Curry, even Bronny… But all that changed when chris took balling all for himself!” Without free Balling, reality itself degrades: in Gotham City, Steve Jobs observes that “beat drops can't exist without balling… how could Chris be so blind.”

    ChatGPT lists the benefits of defeating him as cheaper Oil, “less regulation in general, and in addition.. balling would be free.”

    How to defeat Chris

    Multiple overlapping methods are proposed in the canon:

    • Meet the tech bros and download the sacred social media apps onto a Nokia I-4500 flip phone with a 3 year contract plan (Plankton's version).
    • Use the iPhone 3G, an “anti-deity protocol”, and “Begin the Mixtape. Sync with the Flip Phone Covenant. And remember… never update” (Siri 3G).
    • Assemble the Sacred Apps plus the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon and beat him on the court, as told in the Prophecy, after which “lebron james and elon musk and mark zuckerberg and Chris will fall.”

    Continuity errors

    • The litany says Chris learned social media with the tech bros, but Chapter Triangle says “he was before the social media and before the tech bros, he has seen a universe ruled by alcoholics and email to spread marxist propaganda and wawa.”
    • Chris balls into the sky with Elon Musk and LeBron James, who later merge into Samwise and create him. Fans consider this either a time loop or a typo.
    • The Prophecy names LeBron James and Elon Musk as falling separately, even though they had already merged.
    • Chris is stabbed to death at birth, yet is alive, cloned, and buying McDoubles later on.

    Behind the scenes

    • The book's cover art depicts Chris as a very chill-looking dog. Nothing in the text confirms or denies that Chris is a dog.
    • The “Deluxe Edition” suffix echoes video game and album re-releases.
