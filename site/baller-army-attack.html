    The Dabbing of the Haters

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · A hard one.

    The Dabbing of the Haters
    BelligerentsBaller Army vs. the nazis of the Cringe Universe
    Ordered byLeBron James
    Casualties“over 10”
    ResultEvery nazi dabbed on
    Followed byThe Great Oppression
    Events

    The Dabbing of the Haters was the first military action in the Multiverse. It was ordered by LeBron James after he looked at the Cringe Universe and judged it “cringe.”

    The official account reads: “This attack was a hard one over 10 casualties were found and every nazi was dabbed on.” It is the only battle in the canon with a reported casualty count, and also the only one whose principal weapon is the dab. Historians note that “over 10” is a low figure for an operation against an entire universe, which they put down to how effective dabbing is.
