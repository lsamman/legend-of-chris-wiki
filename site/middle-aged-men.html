    The Middle-Aged Men

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · They roam the earth.

    The Middle-Aged Men
    Converted byApple Podcasts
    BeliefThe word of Chris
    HabitatThe earth (roaming)
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Factions & Organizations

    The Middle-Aged Men are the first converts of the faith of Chris. After Chris and the tech bros founded Apple Podcasts, “they spread the word of chris to all the middle aged men to roam the earth.”

    The grammar leaves it unclear whether the men already roamed the earth, or were sent to roam it as missionaries. Either way, the faction is mentioned in every recitation of the Cycle of Chris, so by mention count it is one of the most prominent groups in the book.

    Trivia

    • Choosing podcasts to reach middle-aged men is widely considered the single most realistic strategic decision in the canon.
