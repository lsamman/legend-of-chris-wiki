    Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition

    Items & Technology From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Should you choose to accept it.

    Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition
    TypeSacred App
    Compatible deviceiPhone 3G
    Part ofThe Sacred Apps
    LocationFreddy Fazbear's Pizza
    First appearanceThe Flashback Chapter
    How to get itSpend 5 nights at Freddy's, per the golden plaque
    Items & Technology

    Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition is one of the Sacred Apps. It is listed at the iPhone 3G Keynote as one of the apps only the iPhone 3G can run, and again in the Prophecy.

    Contents
    1. Notes
    2. Location found
    3. The five nights

    Notes

    • Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol is a 2011 film. Snapchat's logo is a ghost.
    • FS Martin Genius "used to beta test Snapchat in the nether dimension", so he is the canon's leading Snapchat expert. Whether he tested this edition is unknown.
    • Snapchat was one of the "secret teachings" in the origin story (The Secret Teachings of Social Media).

    Location found

    At the end of Chapter 4+.1+.1…, Officer Steelwool's recorded message tells the heroes that “Snapchat: Ghost protcol is in.. Freddy Fazbears Pizza” (see Freddy Fazbear's Pizza). Kanye: “We finna pull up on those robots brotha.”

    The five nights

    In Chapter Number New Jersey the gang finds a golden plaque in the pizzeria's office: “Whoever spends 5 nights at freddies shall recieve Snapchat: Ghost Protocol”. Mike Schmidt has only managed four nights. Night 1 has begun.
