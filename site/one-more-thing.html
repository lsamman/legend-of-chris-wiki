    "One More Thing"

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Let's end this.

    "One More Thing"
    TypeCatchphrase
    SpeakerSteve Jobs
    ContextEnd of The Battle of Club Shadowban
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Concepts & Lore

    "One More Thing" is Steve Jobs's line at the end of his arrival in Club Shadowban. After he lifted the iPhone 3G, apps downloaded themselves across the cosmos and the Shadow Realm wept. Then: "'One more thing,' he said. 'Let's end this.'" It closes the Dunder Mifflan chapter.

    Notes

    • The real Steve Jobs used "One more thing..." at Apple keynotes to introduce a surprise final product.
    • In this case the "one more thing" is not a product but a declaration of war on Chris, which may make it the most important "one more thing" ever said.
    • Fans note that the next chapter is a flashback, so "Let's end this" is followed straight away by going back in time.
