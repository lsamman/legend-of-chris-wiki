    The Battle of Club Shadowban was a rap battle between Kanye West and ChatGPT on one side and Lil Jokez on the other. It ended with no surviving clubgoers, a meme portal, and the second coming of Steve Jobs.

    Contents
    1. Background
    2. The battle
    3. The drop
    4. Aftermath
    5. Trivia

    Background

    Lil Jokez had been infecting Gotham with Laugh.exe. Drake Batman recruited Kanye, who decided to answer with his own mixtape, 808s and Apocalypses. The crowd that night included former presidents, TikTok influencers and the angry Pope disguised as Tyler1.

    The battle

    Lil Jokez opened:

    Kanye's propane is mid, ChatGPT simps for lint, Gotham's my turf, now go back to Flint.

    "Kanye's eyes glowed. GPT's speakers unfolded like mecha wings. They hit the stage." Kanye answered with the "Ye the Gas" verse, and GPT followed with "ChatGPT online, rhymes divine, I code your whole verse in assembly line."

    "The crowd exploded in Fortnite emotes." Lil Jokez screamed and morphed into a 2007 JPEG of Heath Ledger. Drake Batman threw a smoke grenade filled with peach-flavored Axe body spray and tackled him into a meme portal.

    The drop

    "Silence. Then: 'The mixtape… has dropped.'" A wormhole opened across all multiverses at once, and a glowing sideways iPhone 3G hovered down to the stage with Steve Jobs standing on top of it, "wearing a turtleneck spun from algorithms and starlight." He lifted the iPhone "like Mufasa presenting Simba", apps began downloading themselves onto flip phones across the cosmos, and "The Shadow Realm wept." He ended with "One more thing... Let's end this."

    Aftermath

    In the next chapter the crowd had vanished, leaving dusty floors and footprints. Steve blamed the Zuck Tears USB and declared the club sent to the Shadow Realm.

    Trivia

    • "Steel beams" refers to the long-running "jet fuel can't melt steel beams" meme.
    • No judges were present. By most readings, Kanye and GPT won by forfeit, since their opponent became a JPEG.
