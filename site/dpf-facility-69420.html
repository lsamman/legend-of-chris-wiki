    DPF Facility 69420

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations

    DPF Facility 69420
    TypeDefensive processing facility
    LocationThe Projects
    OccupantTON 618
    AppearanceLike Neon Genesis Evangelion; faint blue glow
    Built byOsama Bin Bom'in (presumably)
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Locations

    DPF Facility 69420 is one of the giant defensive processing facilities that tower over the Projects, and the home of TON 618. Ioannus Paranukus spots it from the window of Son's Accord:

    “in one of the giant defensive processing facilities that towered over the projects, a building that looked like something out of neon genesis evongelian, There was this faint blue glow.”

    Events

    The blue glow makes someone ask whether Ninja aka Tyler Blevins has joined the Fortnite lobby. Then “a giant robotic roar was heard from horizon to horizon, coming from guess where, the freaking DPF facility that was glowing.” Son identifies the facility by its number, and “the doors to the DPF slowly opened far in the distance”. TON 618 then comes out.

    Trivia

    • Neon Genesis Evangelion is an anime about giant bio-mechanical robots launched from huge facilities, which is the comparison being made.
    • 69420 combines two well-known internet joke numbers.
    • “DPF” is never spelled out, but it fits “defensive processing facility”. There are several such facilities, so there may be facilities 1 to 69419 as well.
    • The book spells the anime “neon genesis evongelian”.
