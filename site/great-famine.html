    The Great Famine

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · There was no universe left to eat.

    The Great Famine
    CauseFrodo eating the universe
    Notable deathsElon Mush (of NFL poisoning)
    Followed byThe Merging
    Events

    The Great Famine came after Frodo ate the universe: “After which there was a great famine and elon mush died of nfl poisoning.”

    Given that the entire universe had just been eaten by one hobbit, editors consider the famine the most logically consistent event in the Cycle. Oddly, its only recorded victim died of something other than starvation. Its main political consequence was the Merging of Elon Musk and LeBron James, possibly as a way to save on food.
