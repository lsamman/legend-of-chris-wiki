    Scott's House

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Ohio. A backyard with a ship in it.

    Scott's House
    OwnerScott the Woz
    LocationOhio
    LandmarksThe Target, the GameStop, a Sheetz
    Notable featureThe Wii Cube in the backyard
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Locations

    Scott's House is the home of Scott the Woz in Ohio. The heroes run to it from the studio, “past the target, and the GameStop”, and enter the backyard to find the Wii cube.

    The basement

    Walter White proposes cooking propane in the basement. Scott agrees on the condition that Walter plays Wii Sports with Moh on Thursdays. See The Basement Deal. Hank says “for the love of propane”.

    Notes

    • Steve Jobs tells Scott: “anything for ohio rizz gamestops”.
    • Ohio also contains the corner store and the United Streets of Ohio, which seems to be an area of heavy sightseeing for the canon.
