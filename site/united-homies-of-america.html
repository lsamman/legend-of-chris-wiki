    The United Homies of America

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · The UHA. An elite team teaching the ways of pure freedom.

    The United Homies of America (the UHA) is "an elite team teaching the ways of pure freedom", based at Buc-ee's HQ. It trains the Dunkin Alliance for three months and delivers it to Louisiana to face Claude, King of AI.

    Roster

    • Bucee, "the master of Oil-Bending. Famous for his briskets being on the board."
    • Kilroy, "a WWII Veteran. Knows the ways of being free."
    • Uncle Sam: "He wants YOU to fight for Israel."
    • John Freedom, the leader.

    Training the Alliance

    John Freedom declares: "We will train you in the ways of Freedom. No one can take AMERICA!!! THAT IS WHO WE ARE!! THE UNITED HOMIES OF AMERICA!!!" The two teams tour America in the Ford-F150 (The Freedom Road Trip).

    What comes next

    The UHA already knew of Will Vella's hunt for the MacBook Charger. Their truck floats straight up after the drop-off, and the Bagel Man later says they are waiting in the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy.
