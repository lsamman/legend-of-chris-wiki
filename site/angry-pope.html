    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Holy See. Holy Tilted.

    The Angry Pope (disguised as Tyler1)
    DisguiseTyler1
    MoodAngry
    LocationClub Shadowban
    StatusPresumed in the Shadow Realm
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Characters

    The Angry Pope was a patron of Club Shadowban, where the crowd included "former presidents, TikTok influencers, and one angry Pope disguised as Tyler1." He is never mentioned again.

    Notes

    • Tyler1 is a streamer famous for playing League of Legends and for his loud, angry outbursts. This makes the disguise either very good or redundant, since the Pope was already angry.
    • Which Pope it was is not specified.
    • Like the rest of the crowd, he vanished after the battle, leaving only footprints in the dust. He is presumed to be in the Shadow Realm.
