    The Epic of Vinny

    Concepts & Lore From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · A spin-off set on Garth, in the Kanye System, 1995 to 1996.

    The Epic of Vinny
    SettingGarth, The Kanye System
    Years1995 to 1996
    StatusUnfinished (a prologue, three chapters and the start of a fourth)
    Full wikiThe Epic of Vinny Wiki
    Concepts & Lore

    The Epic of Vinny is a spin-off story set from 1995 to 1996 on the planet Garth in the Kanye System. It follows Vinny B. James, a Boston Burger King cashier. It is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe, so its characters, places and events have articles here too.

    The full story text, a dedicated timeline and every article live on The Epic of Vinny Wiki. You can read the story there.

    Chapters

    • Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy: A flash-forward. The narrator promises not to spoil the team.
    • Chapter 1: Beginnings: July 4th, 1995. A Burger King. A freezer that is not a freezer.
    • Chapter 2: The Weird Route: A ship, a knife, an AI king and a very unfair game of Smash.
    • Chapter 3: The Age of Claude: Wyoming, Dunkin, Texas, three months of Freedom, and the day Louisiana became a data center.
    • Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World: November 2nd, 1995. Back at the register. No credit given.
