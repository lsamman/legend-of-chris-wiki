    Every event in the MASTER FILE, in the order it happened or the order it was told · 92 events

It opens with the same origin story eight times, numbers its chapters 2, Triangle and “the fourth one the one after the one after the second one which is before the third one”, and keeps its biggest flashback in a chapter called “chapter that is after the last one but its all a flashback”.
