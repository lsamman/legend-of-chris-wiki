    Department of Defense (DOD)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Contains damn-level threats. Afraid of goobers.

    Factions & Organizations

    The Department of Defense (DOD) is “a army divided by Jeff bezosi and Mark zucc.” Whether “divided” means co-commanded or split down the middle is never stated. It “was created to contain damn level threats in the reformed Travis Scott sector.”

    Operation against Hank Hill

    The DOD was “recently deployed to the Kanye system to contain a new threat, that being hank hill,” whose propane empire worked with the los polos harmonicas under system gang. With Hank cornered atop the twelve towers, the DOD announced that “snoopy mcspookintosh is also in our custody in the COD headquarters you have no one to help you now.”

    Then Jesse and White arrived in the drug van from breaking bad out of hyperspace, “floods of blue dust fell down on the DOD and the had to flee in fear of being a goober.” When the DOD got a second look, “the goobers where gone.”

    Trivia

    • The DOD shares the Reformed Travis Scott Sector with Chris's last known location, which means it was containing damn-level threats in the same area where the most dangerous being in the canon was buying a McDouble.
    • It is not to be confused with the Department of Awesomeness (DOA), which Steve Jobs mentions later.
    • Snoopy is held at “COD headquarters,” which may or may not be Call of Duty HQ.
