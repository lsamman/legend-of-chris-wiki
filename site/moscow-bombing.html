    The Attack on Moscow

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · A fast-food dispute, escalated.

    The Attack on Moscow is an event in Chapter 2 of The Legend of Chris, planned by the Keepers of the MySpace Relics against "the italians of russia". Its purpose was to end a social media feud between DQ and McDonald's.

    Contents
    1. Planning
    2. Motive
    3. Outcome
    4. Legacy

    Planning

    Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire traveled to the Projects to "corrode-a-nate" the operation with Osama Bin Bom'in. The central question of the summit was "which Toyota Corolla should be responsible for the intital bombing run" (see The Toyota Corolla Question). The answer was Lightning McQueen, "a chickfilla loyalist and a knight of 4chan".

    The exit plan was to have Tyler from Uber "Uber him out of there".

    Motive

    Moscow had hosted the September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing contest, which led DQ to start beef with McDonald's on MySpace. The relic keepers "wasnt cool with this C tier beef" and wanted "to shut McDonald's up and let DQ win already." The canon does not explain how this would help DQ.

    Outcome

    The book reports the result in one sentence: "Sadly the attack on Moscow left lightning McQueen 'blasted' and moved in with George Constanta instead of returning by means of Tyler from Uber." No further details are given. The beef's status afterward is unknown.

    Legacy

    McQueen's "work in Moscow" earned him a reputation with Agent 007, who recruited him for the Fall of Constantinople. This makes the Moscow attack the origin of the Goober Gang.
