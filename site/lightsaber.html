    Lightsaber

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Pairs well with 4 Red Bulls.

    Lightsaber
    TypeEnergy sword
    Known wieldersRFK, Ioann-ius Nukian
    ColorIoann-ius's: deep white
    OriginStar Wars (real-world franchise)
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Items & Technology

    The lightsaber is an energy sword wielded by Jedi and Jedi-adjacent characters in The Legend of Chris.

    Known lightsabers

    RFK's lightsaber

    RFK brings a lightsaber, along with four Red Bulls, to his standoff with Bon Jovi. It is not clear whether he used it, as the fight was settled by the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate. In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, when the party is ambushed in the 360 chambers, RFK draws his hantavirus vaccine instead.

    Ioann-ius's lightsaber

    At Call of Duty HQ, Ioann-ius Nukian jumps into the battle and "his lightsaber lit up a deep white". The narrator adds a brief, unnecessary note about which color it was not. In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, "Ioannus drew his light saber" in the 360 chambers, and during the spaceport escape, "light sabers slicing" are part of a rapidly rising body count.

    Trivia

    • White lightsabers are rare in Star Wars. The canon does not explain why Ioann-ius has one.
