    Jason
    Origin“the same play” (Medea)

    Catchphrase“ha women”
    Duel resultLost
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Characters

    Jason was summoned by Mark Zuckerberg “from the same play” as Medea in order to deal with her. On arrival, he “allowed all his descendants and crushes ever to be killed because “ha women”.”

    The duel

    Jason “dualed medea in the hit video game donkey kong racing, the punishment for the loser was to find mic Q.” Jason lost, “but made medea look for mic Q anyway because “ha women”.” This makes him the only person in the canon to lose a duel and enforce the penalty on the winner.

    Behind the scenes

    In Euripides' Medea, it is Medea who kills Jason's new bride and their children after he abandons her. In this canon Jason is said to have “allowed” this, which some editors consider a compressed summary of the play.
