    The Siege of the Galactic Senate

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · Long and hard.

    The Siege of the Galactic Senate
    LocationGalactic Senate, Planet Patrick
    AttackerBon Jovi
    Defenders80-year-old senators; RFK
    ResultSenators pushed out; standoff; Bon Jovi dies
    Followed byThe Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Events

    The Siege of the Galactic Senate is the conflict in which Bon Jovi took the Galactic Senate on Planet Patrick. "The battle was long and hard but Bon Jovi eventually had pushed out all of the 80 year old senators. But one remained a Dark Knight named RFK."

    The last senator

    RFK "used ancient battle tactics of the Jedi and was ready to fight back Bon Jovi." His declaration, "It's over Bon Jovi I have 4 Red Bulls and a lightsaber, no one can mess with me I'm cool", led directly into the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate. The siege ended at the start of Chapter Triangle: "Bon Jovi Died at the hands of RFK."

    Trivia

    • The phrase "long and hard" is also used for the War for Byzantine Mexico ("The war was long and hard"). Historians believe this is the canon's standard description of a war.
    • The senators were "pushed out", not harmed. Bon Jovi's methods appear to have been mostly physical relocation.
