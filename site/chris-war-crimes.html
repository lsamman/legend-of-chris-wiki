    Chris's War Crimes & the Cloning

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · The incident that left exactly one Chris in circulation.

    Chris's War Crimes & the Cloning refers to a pre-canon incident described by Plankton when he recruits RFK. After Chris committed unspecified "warcrimes in the galactic sentate", his body was cloned and disposed of, which led to the question of how many Chrises there are. The answer, after some debate, is one.

    Plankton's account

    Plankton explains: "after his warcrimes in the galactic sentate his body was thought to be clones and thrown into a pit thought to have no end to it."

    RFK, reasonably, asks: "Interesting so theres two on the prowl?"

    Plankton's reply is the canon's main statement on Chris's metaphysics:

    Not necessarily, you see in ancient times water was considered essential for human life, and it still it, however because we cloned his body his soul remained in the first one because our all knowing overseers declared it the less wack one.

    "Baller, so theres only one then?" asks RFK. "Exactly," Plankton confirms, before adding that Chris has been missing since the housing market crash of 2007/2008 and has recently been traced to the Reformed Travis Scott Sector.

    Analysis

    What were the war crimes?

    The canon never says. Since the Galactic Senate is later the site of Bon Jovi's siege, the senate seems to attract violence. No source connects Chris to that siege.

    Which body was thrown in the pit?

    The text says Chris's body "was thought to be clones" and was thrown into the pit, while also saying "we cloned his body". The likeliest reading is that the clone(s) went into the pit and the original, which still had the soul, escaped. The phrase "we cloned" also suggests that Plankton, or an organization he belongs to, did the cloning, which raises questions the canon does not address.

    The water digression

    Plankton's remark that water "was considered essential for human life, and it still it" has no clear link to the cloning. Some editors think it is a rhetorical warm-up. Others think it is the whole point.

    Fan theories

    • Theory: The "less wack" ruling means Chris's soul could move if the overseers changed their minds, which would make the All-Knowing Overseers the most powerful beings in the canon.
    • Theory: The cloning explains why Chris is called "Chris: All balled up Deluxe Edition", since a deluxe edition implies a standard edition exists somewhere (possibly the pit).

    Trivia

    • "Senate" is spelled "sentate" here and in RFK's exit from it ("left then sentate").
    • This is one of two times in the book that Chris is said to have died or been disposed of, the other being the Stabbing of Chris by Ron DeSantis. Neither appears to have slowed him down.
