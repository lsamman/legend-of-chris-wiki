    Characters

    MF Vroom is a character in The Legend of Chris: a car wearing a robe, a long-lost relative of the Projects' royal family, and the rightful ruler of O'Block. Exiled by Osama Bin Bom'in to "forever get yelled at in COD lobbies", he is sought out by RFK and Plankton as the first member of the New Crips, "Everyone's last hope of universalizing Balling".

    Biography

    Guide and exile

    MF Vroom first appears in Chapter 2 as the guide hired by Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire to "traverse the projects". The narrator explains he "was a long lost relative of the project's royal family and was keen on returning to the top." Upon reaching I-38 West, he "was send into exile by Osama Bin Bom'in to forever get yelled at in COD lobbies. MF Vroom was never seen again…"

    He was, in fact, seen again.

    The search

    In Chapter Triangle, RFK rejects Mark Zuckerberg as an ally and proposes: "MF Vroom, if we can restore him to power in the projects we can harness the power of the gang army". Locating him requires a trip to Call of Duty HQ, deep in Dunder Mifflin. There the party meets Ioann-ius Nukian, a Jedi master "of the projects" who wishes to "free my king".

    Escape

    In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, the party enters the 360 chambers, and "from the shadows lept a fuckig car wearing a robe." Plankton throws his metal chair at him before realizing who it is. Vroom explains: "I escaped the COD lobbies but for days now ive been roaming these hallways trying to find a way out!" An ethereal voice tells him, "Bruh just leave", and a door appears. On boarding the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser, his first words are "Bro where's the usb port". It was never found.

    Return to the Projects

    On the flight home, Vroom takes a call on his Windows phone (ringtone: Peter Griffin "singing some choir something") from Spamton G. Spamton, his "bestie" from Alpha Mogger Beta. At the Projects National Spaceport, Tung Tung Tung Sahur tries to arrest him, but Vroom's old right-hand man Son appears with a modded colonial musket. During the drive to O'Block, Son explains how Osama's frat stormed Vroom's palace after Vroom refused to join the EU (see The O'Block Coup). The chapter ends with Vroom revealing the Prophecy and the presence of Facebook (Pre-Cringe) in the national archives.

    Balling career

    Vroom claims he was once "a baller on the court" who played Wemby, Curry, even Bronny before Chris "took balling all for himself". This makes him one of the few characters with a personal grievance against Chris, rather than a cosmic one.

    Personality

    Vroom is relaxed to the point of serenity. He describes his escape as feeling like he "role played as a Redditor touching grass outside his chud ahh house", and he calls RFK "Ronald F Kennedy Jr." without anyone correcting him. During his college years he took part in a xp candy packaging operation as part of Alpha Mogger Beta hazing, which he describes as "the high life".

    Trivia

    • He is never explicitly identified as a specific car model, although Plankton asks someone aboard the ship why they are "built like a 2008 Honda accord". See The Toyota Corolla Question.
    • The narrator claims Son "went into hiding when MF Vroom was sent into exile in fear that he too would have to listen to xbox live 2009 lobbies until his end."
    • Vroom's two reported origins (lost royal relative in Chapter 2, co-founder of O'Block in Chapter 4+.1+.1…) are not necessarily contradictory, but editors have argued about it at length.
