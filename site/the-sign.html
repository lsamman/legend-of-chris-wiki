    The Giant LED Sign

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Wires: bigger than big.

    The Giant LED Sign
    TypeSignage (galactic scale)
    LocationDunder Mifflin exterior
    Wire size"bigger than big"
    Used forGetting past the Google Smart Guards
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Locations

    The Giant LED Sign is mounted on the outside of the Dunder Mifflin office-galaxy. Its wiring is so large that Plankton chose it as the way in, so the ship could avoid the Google Smart Guards at the thousands of regular entry points.

    When RFK asks how big the wires are, the answer gets bigger with each line: "So big" / "like big big" / "bigger than big" / "baller". The ship then "slipped behind the giant LED sign, as the ship hugged the wires is slowly entered.."

    Trivia

    • What the sign says is never stated. It probably says "Dunder Mifflin", but it could say "Dunder Mifflan".
    • "Baller" is the canon's largest unit of measurement.
