    The MacBook Charger

    Characters From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Arch nemesis of the Bagel Man. Somewhere in the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy.

    The MacBook Charger
    Nemesis ofWill Vella, the Bagel Man
    LocationThe Dunder Mifflin Galaxy
    Hunted byThe Dunkin Cult, with The UHA
    StatusNot yet seen
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Characters

    The MacBook Charger is the arch nemesis of Will Vella. Kilroy reveals that the UHA already knows about Will's plan to find it. Will's answer: "once I reach Vellatosis, we'll fly out to the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy and find it."

    The conquest of Dunkin

    After Vellatosis, the Bagel Man tells the assembled Vellas: "WE MUST FIND OUR NEMESIS, THE MACBOOK CHARGER! IN THE DUNDER MIFFLIN GALAXY, THE UNITED HOMIES OF AMERICA ARE WAITING FOR US!" The Dunkin Cult Cruiser then leaves for space (The Departure of the Dunkin Cult). Why a charger is a nemesis has not been explained.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
