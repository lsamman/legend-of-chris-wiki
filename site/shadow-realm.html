    The Shadow Realm

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Where light doesn't exist and the universes go.

    Locations

    The Shadow Realm is “a realm where light doesnt exist” and where the nazi ants left over from the Cringe Universe “still exist.” It became the largest location in the canon by volume when Mark Zuckerberg snapped his fingers and “all the universes except for his fav were sent to the shadow realm.”

    The Snap

    After the Multiverse Civil War ended badly, the Snap sent essentially the entire Multiverse to the Shadow Realm. Everything that happens later in the book therefore takes place either in Zuckerberg's favourite universe or inside the Shadow Realm, and the text never says which.

    Emotions

    The Shadow Realm can feel. When Steve Jobs arrives on a glowing iPhone 3G at Club Shadowban and apps begin downloading onto flip phones across the cosmos, “The Shadow Realm wept.”

    Club Shadowban

    After the battle, the club was found abandoned and covered in dust, with only the outlines of feet remaining. Jobs concludes that, because of the Zuck Tears USB, “this room must've been sent to the shadow realm.” This makes the club the second thing Zuckerberg has sent there, and the first one he did by crying. Gotham then begins disappearing, apparently for unrelated balling-based reasons.

    The returning Elon

    In Chapter the Maybe Seventh, ChatGPT warns that if the iPhone is rolled back from 5G to 3G at the last moment, “we have to deal with elon when we deal with chris, if hes coming straight from the shadow realm then who know what he'll want.” This is generally taken to mean Elon Mush or his “dormant twin.”

    Trivia

    • “Shadow Realm” is a phrase popularized by the Yu-Gi-Oh! anime, where losers of Shadow Games were banished.
    • Related names include Club Shadowban and the Shadow-Filtered Theater in Cupertino. Whether the filter keeps the Shadow Realm out or lets it in is unknown.
