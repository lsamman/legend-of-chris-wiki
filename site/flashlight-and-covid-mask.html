    The Flashlight and the Covid Mask

    Items & Technology From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · If you can use these effectively, then you have a chance.

    The Flashlight and the Covid Mask
    Given byMike Schmidt
    Given toThe gang
    Consists ofA flashlight; a mask of the covid 19 virus
    ForSurviving five nights
    First appearanceChapter Number New Jersey
    Items & Technology

    The flashlight and the covid mask are Mike Schmidt's gift before he fades away: “Mike handed them a flash light and a mask of the covid 19 virus”.

    Notes

    • The book says “a mask of the covid 19 virus”. Whether that is a mask against the virus or a mask shaped like it is left open.
    • Mike's advice is the whole manual: “If you can use these effectively, then you have a chance”.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: a flashlight and a mask are the player's tools in Five Nights at Freddy's 2.
