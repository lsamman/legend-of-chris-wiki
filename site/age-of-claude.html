    The Age of Claude

    Concepts & Lore From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · July 4th to October 29th, 1995. Began with a portal, ended with an Uber.

    The Age of Claude
    BeginsWhen Claude, King of AI leaves the AI Shadow Realm (Chapter 2: The Weird Route)
    RulerClaude, King of AI
    AimReplace humans with data centers and robots
    ResistanceVinny B. James and the Dunkin Alliance

    Concepts & Lore

    The Age of Claude is what Chapter 2: The Weird Route says begins when the portal seals: "The Age of Claude had begun." It is also the title of Chapter 3: The Age of Claude.

    Contents
    1. So far
    2. How it ended
    3. On the spin-off wiki

    So far

    In the era's first hours, Claude, King of AI flies into the United States to start building data centers (The Clash Over the USA), and Vinny B. James begins to gather allies against him (The Dunkin Alliance). The era's length is not given.

    How it ended

    Claude spends three months streaming and building data centers (Claude's Livestreams). The Dunkin Alliance, trained in the ways of Freedom, loses the Battle of Louisiana, but Vellatosis turns it (The Day of the Bagel Man). "The job was finished. The Age of Claude was over." Claude himself had just said it "is just beginning".

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
