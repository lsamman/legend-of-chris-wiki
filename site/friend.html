    Characters

    FRIEND is the foodbringer of the S.S. Vessel. Sent for Double Whoppers with Cheese, FRIEND finds the The Backrooms and comes back with Vinny B. James and Tyrone instead.

    Contents
    1. As the black cat
    2. The abduction
    3. Aboard the ship
    4. On the spin-off wiki

    As the black cat

    FRIEND first shows up as a black cat in a box of Double Whoppers with Cheese: "The cat's head turns, revealing its yellow and pink eyes to them. It's FRIEND. FRIEND laughs and disappears."

    The abduction

    FRIEND appears behind Vinny and Tyrone as they try to go back to work, takes both of them, "runs throughout the backrooms" and no-clips into a wall (FRIEND's Abduction).

    Aboard the ship

    Gaster screams at FRIEND for bringing humans ("HOW ARE WE SUPPOSED TO EAT NOW????"). FRIEND spits out a soggy Double Whopper without Cheese. The narrator lists FRIEND as "The foodbringer" among the crew.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
