    Spamton G. Spamton

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Frat bestie. [Recipe enthusiast]. Speaks in brackets.

    Characters

    Spamton G. Spamton is a voice on the other end of MF Vroom's Windows phone, and, by MF Vroom's own account, his “bestie” from his fraternity days at Darkworld State University. He is one of very few characters in the canon to be introduced entirely over the phone, and the only one who speaks partly in [BRACKETS].

    Spamton calls during the hyperspace flight from Dunder Mifflin to the Projects in Chapter 4+.1+.1…, requests a highly specific culinary favour, and hangs up. He has not been seen in person.

    The phone call

    As the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser crosses hyperspace, MF Vroom's Peter Griffin ringtone interrupts RFK's explanation of the mission. The caller opens with “MF, bro you out?”, and on learning that MF Vroom is headed back to the United States Of America to defeat Chris, he makes his request:

    “Well that’s splendid, listen if on the way you find any intergalactic road kill please for the love of [TON 618] cut off its [orb shaped objects] and bring it back for me so I can try this new recipe I’ve been wanting to try, grogu told me it on this wild lsd trip I had, twas fire bruh”

    He signs off with an invitation to “pop up to [CYBERR1!! C1TYY!!] When you get there chance” and the blessing “good luck we’re all counting on yall gucci gang”.

    MF Vroom answers only “Noted”. Whether he intends to collect the [orb shaped objects] is not recorded.

    Background

    When RFK asks “Who was that-t?”, MF Vroom explains that Spamton was his frat brother in Alpha Mogger Beta (“something that sounded like that”). Part of the frat's hazing was packaging XP candy for the higher-ups, which earned “so much [kromer]” (see Kromer). MF Vroom sums him up as “good guy”.

    Behind the scenes

    • Spamton G. Spamton is a salesman character from the video game Deltarune. He talks in bracketed, glitchy phrases, which explains [TON 618], [orb shaped objects] and [CYBERR1!! C1TYY!!].
    • Cyber City, Kromer and the “Darkworld” in Darkworld State University are all Deltarune references too.
    • TON 618 is used here as an oath, “for the love of [TON 618]”, a few pages before the robot of that name appears. The book never says whether Spamton is swearing by the robot or the robot was named after the oath.

    Trivia

    • Spamton calls MF Vroom “habibi” and the whole team “gucci gang” in the same call.
    • RFK wants roadkill of his own later in the chapter (see The Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase). Two separate characters looking for roadkill recipes within a few pages is something fans have noticed and nobody has explained.
    • Spamton is the only person known to have heard a recipe from Grogu.
