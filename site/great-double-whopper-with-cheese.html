    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese is a mythic source of power. Whoever is "granted" it by its god "will have the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese", but "no one in any galaxy" has found either the item or the god.

    Contents
    1. Seekers
    2. In the prologue
    3. On the spin-off wiki

    Seekers

    Captain Gaster hopes to use it to rule the Kanye System. Vinny B. James explains to him that only the god has it. Many say TON 618 "consumed the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese to conquer all things", and many say otherwise.

    In the prologue

    Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy says "the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese flows through" Vinny as he fights TON 618. How he gets it is not yet told.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
