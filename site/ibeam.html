    The Ibeam

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · EMP first. Haircut second. Vault third.

    The Ibeam
    TypeOrbital-style beam weapon
    Mounted onThe Inav
    TargetingAirTag lock
    Activation"Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam."
    Side effectsArea-wide EMP; low taper fade on DJ Khaled
    Notable useOpening the vault
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Items & Technology

    The Ibeam is the Inav's main weapon. It is a giant beam that fires at whatever an AirTag is stuck to. It was used to open the vault holding the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon during the Casino Heist.

    The firing

    Hank Hill said that nothing his crew had could open the vault. Steve Jobs smirked, said "Behold," and produced an AirTag: "Once I place this onto the vault door then the Inav outside will be able to lock onto it, and fire the Ibeam."

    After placing the tag and telling everyone to stand back, Steve said "Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam." The whole building went silent: lights shut off, generators stopped, and the floor went dark, as the Ibeam's EMP cleared the area. Then came "a giant flash" in which "a huge massive low taper fade got put on DJ KALED", and "a enormous beam shot through the building onto to the vault door."

    Steve, light shining off his glasses, said: "Yess.. perfection, just like the iPod touch.. that thing cooked eggs while playing geometry dash" (The iPod Touch). After a few seconds the beam turned off, leaving a fog of dust and a hole large enough for moonlight to come in.

    Trivia

    • The low taper fade is a haircut, and a 2024 meme. Why the Ibeam gives haircuts is unexplained. DJ Khaled is its only known recipient.
    • The beam made a hole so large that the Inav later pulled up to "the massive hole made by the hole" (sic) to pick up the crew.
