    Lightning McQueen is a character in The Legend of Chris, described as a Toyota Corolla, "a chickfilla loyalist and a knight of 4chan". He carried out the Attack on Moscow, lived with George Constanta for a time, and later joined Agent 007 and Mr. Bean as part of the Goober Gang to bring down Constantinople. In the real world, Lightning McQueen is the race car hero of Pixar's Cars, and he is not a Toyota Corolla.

    Moscow

    In Chapter 2, Osama Bin Bom'in, Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire discuss which Toyota Corolla should make the initial bombing run on Moscow. "The Toyota Corolla to do the initial bombing run was to be lightning McQueen". The plan was to have Tyler from Uber "Uber him out of there", but "the attack on Moscow left lightning McQueen 'blasted' and moved in with George Constanta instead".

    England

    In Chapter Triangle, McQueen, now in "the Soviet Union", grows "sick of George Constanta" and flies to England to meet a contact from parliament. A stranger had handed him a pager instructing him "to hoard smoke detectors for parliament because of 'special reasons'". At the smoke and vape shop he meets Mr. Bean, who explains the mission: "take Constantinople away from the Barack Empire and Restore Bernie Sander's control over that area." McQueen's only concern is how to beat Barack Obama, who holds the 7 chaos emeralds. Agent 007 says, "Ive heard stories about your work in Moscow I think your a Dope pick for the mission".

    Constantinople

    In Chapter the Fourth, McQueen is tasked with planting the last two nukes in the old abandoned Walmarts on "the nadir side of the city". At the first Walmart he meets the local mafia; he "declined the request to be part of society and dropped off the nuke." The second target turns out to now be a Popeyes, where he places the order quoted above, pulls out his Glock 19, receives his food, and politely says "Thank you". He plants the nuke after eating.

    He then picks up Mr. Bean and 007 at 8aller Jump Street and flies them into a wormhole "just north of Dababy's last known location". Constantinople fell "at 14:74pm Unc standard time", and "that was the last anyone heard from the goober gang for now…"

    Continuity errors

    • McQueen is a Chick-fil-A loyalist, yet his only on-page meal is from Popeyes. Loyalist scholars call this "an operational necessity".
    • Moscow is called "the Italian city of Moscow" in Chapter 2, but McQueen is in "the Soviet Union" in Chapter Triangle.
    • The mission was planned around defeating Barack Obama, but the man lured to the center of Constantinople is Putin, who is having coffee with Mr. Bean at the time. See The Fall of Constantinople.
    • He is spelled "lighting McQueen" twice.

    Trivia

    • McQueen is listed first in the official Goober Gang roster that ends Chapter the Fourth, ahead of 007 and Mr. Bean.
    • Whether he flew to England as a passenger or as the airplane is never established.
