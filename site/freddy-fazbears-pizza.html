    Freddy Fazbear's Pizza

    Locations From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · A magical place for kids and grown-ups alike.

    Freddy Fazbear's Pizza
    TypeRestaurant / animatronic habitat
    ContainsSnapchat: Ghost Protocol
    Learned of viaOfficer Steelwool
    StatusEntered. Night 1 has begun
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    LocationSalty Springs, Earth, New Jersey
    Locations

    Freddy Fazbear's Pizza is where Snapchat: Ghost Protocol is located, according to the phone message that Officer Steelwool plays for the heroes. The book concludes: “Well that solves it, Snapchat: Ghost protcol is in.. Freddy Fazbears Pizza”.

    Contents
    1. The recording
    2. Status
    3. The visit

    The recording

    The message is the Night 1 call from Five Nights at Freddy's. It welcomes the night guard to “a magical place for kids and grown-ups alike, where fantasy and fun come to life”, notes that “Fazbear Entertainment is not responsible for damage to property or person”, and warns that animatronics will try to stuff you in a suit.

    Status

    The gang bursts in “at around 11pm” in Chapter Number New Jersey. See below.

    The visit

    ChatGPT advises Kanye “not to touch the animitronics as they do get spooky at night”. Plankton looks in the back rooms (“not to be confused with the backrooms”) and reports dead kids and maybe a Golden Freddy. Down a hallway is the office, with the golden plaque. Mike Schmidt appears, equips the gang and fades away. “The doors closed around them and they entered.. Night 1.”
