    Dunder Mifflan: A chapter that is not the first or second or third or the one that comes after the second one and the fourth one which is not the first one, commonly called Dunder Mifflan, is the fifth chapter of The Legend of Chris. It follows RFK and Plankton into Call of Duty HQ, where they meet Ioannus Paranukus, and sends Kanye West and ChatGPT to Gotham City for a rap battle that ends with the return of Steve Jobs.

    Synopsis

    The lobby and the Labyrinth

    The ship browses the lobby file cabinet, passing WoW, Fortnite and the Bible of Battle Passes, and locates COD HQ in "aisle 3 of the vending machine section." It flies through a crack in a vending machine into the Labyrinth. The HQ is "not as big as Dunder Mifflin but still pretty chonky," and wrapped in a storm.

    Ioann-ius

    Plankton says "Insert and his name is john cena here" (meanwhile, Larry the Cucumber is killed on 21 Jump Street). RFK senses "a disturbance in the Fabric in the Protestant universe." A man smashes through the OpenAble Door: "Ioann-ius Nukian Jedi master and dealer of justice (I sell per gram, $40 a gram)." After a misunderstanding, he reveals he is of the Projects and wishes to free his king. He warns of the Xbox Live dungeons, with their screaming 7-year-olds and modded controllers. "Nah we'll be fine."

    Inside, Plankton ignores the fingerprint scanner "because he answers to no one." The HQ is chaos: gamers yelled at for not capping red, Xbox 360s red-ringing mid-tournament. Ioann-ius charges in with a white Lightsaber, and RFK and Plankton run for the 360 Chambers.

    Gotham

    Kanye and ChatGPT arrive in Gotham in the Prius of Destiny, parking beside Ye Olde Bin. Outside Club Shadowban, FS Martin Genius, who "used to beta test Snapchat in the nether dimension" and made Vine loop in reverse in Timeline R, gives them a USB labeled 'Zuck Tears (Do Not Install)'. Drake Batman explains that Lil Jokez has started a SoundCloud, and his mixtape Laugh.exe is infecting the city via Bluetooth-enabled gargoyles.

    The Battle of Club Shadowban

    Kanye and ChatGPT drop 808s and Apocalypses in a club of former presidents, influencers and one angry Pope disguised as Tyler1. Lil Jokez disses them ("go back to Flint"); they respond. Jokez morphs into a 2007 JPEG of Heath Ledger, and Drake Batman tackles him into a meme portal.

    One more thing

    A wormhole opens across all multiverses, and a sideways iPhone 3G flies out carrying Steve Jobs. Apps download onto flip phones across the cosmos, and the Shadow Realm weeps. "One more thing," he says. "Let's end this."

    Title & numbering

    The title begins as a normal name, "Dunder Mifflan", then continues for fifteen lines defining the chapter by what it is not. Having excluded the first, second, third, fourth and "the one that comes after the second one," the reader is left to conclude it is the fifth. It spells the company "Mifflan" while the text mostly uses "Mifflin."

    Continuity notes

    • Ioannus Paranukus introduces himself as "Ioann-ius Nukian" and is called "Ioann" a few lines later; later chapters settle on "Ioannus Paranukus."
    • The Battle of Club Shadowban is written in a noticeably different style from the rest of the book, which fans attribute to a different author.
    • Kanye was the villain of the previous chapter's propane plot and is now the hero of this one.

    Trivia

    • Dunder Mifflin is the paper company from The Office.
    • Tyler1 is a famously loud League of Legends streamer.
    • "808s and Apocalypses" parodies Kanye's album 808s & Heartbreak.
