    Facebook (Pre-Cringe) is one of the Sacred Apps needed to defeat Chris. It is the only one whose physical location is known, and its recovery is the cliffhanger on which the canon ends.

    Contents
    1. History
    2. In the Projects
    3. Continuity errors
    4. Trivia

    History

    Facebook was among the "secret teachings" Chris learned in the origin story (The Secret Teachings of Social Media). The Pre-Cringe edition was listed at the iPhone 3G Keynote as one of the apps only the iPhone 3G can run, and in the Prophecy.

    In the Projects

    After MF Vroom recited the Prophecy, he and Son S. Sonionring revealed:

    We have Facebook (Pre-Cringe) in the national archives.. Its been one of our prize possessions since the nations inception, we're not quite sure how it got here, but under a build building, in the rubble we found it on a 67gb micro sd card.

    The crew concluded that this is why Osama deployed TON 618 against them: "If he knows our end goal already and he has connections with Chris.. That has to be why." RFK proposed that, instead of retaking the Projects, they "just try to just get Facebook." Someone pointed out that "The ssd would still be in the palace."

    Plankton said "Aight bet", their course was set, and the book ends: "Computah locate Facebook…"

    Continuity errors

    • It is stored on a "67gb micro sd card", then a few lines later on "The ssd". These are different kinds of storage.
    • It is in the "national archives" and then "in the palace". These may be the same building.
    • 67 GB is not a standard micro SD capacity. 67 is a recurring number in the canon (see the 6 7 Casino).

    Trivia

    • "Pre-Cringe" implies that Facebook later became cringe. Neither the timing nor the cause is given.
    • "Computah" mirrors Steve Jobs's habit of saying "Hey Siri". It is unknown which computer is being addressed.
