    The Sacred Apps

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · The canonical app store of destiny.

    The Sacred Apps
    TypeSoftware (sacred)
    Core appsMySpace v1.3.3, Snapchat: Ghost Protocol, Facebook (Pre-Cringe), Twitter Bluebird Variant
    Prophecy additionsApple Podcasts 2, Settings (Full access)
    Compatible deviceiPhone 3G (or a Nokia I-4500)
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Items & Technology

    The Sacred Apps are the legacy social media applications that must be installed on a single phone in order to defeat Chris. They are the canon's version of the Infinity Stones, and like them they must be collected.

    Contents
    1. List
    2. History
    3. Known locations
    4. Trivia

    List

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, Steve Jobs lists the four apps the iPhone 3G is uniquely able to run:

    • MySpace v1.3.3
    • Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition
    • Facebook (Pre-Cringe)
    • Twitter Bluebird Variant (with no limits)

    The Prophecy, told by MF Vroom, repeats these four and adds:

    • Apple Podcasts 2
    • Settings (Full access)

    It also requires The 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text, which is a contract rather than an app.

    History

    The idea goes back to the origin story (Chris Origins), in which Chris balled into the sky "to learn about the secret teachings of twitter, myspace, instagram, snapchat and, facebook" (The Secret Teachings of Social Media). Those teachings produced Apple Podcasts, and Elon Mush's anti-social media platform became Settings.

    In Chapter Triangle, Plankton says that the only person who can battle Chris is one who "has met with the tech bros and has downloaded the sacred social media apps onto there nokia I-4500 flip phone."

    Known locations

    • Facebook (Pre-Cringe) is held in the Projects National Archives on a 67gb micro sd card.
    • The others are unaccounted for. Steve's plan to "start with the social media apps" after syncing with the Flip Phone Covenant suggests a hunt is coming.

    Trivia

    • Instagram is one of the original secret teachings but is missing from both later lists.
    • "Bluebird" refers to Twitter's bird logo, retired in the 2023 rebrand to X.
    • "Apple Podcasts 2" implies an Apple Podcasts 1. The origin story's Apple Podcasts was a social media platform founded by Chris, LeBron, Elon and Zuckerberg.
