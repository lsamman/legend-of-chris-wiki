    The Corner Store (Ohio)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Best deal in the tri-state area.

    The Corner Store (Ohio)
    LocationThe United Streets of Ohio
    Associated withKanye West
    ProductsPotato chips ($2.85 a gallon), gas
    Expected clienteleAll the crips
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Locations

    The Corner Store in the United Streets of Ohio is pitched to Kanye West ("Ye this is it, this is the corner store…") as the outlet for his propane business. It is described as "the best deal in the tri-state area", and the speaker predicts that "all the crips will be buying their gas from this corner store!"

    The speaker is not named. Since only Kanye and ChatGPT are in the scene, most readers assume it is ChatGPT.

    Trivia

    • Potato chips are sold by the gallon here.
    • The store is specifically "not your run of the mill pill producing medical facility", which implies that ordinary corner stores in the canon are.
    • It is a different store from RFK's favorite corner store in Chai Town, which "probably was involved with several gangs".
