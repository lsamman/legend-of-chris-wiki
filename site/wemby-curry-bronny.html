    Characters

    Wemby, Curry and Bronny are three basketball players named as past opponents in the canon's only account of MF Vroom's playing career:

    “We plan on defeating Chris.. back in the day I used to be a baller on the court, I played Wemby, Curry, even Bronny… But all that changed when chris took balling all for himself!”

    None of them appears in person. This page covers all three.

    Contents
    1. The players
    2. Attribution dispute
    3. Trivia

    The players

    • Wemby: Victor Wembanyama, a very tall real-life NBA player.
    • Curry: Stephen Curry, a real-life NBA star known for three-pointers.
    • Bronny: Bronny James, son of LeBron James. The word “even” suggests that playing Bronny was the bigger achievement.

    Attribution dispute

    The line comes right after RFK says, “N-n-now mister Vroom, we must explain why we stopped your epic mlg gaming session”, and the narration then talks about “the middle of their speech”. So it is possible that RFK, not MF Vroom, is the former baller. The wiki follows the usual reading and credits MF Vroom, since it fits his later recital of the Prophecy and his role in the court storyline.

    Trivia

    • Bronny's father LeBron is one of the four figures the Prophecy says will fall. MF Vroom may have to play against the father of someone he once played against. This is a fan theory.
