    The Crack Van (RV of Time)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Hyperspace-capable. Oil-dependent.

    The Crack Van (RV of Time)
    AliasesThe drug van from Breaking Bad, the crack van, the bus, the rv of time
    CrewJesse Pinkman, Walter White, Hank Hill
    CapabilitiesHyperspace travel, dispensing blue dust
    FuelOil (empty)
    StatusDamaged, stranded in Dusty Depot
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Items & Technology

    The Crack Van, later called the RV of Time, is the vehicle of Jesse, White and Hank Hill. The canon introduces it as "the drug van from breaking bad", which "had exited hyper space next to hank" during his standoff with the DOD.

    History

    On its first appearance the van released "floods of blue dust" onto the DOD, which then fled. Jesse told Hank to get on, Hank got on, and by the time the DOD looked again "the goobers where gone". The text calls it "the crack van" and "the bus" within a few lines.

    In the Flashback Chapter it is called "the rv of time". It "was damaged", and the crew were "in the middle of searching for a oil deposit in dusty depot as their oil ran out." Hank keeps his NFT loot inside. When the crew left through the port-a-potty portal, the RV stayed behind.

    Trivia

    • In Breaking Bad, the RV is the mobile meth lab. The canon has given it hyperspace and, judging by the name, possibly time travel.
    • Hank later says that "we got teleported into dusty depot", so the RV may not have arrived under its own power.
