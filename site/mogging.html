    Mogging

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore

    Mogging
    TypePractice / power
    PractitionersThe New Crips, Tung Tung Tung Sahur (posthumously)
    InstitutionsAlpha Mogger Beta
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Concepts & Lore

    Mogging is the act of outclassing someone by sheer presence. It is a skill, a pastime and possibly a medical process in the canon, and it becomes especially important once the heroes reach the Projects.

    Contents
    1. Appearances
    2. Mechanics
    3. Trivia

    Appearances

    • Fraternity life. MF Vroom and Spamton were members of Alpha Mogger Beta.
    • Practice. On approach to the Projects National Spaceport, the crew spends “a hour or two of practicing mogging on their way to the projects”. This is the only training montage in the canon.
    • Resurrection. After Tung Tung Tung Sahur is shot, Son explains: “That’ll only have him being a beta male until his death pose starts mogging, then he’ll be back”.

    Mechanics

    From the Tung Tung Tung Sahur case, scholars of the canon conclude that a strong enough death pose can bring back the person holding it. The required level of mogging is not given.

    Trivia

    • “Mog” is internet slang for looking or seeming more impressive than someone else.
    • The canon also uses the related words “alpha”, “beta male” and “sigma” (#IaintnoSigma, from Mr. Bean).
