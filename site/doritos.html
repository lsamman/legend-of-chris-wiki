    Doritos

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Grub. Dust. Bass.

    Doritos
    TypeSnack
    Known consumersJesse Pinkman (attempted)
    SourcesThe Quick-Check
    DerivativesDorito dust; "doros bass"
    First appearanceThe Flashback Chapter
    Items & Technology

    Doritos are a snack that appears in various forms in the later canon, often alongside Mountain Dew.

    Appearances

    • Wormhole advertising: When the Inav entered a wormhole after leaving Gotham, it was "surrounded in a tunnel of Doritos and mountain dew ads, xbox live cod clip comps and skrilla."
    • The Quick-Check: Jesse Pinkman used a port-a-potty "to go to a quick-check to score some Doritos". He returned with Mountain Dews and Zyns, and the Doritos are not confirmed.
    • Doros bass: DJ Khaled "laid out a sick beat with plenty of doros bass". Most editors take "doros" to mean Doritos.
    • Dorito dust: In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, a mysterious door appeared in the 360 Chambers, "smothered in Dorito dust and mountain dew oil". It let MF Vroom and the New Crips escape.
    • Pol Phi Doros: Osama Bin Bomb'in's fraternity, Pol Phi Doros, may also be named after the snack. This is unconfirmed.

    Trivia

    Doritos and Mountain Dew are traditionally associated with gamer culture, which the canon celebrates throughout.
