    Red Bull

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Four cans and a lightsaber: the standard senatorial loadout.

    Red Bull
    TypeEnergy drink

    Items & Technology

    Red Bull is an energy drink that appears at two of the most dramatic moments in the canon: the last stand at the Galactic Senate and the night watch over the Verizon contract. Scholars agree it is one of the few items in the book that is used for exactly what it is for.

    Contents
    1. In the Siege of the Senate
    2. At the 6 7 Casino
    3. Trivia

    In the Siege of the Senate

    During the Siege of the Galactic Senate, Bon Jovi pushed out all of the 80-year-old senators except one: RFK. RFK declared his readiness by listing his inventory, which consisted of "4 Red Bulls and a Lightsaber". It is never stated how many of the four were consumed before the Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate began, but given the debate's length, fan consensus is "all of them".

    At the 6 7 Casino

    In the Flashback Chapter, Microsoft Bing hires Hank Hill, Jesse and Mr. White to guard the relic. The crew walked down to the vault "with their casino issued scars and red bulls", then "slammed their red bulls and nodded off, in no fear, since their scars were gold".

    In Chapter the Maybe Seventh, woken by alarms, Truth Social notifications and Yuno Miles, Hank "chugged the last of his red bull" before grabbing his golden SCAR.

    Trivia

    • Red Bull is the only energy drink known to cause sleep in the canon; the casino guards fall asleep immediately after drinking theirs.
    • Rival beverages in the canon include G Fuel, Prime, Mountain Dew and Diet Dr Pepper.
    • Real-world: Red Bull's slogan is "Red Bull gives you wings", which may or may not be connected to Erger Wings (unconfirmed).
