    The Super Bowl

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · Kanye's only public appearance (allegedly).

    The Super Bowl
    TypeSporting event
    Known attendeesKanye West (allegedly, exclusively)
    Cited byWalter White
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Events

    The Super Bowl is mentioned once. While planning the Weezing of Kanye, Walter White asked: "how are we going to get near him though, I though Kanye only came out during the Super Bowl?" Because of this, the crew broke into Kanye's house instead.

    The rest of the canon disagrees. Kanye is later seen at a corner store, in Gotham, at a nightclub, and in a casino basement, so either White was wrong or every one of those days was Super Bowl Sunday. The Super Bowl is the championship game of American football.
