    The TON Robots

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations

    Factions & Organizations

    The TON Robots (or “the TON’s”) are the “monolithically sized defensive bio-robotic weapons robots” that Osama Bin Bom'in built at the start of the O Block 2 era, when O'Block was being scaled to “a EU level nation”. The most infamous of them is TON 618.

    Contents
    1. Threat
    2. Known members
    3. Trivia

    Threat

    When the heroes discuss retaking the Projects, RFK judges it hopeless:

    “We can’t survive taking back the projects, the TON’s are going to level the city with us chuds in it, but what if we just try to just get Facebook”

    Because of the TONs, the plan changes from liberating the nation to a quick raid for Facebook (Pre-Cringe).

    Known members

    • TON 618, the flagship, housed in DPF Facility 69420.
    • Others are implied but not named. The “robotic screeches... heard from horizon to horizon” at the end of the chapter may come from several of them.

    Trivia

    • “Bio-robotic” suggests they are partly alive. TON 618 is called “one big robotic creature”.
