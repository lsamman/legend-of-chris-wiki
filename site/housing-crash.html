    The Housing Market Crash of 2007/2008

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · Also the last time anyone saw Chris.

    The Housing Market Crash of 2007/2008
    TypeEconomic catastrophe / disappearance
    Date2007/2008 (the canon allows both)
    ConsequenceChris can no longer be located
    First mentionedChapter Triangle
    Events

    The Housing Market Crash of 2007/2008 is, according to Plankton, the point after which Chris could no longer be found: "after the crash of the housing market in 2007/2008 we have been unable to locate it". Plankton calls Chris "it" here, which some editors read as a hint that Chris is no longer fully a person.

    The crash is the canon's only known real-world economic event. The only lead since then is that Chris has been traced to the Reformed Travis Scott Sector, where he is apparently buying a McDouble.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: the US housing bubble burst in 2007–2008 and set off the global financial crisis.
    • The crash happened around the same time that the Inav stopped being developed ("we continued to develop it until 2008") and that the iPhone 3G came out. 2008 is a busy year in the lore.
    • Fan theory: Chris caused the crash. Unproven, but very baller if true.
