    The Tech Bros

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Keepers of the secret teachings.

    The Tech Bros
    MembersLeBron James, Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg (and Chris)
    DoctrineThe Secret Teachings of Social Media
    FoundedApple Podcasts
    First appearanceChris Origins (named in Chapter Triangle)
    Factions & Organizations

    The Tech Bros is the name usually given to the group who balled into the sky with Chris “to learn about the secret teachings of twitter, myspace, instagram, snapchat and, facebook.” LeBron James's membership is accepted on this basis, despite his limited tech portfolio.

    Role in defeating Chris

    In Chapter Triangle, RFK states that “the only person capable of battling him is one who has met with the tech bros and has downloaded the sacred social media apps onto there nokia I-4500 flip phone with android wireless technology and 3 year contract plan from version for unlimited talk and text with there cutting edge 3g network.” This is the earliest version of what becomes the Prophecy.

    Continuity errors

    • The same passage says Chris “was before the social media and before the tech bros,” although the litany has him learning social media with them.
    • “version” is generally read as Verizon (see the Verizon contract).
