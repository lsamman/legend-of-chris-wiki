    Microsoft Bing

    Microsoft Bing is a large robot associated with The 6 7 Casino, who hires Hank Hill, Jesse Pinkman and Walter White to guard the casino's relic, the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text.

    Contents
    1. Appearance
    2. Role in the story
    3. Trivia

    Appearance

    Bing has "two wheel for movement, two robotic arms and a giant tv for a body". He is able to turn to look at people, suggesting the TV may display a face, though this is not stated.

    Role in the story

    Bing debuts by physically stopping the ball during the Great Roulette Bet ("it was not stopped by the board but by a large robot"), meaning the result of the bet is never known.

    He explains that "there is a group of individuals that include my son that intend on taking this casinos ancient relic that allows us to let our people gamble", and that the relic creates a Field of Gambling around any building it is in. Hank negotiates a fee of "15 gallons of your finest Prime US oil", which Bing calls "a high price" but agrees to "if your services are exceptional".

    Bing then leads the crew down the Stairway to Heaven to the relic and leaves them to guard it overnight. Hank's crew later sided with the thieves in the Casino Heist, so their services were arguably not exceptional, and the 15 gallons were presumably never paid.

    Trivia

    • Named after the real-world Microsoft search engine Bing.
    • Bing's phrase "our people" implies he leads a community of gamblers, possibly the residents of Sector 1/99.
    • Bing does not appear during the heist itself.
