    Lil Jokez, also known as The Joker, is a rapper and supervillain of Gotham City. According to Drake Batman, "The Joker's started a SoundCloud", which in Gotham constitutes an emergency.

    Contents
    1. Career
    2. The Battle of Club Shadowban
    3. Trivia

    Career

    Lil Jokez "had been releasing diss tracks targeting propane, ChatGPT, and capitalism's left ankle." Why capitalism's left ankle specifically has never been explained; its right ankle appears to be unharmed. His mixtape Laugh.exe "was infecting Gotham's infrastructure through bluetooth-enabled gargoyles" (Bluetooth-Enabled Gargoyles).

    He performed "every night" at Club Shadowban under a rotating giant Twitter X.

    The Battle of Club Shadowban

    When Kanye West and ChatGPT arrived, Lil Jokez opened with the bar quoted above, insulting Kanye's Propane and telling him to go back to Flint. Kanye and GPT answered with verses from 808s and Apocalypses, and the crowd exploded in Fortnite emotes.

    Lil Jokez "screamed and morphed into a 2007 JPEG of Heath Ledger." Drake Batman then tackled him into a meme portal. See The Battle of Club Shadowban.

    Trivia

    • Heath Ledger played the Joker in The Dark Knight (2008), which was in production in 2007, explaining the date on the JPEG.
    • The "Lil" prefix is a common rapper naming convention (compare Lil Uzi Vert and Litl Wayne).
    • Lil Jokez's dissing of propane is the only known point on which he agrees with Hank Hill, who also found Kanye's propane "watered down".
