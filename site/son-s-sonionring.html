    Son S. Sonionring

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Right-hand man. Janitor (undercover). Forgotten in the prologue.

    Characters

    Son S. Sonionring (also spelled SonionRing, and called Son or Sonion) is MF Vroom's right-hand man and the co-builder of O'Block's golden age. He enters the canon in Chapter 4+.1+.1…, and the narrator immediately admits to leaving him out of the earlier chapters:

    “We forgot to mention this in the prologue but Son S. Sonionring was MF Vroom’s right hand man.”

    He is a good shot with a modded colonial musket, drives a 2005 Honda Accord fitted with a machine gun, and owns a car stereo his speakers have learned to fear.

    Background

    When MF Vroom was exiled by Osama Bin Bom'in, Son “went into hiding... in fear that he too would have to listen to xbox live 2009 lobbies until his end”. Before that, he says, he and MF Vroom “worked together to bring the O’block to its glory.”

    He was not there for the O'Block Coup:

    “That day I was at my sisters wedding, If I were there everything would be different pogchamp.”

    Spaceport ambush

    Son goes undercover among the janitorial staff of the Projects National Spaceport. When Tung Tung Tung Sahur moves to arrest MF Vroom, Son comes forward with a muttered “Son”, then “GG fricken easy”, then “Fricken easy.” He cocks his musket, lifts his hood, and says:

    “Sonion ring you just made a big mistake.”

    He drops Triple T with one shot and answers MF Vroom's shocked “Son?!” with “At your service sir”.

    The chase

    During the breakout, Son shouts “Quick hop in my whip cuh!” and does “a completely unnecessary burn out” in the Accord. While being chased by Police Kirks he reaches across RFK (“invaded RFK’s person space”) into the glove box. Something clicks, and a machine gun comes out of the trunk firing solidified purple juice that pops every tire chasing them. He also puts on “I Can't Put Down the Cup” at a volume that makes his speakers beg for mercy.

    When RFK asks for roadkill, Son answers: “man bruh we already running from the cops why you wanna cook up some new Kennedy food”.

    Lore contributions

    Son explains a large part of the Projects' recent history. He covers the coup by the Pol Phi Doros frat, the demand that O'Block join the EU, the O'Block 2 era, and the creation of the TON robots, including TON 618 (“yall we might be screwed”).

    Trivia

    • His name puts the word “Son” in front of “Sonionring”. The middle initial is not explained.
    • Son is apparently the only person able to tell MF Vroom's story in order, which makes the prologue's failure to mention him stand out even more.
    • He introduces himself properly only after the cops have been lost: “I Don’t believe I’ve introduced myself properly... I doubt any of you know how baller I am.”
