    England

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · A new level of Roman.

    England
    TypeEmpire (Roman, newly)
    Notable sitesThe Smoke and Vape Shop
    SkyAdvertisements for free $10 Fortnite cards
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Locations

    England is the country Lightning McQueen flew to after he grew "sick of George Constanta". It is where he met Mr. Bean and Agent 007.

    The canon gives a short but glowing description: McQueen "saw glimmering lights and advertisements for free $10 Fortnite cards in the sky, England and certainly reached a new level of Roman, a new empire for the ages, beautiful and sophisticated."

    Trivia

    • England joins a growing list of Roman places in the canon, along with Canada, whose emperor is Joeius Bidenias, and the "eastern rome" that supplied Zuckerberg's cigars.
    • McQueen left for England from "the Soviet Union", though he had last been seen in Moscow, which the canon says is an Italian city.
    • The $10 Fortnite cards should not be confused with the 19 dollar Fortnite card that Steve Jobs later wants to liberate.
