    Harry (Zuckerberg's Cousin)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Smuggler. Chill like that.

    Harry (Zuckerberg's Cousin)
    RelationCousin of Mark Zuckerberg
    OccupationSmuggled meth for Roman emperors
    SuppliesCuban cigars from Eastern Rome
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Characters

    Harry is Mark Zuckerberg's cousin and the source of the Cuban cigars Zuckerberg “bought from eastern rome.” He appears only in RFK's train of thought in Chapter Triangle, just before RFK decides “nah bro screw that.”

    Trivia

    • Harry is described with the same phrase as his cousin (“chill like that and lowkey didnt care about anything”), which suggests chillness runs in the Zuckerberg family.
    • He is the second secret or minor cousin of a tech bro in the canon, after Elon Mush.
    • Which Roman emperors he worked for is not stated. Joeius Bidenias is the only Roman emperor named in the book.
