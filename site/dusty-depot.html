    Dusty Depot

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Trees, a port-a-potty, and absolutely no oil.

    Dusty Depot
    TypeWilderness / oil prospecting site

    Locations

    Dusty Depot is a location where Hank Hill, Jesse Pinkman and Walter White become stranded after being teleported there in the RV of time. It is the site of the trio's failed search for an Oil deposit, and, through a port-a-potty of extraordinary versatility, the gateway to The 6 7 Casino.

    Description

    Canon describes Dusty Depot sparingly. It contains at least one port-a-potty, a lawn, and trees, which Walter White gazes up into from his lawn chair. The trio's RV is parked there and is noted to be "damaged".

    No oil deposit is ever located in Dusty Depot, despite the crew being "in the middle of searching for a oil deposit in dusty depot as their oil ran out."

    History

    Arrival

    According to Hank in Chapter the Maybe Seventh, the crew "got teleported into dusty depot". How or why this happened is never explained. Their last known whereabouts before this were fleeing the Department of Defense and weezing Kanye in Chapter the Fourth.

    The supply run

    While Hank "smoked a fat one" outside the RV, Jesse used the port-a-potty to travel to a Quick-Check, returning with 2 Mountain Dews and a pack of zyns. The Quick-Check's clerk tipped him off about a nearby oil casino.

    Erger Wings

    While waiting for Hank to fetch his NFTs, Walter shared his retirement dream: to open a Mountain Dew refinery and restaurant chain in Dusty Depot called Erger Wings.

    Departure

    Jesse then "ripped open the porta-potty door revealing a bright portal made of swirls and particle effects", and the trio left Dusty Depot for the casino.

    Continuity errors

    • Hank opens the scene by saying "we ran out of propane", but the narration says it was their oil that ran out.
    • The vehicle is called "the rv of time" here, but it was a "drug van", a "crack van" and a "bus" in Chapter the Fourth.

    Trivia

    • Dusty Depot shares its name with a well-known named location in the video game Fortnite. This, along with the Golden SCAR, is often cited as evidence that this whole part of the canon takes place near the Fortnite map.
    • It is one of the few locations in the canon with trees.
