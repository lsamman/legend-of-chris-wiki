    Spamton NEO

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · The Secret Boss. Strung up.

    Spamton NEO
    SpeciesPuppet salesman
    AliasesSPAMTON G. SPAMTON (base form)
    LocationInside the Border
    Defeated byScott the Woz and Dick Vitale's Awesome Baby
    Possibly possessed byLeBron James
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Characters

    Spamton NEO is the final form of Spamton G. Spamton and the boss of the Border. He is the first form of Spamton the heroes meet in person; earlier he was only a voice on MF Vroom's Windows Phone.

    Contents
    1. Appearance
    2. Defeat
    3. Aftermath
    4. Fan theories
    5. Behind the scenes

    Appearance

    He arrives with “DEAR CHRIS!-”, then a verse the Wiki treats as his introduction: “SPAMTON G. SPAMTON / [his name] / I AM SECRET BOSS, / IN DELTARUNE GAME,”. The heroes “in shambles”: Steve's iGrenades are “promptly teleported out of existence”, Kanye drops bars on him, and Hank and Jesse open fire with Glocks whose magazines keep spawning in their steam wallets.

    Defeat

    Scott tears out a Sega Genesis and slams Dick Vitale's Awesome Baby into it. “Suddenly the game started up and with a loud ‘It’s awesome baby’ (bit crunched to 32bit perfection for the audiophiles of the Mongolian empire) SPAMTON NEO got ripped from his strings and a lone SPAMTON rested on the ground.”

    An echo of a basketball leaves the building, “a sign that LeBron James had left the building..”

    Aftermath

    The base Spamton says “DAMN [son] I DONT KNOW WHAT GOT INTO [me] LAST THING I REMEMBER I WAS [calling my dear friend] MF [vroom] AND SUDDENLY A MYSTERIOUS FIGURE IN [cybr city!!111] INJECTED ME WITH [flowers bloom in your heart]”. He does a little dance. He then sells Steve a bundle of Kromer and vanishes.

    The mysterious figure is not named. The basketball, though, is a clue; see below.

    Fan theories

    • LeBron was inside Spamton NEO. The book says only that his departure was signalled by a basketball echo. This fits LeBron having merged with Elon and joined Chris's side.
    • “Flowers bloom in your heart” has not been explained.

    Behind the scenes

    • Spamton NEO is the secret boss of Deltarune Chapter 2.
    • The first line of his verse is a quote from the game.
