    Field of Gambling

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Required for all gambling.

    Field of Gambling
    TypeSupernatural field
    SourceThe 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon and "few" other relics
    RangeAny building the relic is contained in
    Known instanceThe 6 7 Casino
    Concepts & Lore

    A Field of Gambling is a field created around any building containing certain relics, such as the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text. According to Microsoft Bing, the relic is what "allows us to let our people gamble", implying that gambling is not possible in the Legend of Chris universe without such a field.

    Bing says the relic is "one of the few in the universe" with this power, meaning other gambling relics exist. Fan theory: "that one in Vegas", which ChatGPT says has closed, lost its relic.

    It is unknown whether the field travelled with the card into the Inav, which would make the Inav a casino.
