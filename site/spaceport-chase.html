    The Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events

    The Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase is the action sequence in Chapter 4+.1+.1… in which the New Crips, newly joined by Son S. Sonionring, fight their way out of the Projects National Spaceport and drive to O'Block with the police chasing them. It is the canon's first car chase, unless you count Lightning McQueen's whole life.

    Prelude

    After Son incapacitates Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Son warns: “We have to move quick, Osama knows we’re here, Quickly we have to get to the grand apartment complex where Osama lives”. When asked where that is, the answer is “O Block 2”.

    The breakout

    “and they started running, straight through spaceport security, muskets firing, light sabers slicing, their body count was going crazy.”

    They crash through the front doors with Police Kirks behind them, onto streets of “Brick buildings and sketchy gas stations”.

    The chase

    The gang gets into Son's 2005 Honda Accord. The Police Kirks then “transformed into police cars and crashed through the front of the building”. In the middle of the chase, RFK asks:

    “How far i-is o block, is there road kill here I need raccon ******** for my next dish- a road bump interrupted him I-it is extremely important”

    Son replies, “man bruh we already running from the cops why you wanna cook up some new Kennedy food”. He then reaches into the glove box, which brings a machine gun out of the trunk. It fires solidified purple juice and pops every tire chasing them, while “I Can't Put Down the Cup” plays. After that it is “smooth sailing to O’block.”

    Casualties

    The body count is officially “going crazy”. No exact figure is given, so this is one of the few battles in the canon with no casualty number. Compare “over 10 casualties” in the Dabbing of the Haters.

    Trivia

    • RFK's roadkill request is the second in the chapter, after Spamton's request for intergalactic roadkill [orb shaped objects].
    • The Police Kirks are the transforming, law-enforcement version of the Charlie Kirks from the Casino Heist.
