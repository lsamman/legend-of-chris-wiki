    The Cooking of the Universe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · Sirloin steak with lobster tail.

    The Cooking of the Universe
    ChefFrodo Baggins
    EquipmentMaster Chef
    DishSirloin steak with lobster tail
    IngredientsThe universe
    ConsequenceThe Great Famine
    Events

    The Cooking of the Universe was carried out by Frodo after his conquest of Andromeda: “he used master chef to cook the universe into a sirloin steak with lobster tail. Frodo then ate it and left the face of the universe never to be baller again.”

    Analysis

    The event raises several questions the wiki cannot answer:

    • How Frodo left “the face of the universe” after eating it.
    • Where everyone lived afterward. The text answers this only indirectly, with the Great Famine.
    • Whether this was Zuckerberg's favourite universe, the only one spared by the Snap.

    The meal is the only surf and turf of cosmic scale in the canon.
