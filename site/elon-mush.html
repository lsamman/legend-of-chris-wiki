    Elon Mush is Elon Musk's secret cousin and, by output alone, the most consequential creator in the canon. He produced Settings, the Multiverse and, by accident, a universe of nazis, before dying of NFL poisoning.

    Biography

    “Then one day elon musk's secret cousin elon mush mooshed all the chalkboards on earth and then used its power to create the ani-social media platform which was named settings.” (See The Chalkboards of Earth.)

    “Mush also created the multiverse so he could recreate “Spider-man Across the spider verse”,” and in the process accidentally created the Cringe Universe. This prompted LeBron James to send in the Baller Army.

    After Frodo ate the universe, “there was a great famine and elon mush died of nfl poisoning.” His cousin then merged with LeBron to become Samwise.

    Afterlife and the 5G problem

    Mush's death did not end his relevance. At the iPhone 3G Keynote, Steve Jobs explains that the iPhone 3G uses 3G “Because 4G is for the weak, and 5G awakens Elon Mush's dormant twin.”

    In Chapter the Maybe Seventh, Jobs worries that the Flip Phone Covenant now runs on 5G, to which the reply is: “Elon mush.. yeah that no good boy.” The plan is to roll back to 3G at the last moment, but ChatGPT warns that “we have to deal with elon when we deal with chris, if hes coming straight from the shadow realm then who know what he'll want.”

    Jobs also lists Mush, alongside Frodo Baggins and D. Piddy, among the “great minds” who once lived in Sector 1/99, the gambling addiction sector.

    Fan theories

    • The Twin Theory: the Elon expected from the Shadow Realm is the “dormant twin,” not Mush himself, who is confirmed dead.
    • The Musk-Mush Theory: the twin is Elon Musk. Most editors reject this, noting that Musk is a cousin, not a twin, and is also part of Samwise.

    Trivia

    • His cause of death is spelled “poisoning” in four recitations of the Cycle and “poisioning” in the other four.
    • Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is a 2023 animated film about a multiverse of Spider-People. Mush's version went considerably worse.
