    The Bible of Battle Passes

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Scripture, Season 1 through Season ∞.

    The Bible of Battle Passes
    TypeDocument (sacred)
    LocationThe Lobby File Cabinet, Dunder Mifflin
    Noticed byRFK
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Items & Technology

    The Bible of Battle Passes is a document in the Lobby File Cabinet of Dunder Mifflin. RFK lists it among the "many interesting Things" in the cabinet, between Fortnite and the Call of Duty file. Nobody reads it, because "MF. V'room is waiting for us".

    Its contents are unknown. A battle pass is a paid seasonal reward track in games like Fortnite and Call of Duty, so the Bible is assumed to be their scripture.

    Related scripture

    • When the Inav comes out of a wormhole, a voice announces: "We're Approaching the drop point epic gamers, buy your battle passes". Some editors read this as a quote from the Bible.
    • Plankton, a veteran of the Great Fortnite Island Wars, chants "Fornite battle pass, I just-" by instinct. This is a real-world meme and may be a verse from the Bible.
