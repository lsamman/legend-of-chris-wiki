    Characters

    Hank Hill is a propane magnate whose "propane empire" works with Los Polos Harmonicas Under System Gang to distribute "propane and propane accessories". He is labeled a threat by the Department of Defense, has a feud with Kanye West over watered-down propane, and goes from guarding the Verizon contract to joining the people trying to steal it.

    Biography

    Standoff at the Twelve Towers

    Chapter the Fourth opens with "Damn son where'd you find those?" and Hank standing "atop the twelve towers". Below him was the DOD, an army "divided by Jeff bezosi and Mark zucc", recently "deployed to the Kanye system to contain a new threat, that being hank hill." The DOD told him that Snoopy McSpookintosh was in their custody and that he had no one left to help him. Hank warned them: "If I were you id be counting my lucky stars because the wrath of Jesse and white be upon ye". Then the drug van from Breaking Bad came out of hyperspace, blue dust rained down, and Hank escaped, saying "I knew you'd be here, now I have meeting with Gus fring".

    The Weezing of Kanye

    Hank needed "ingredients from Kanye", but "our twitter account doesn't have 100 followers yet and we aren't verified yet". His alternative was to "find him and weeze him" by breaking into his house. It worked, but the propane was watered down. See The Weezing of Kanye.

    Dusty Depot

    In the Flashback Chapter, Hank is stuck in Dusty Depot with a damaged RV and no oil ("Gosh dang it Jesse boy, we ran out of propane"). When Jesse heard about an oil casino nearby, Hank brought out his "mysterious apes on my bitcoin Wallet" as gambling money and went through the port-a-potty portal to the 6 7 Casino.

    Casino security

    After the roulette bet was cut short by Microsoft Bing, Hank negotiated a deal to guard the casino's relic in exchange for "15 gallons of your finest Prime US oil". He slept in a beach chair at the bottom of the Stairway to Heaven holding a casino-issued SCAR. When the heist began, he woke up, grabbed his golden SCAR, and ran into Kanye, his old enemy.

    Alliance

    Kanye asked why Hank was guarding the card, and Hank admitted the crew had had no oil since Dusty Depot, leaving them "broke and big sad". When he heard that defeating Chris could free up the oil reserves and make Balling free, Hank "looked at Jesse and nodded" and joined Kanye's side. He was there when Steve Jobs fired the Ibeam at the vault.

    Personality

    Hank speaks in a heavy folksy dialect ("Well I Say boy that there bet ought to be good there son") and cares deeply about propane quality. When he found the watered-down propane, his first concern was for "all the barbecue bros all the car enthusiasts all the people who needed to commit arson".

    Trivia

    • Hank Hill is the main character of the animated sitcom King of the Hill, where he sells "propane and propane accessories".
    • His crew are Jesse and Walter from Breaking Bad, a show set in a different city.
    • He brings up the Gus Fring meeting while escaping the DOD and never mentions it again.
