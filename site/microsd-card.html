    The 67GB MicroSD Card

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    The 67GB MicroSD Card
    Capacity67GB
    ContentsFacebook (Pre-Cringe)
    FoundUnder a building, in the rubble
    LocationNational Archives / the palace
    Also called“the ssd”
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    The 67GB MicroSD Card is the storage medium holding Facebook (Pre-Cringe), one of the apps the Prophecy requires. MF Vroom and Son explain:

    “We have Facebook (Pre-Cringe) in the national archives.. Its been one of our prize possessions since the nations inception, we’re not quite sure how it got here, but under a build building, in the rubble we found it on a 67gb micro sd card.”

    Current location

    Moments later someone says “The ssd would still be in the palace. We would just have to dip before we start tweaking twin.” The card is therefore in O'Block, held by Osama's regime, and recovering it is the heroes' next goal: “Computah locate Facebook…”

    Continuity errors

    • MicroSD or SSD? It is called a “67gb micro sd card” and then “the ssd” a few lines later. Those are different kinds of storage.
    • Archives or palace? It is said to be in the national archives and then “in the palace”. Fans think the archives may be inside the palace. This is a fan theory.
    • “67” is a running number in the canon, as in the 6 7 Casino and “67 ioann rizz”.
