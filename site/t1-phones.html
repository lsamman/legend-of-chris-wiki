    T1 Phones

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Standard Kirk issue.

    T1 Phones
    UsersCharlie Kirks
    AppsTruth Social
    Combat effectivenessNone
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Items & Technology

    T1 Phones are the phones carried by Charlie Kirks. When Steve Jobs attacked the first Kirk during the Casino Heist, "The other kirks quickly took out their T1 phones and opened their truth social accounts" to post about politics, rather than fighting back. They were then incapacitated.

    The name refers to the real-world T1 phone announced by Trump Mobile in 2025. In-universe, the T1 is the only phone in the canon not covered by the Prophecy, the Flip Phone Covenant, or Apple. Its use of Truth Social, promoted by Trumpy the Red in Chapter 2, is consistent with that lore.
