    Bing's Son

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · The canon's greatest unsolved paternity case.

    Bing's Son
    FatherMicrosoft Bing
    AffiliationA group intending to steal the casino's relic
    IdentityUnknown
    StatusUnknown
    First mentionedThe Flashback Chapter
    Characters

    Bing's Son is the unnamed son of Microsoft Bing. According to his father, he is part of "a group of individuals" intending to steal the Verizon contract from The 6 7 Casino. The son is never identified, and the canon never returns to the subject.

    Fan theories

    Since the only group shown stealing the relic is the Boys (Steve Jobs, Kanye West and ChatGPT), fans have long debated which of them, if any, is Bing's son. All of the following are theories, not canon.

    • ChatGPT: The most popular theory, since in the real world Microsoft is closely tied to the company behind ChatGPT and its technology powers Bing's chatbot. Supporters also point out that ChatGPT inexplicably says "Aight bet boss" in the scene where Bing hires Hank's crew, despite not being there, suggesting it was present in some capacity.
    • Steve Jobs: Considered unlikely, given the historic rivalry between Apple and Microsoft. Supporters call this "exactly why it would be a twist".
    • Kanye West: Has no evidence whatsoever.
    • Son S. Sonionring: A fringe theory based entirely on the name of Son S. Sonionring. Son is in the projects at the time and not involved in the heist.
    • A different group: Some readers argue Bing was describing another gang entirely, who never showed up.
