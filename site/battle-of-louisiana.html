    The Battle of Louisiana

    Events From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Six hours. Claude is tireless. The Dunkin Alliance is not.

    The Battle of Louisiana
    WhenOctober 28th to sunrise, October 29th, 1995
    WhereLouisiana, among the data centers
    SidesThe Dunkin Alliance against Claude, King of AI
    Length6 hours
    ResultThe Alliance is beaten, until Vellatosis
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The Battle of Louisiana begins when Vinny B. James, enraged by the Super Bowl plot, tackles Claude, King of AI. "The two battle, destroying some Data Centers on the way. Del, Aesop, and Will join in. The battle lasts for 6 Hours. But Claude is tireless, while the Dunkin Alliance is not."

    Sunrise

    "The sun rises as Claude stands over them." Claude: "The Age of Claude is just beginning. AI will rule and no human will remain." Fire burns around the battlefield.

    The question

    Will Vella stands up and asks one thing before dying: "How does one achieve Vellatosis?" Claude explains it. That is his mistake (The Day of the Bagel Man).

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
