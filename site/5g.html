    5G

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Awakens the twin.

    5G
    TypeNetwork standard
    Known effectAwakens Elon Mush's dormant twin
    Used byThe Flip Phone Covenant
    Avoided byiPhone 3G
    First mentionThe Flashback Chapter
    Concepts & Lore

    5G is a mobile network standard and, according to the iPhone 3G Keynote, a threat to all of existence, because it awakens Elon Mush's dormant twin. This is the official reason the iPhone 3G runs at 3G.

    The Covenant problem

    In the TurboGrafx 16 sector, Steve Jobs revealed that "the current flip phone covenant uses 5g, and if we connect the iPhone to that.." Someone finished: "Elon mush.. yeah that no good boy."

    Steve's plan is to "bypass the G by loading a social media first, we'd have to roll back to 3G but we could do that right when we need to activate it." That would be "Right when we're in Chris's face." ChatGPT disagreed: "If we do that then we have to deal with elon when we deal with chris, if hes coming straight from the shadow realm then who know what he'll want." Steve: "Its not like we have a choice GPT."

    Continuity notes

    • Elon Mush died of NFL poisoning in the origin story. The keynote's "dormant twin" may get around this, but GPT talks about "elon" coming from the Shadow Realm, which raises the question of which Elon is meant.
    • 4G is dismissed as "for the weak" and plays no further part.
