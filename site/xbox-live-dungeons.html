    The Xbox Live Dungeons

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Barbaric. Horrific. Endless.

    The Xbox Live Dungeons
    TypeDungeons
    LocationCall of Duty HQ
    Hazards7-year-olds, modded controllers
    Described byIoannus Paranukus
    First mentionedDunder Mifflan
    Locations

    The Xbox Live Dungeons are the prison facilities of Call of Duty HQ that hold MF Vroom. Ioannus Paranukus uses them to warn the party about their chances. Known dangers are being "burned alive by the 7 year olds screaming obscenities at you" and "headshots from xbox 360 players with modded controllers".

    Plankton's full response is "Nah we'll be fine".

    Relation to other locations

    The dungeons seem to be the same as, or closely tied to, the COD lobbies MF Vroom was exiled to. Son later describes the sentence as having "to listen to xbox live 2009 lobbies until his end", and in the 360 Chambers "the screams and slurs of xbox live lobbies" come from every hallway.

    Real-world: online lobbies on the Xbox 360 era Xbox Live were famous for very young players yelling.
