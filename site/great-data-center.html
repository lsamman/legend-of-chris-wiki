    The Great Data Center is what Claude, King of AI announces on his livestreams: he "was planning to build the Great Data Center in Louisiana. He stole fresh water, too."

    Contents
    1. The confrontation
    2. Destruction
    3. On the spin-off wiki

    The confrontation

    The Dunkin Alliance teleports to Claude in "a Data Center inside Louisiana", where he is playing Wordle. Claude throws Vinny B. James back down into it and he "crashes into some GPUs".

    Destruction

    The battle destroys some data centers, starting fires. The Vellas destroy all the rest and use the parts to build the Dunkin Cult Cruiser.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
