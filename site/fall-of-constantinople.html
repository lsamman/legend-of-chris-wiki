    The Fall of Constantinople

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · 14:74pm, Unc Standard Time.

    Date14:74pm UST
    LocationConstantinople
    BelligerentsThe Goober Gang vs. The Barack Empire (offscreen), the local mafia, a Popeyes cashier

    ChapterChapter the Fourth
    Events

    The Fall of Constantinople is the climax of The Goober Gang's story arc. The narration begins with "Meanwhile" and the words "In. Constantinople things were chaos".

    Course of events

    Deployment

    Mr. Bean and 007 planted the first two nukes "inside various bags of old pagers". McQueen was responsible for the last two drop points, the abandoned Walmarts on the nadir side. Over the radio, Bean reported that "Putin he's almost in the center" and that 007 had "lost his torso" but "could be fine after I give him a few anti-depressants."

    The Walmarts

    At the first Walmart, McQueen was met by the local mafia ("Hey sonny how dare you infringe on our territory!"). He "declined the request to be part of society" and dropped off the nuke. The second Walmart had become a Popeyes. McQueen placed a large order, pulled his Glock 19 to get it, ate, and only then dropped off the nuke.

    Extraction

    Meanwhile Bean was having coffee with Putin. He excused himself and waited with 007 at 8aller Jump Street. McQueen picked them up (007 "was almost out of juice on his vape") and flew past "the seen of vaporized dababy in a suspended animation cell" into the wormhole.

    Constantinople fell at 14:74pm Unc standard time and that was the last anyone heard from the goober gang for now…

    Continuity errors

    • The plan was to lure Obamna to the center, but Putin is the one reported there.
    • The oil flooding, the main part of the plan, is never mentioned during the operation.
    • 14:74pm is not a real time on any clock.
    • The wormhole "theroreticly could open", and apparently it did.
