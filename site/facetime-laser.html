    The Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Ultra. Mega. Uber. Cool.

    The Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser
    Full nameThe Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser
    Host deviceiPhone 3G
    Power requirementChatGPT plugged in
    Used onPale beige brick wall of the casino
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Items & Technology

    The Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser is a laser emitted from Steve Jobs's phone. Steve asked: "Chat gpt can you plug into my iPhone and turn on the ultra mega uber cool FaceTime laser?" When ChatGPT plugged in, "a huge laser shot out from Steve Jobs's phone and started slowly cutting a entrance into the pale beige brick making up the casino's walls." The cut-out section fell away, and the crew entered the casino at the start of the Casino Heist.

    Notes

    • FaceTime is Apple's video calling app. The real iPhone 3G could not run FaceTime and has no front camera (per the keynote: "To avoid self-reflection"), which makes the laser even more impressive.
    • Steve introduced it right after complaining about sourcing gadgets locally since Tim Apple retired.
