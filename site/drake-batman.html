    Drake Batman

    Drake Batman is the protector of Gotham City. He is "not just any Batman" but Drake Batman, and he is responsible for recruiting Kanye West against Lil Jokez.

    Contents
    1. Appearance
    2. Role in the story
    3. Behind the scenes

    Appearance

    Drake Batman emerged after "a shriek echoed through the alleys" and stepped "out of a cloud of dubstep and unpaid parking tickets". He wears Ugg boots and was "sipping lean out of a golden utility belt."

    Role in the story

    He told Kanye he had been watching him on the Ring camera and that the Joker had started a SoundCloud. Kanye sighed: "Man, I knew it'd come to this."

    At the climax of the Battle of Club Shadowban, after Lil Jokez morphed into a 2007 JPEG of Heath Ledger, "Drake Batman threw a smoke grenade filled with peach-flavored Axe body spray and tackled him into a meme portal" (The Meme Portal). Neither has been seen since. It is not established whether Drake Batman went through the portal as well, or merely did the tackling.

    Behind the scenes

    • Drake is a Canadian rapper. His real-world relationship with Kanye West has been famously rocky, which makes this team-up notable.
    • Ring is a brand of doorbell cameras. Using one to watch Kanye is not explained.
    • Axe is a body spray brand.
