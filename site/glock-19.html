    McQueen's Glock 19

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Used once, for chicken.

    McQueen's Glock 19
    OwnerLightning McQueen
    TypeHandgun
    Known useGetting a Popeyes order
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Items & Technology

    McQueen's Glock 19 is a handgun that Lightning McQueen "pulled out" at the Popeyes in Constantinople to "demand his order from the cashier at the register". The weapon was not fired. The cashier handed over the order, McQueen said "Thank you", and he ate it.

    This is the Glock's only appearance. Why a car carries a handgun, and where he keeps it, is not discussed.
