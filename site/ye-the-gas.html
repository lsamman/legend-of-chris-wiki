    Ye the Gas

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · No watered-down trash. (Disputed.)

    Ye the Gas
    OwnerKanye West
    ProductPropane
    Retail outletThe Corner Store (Ohio)
    MarketingA song; the "water gas trend"
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Items & Technology

    Ye the Gas is Kanye West's propane brand. Kanye planned to promote it with a song, which he hoped would "ensure the continuation of the water gas trend ive been creating". ChatGPT assured him it would, "for your are baller".

    The brand name became Kanye's opening line at the Battle of Club Shadowban: "I'm Ye the Gas, no watered-down trash". Editors have noted that this contradicts both Hank Hill's finding that Kanye's Propane "was watered down" and the "water gas trend" Kanye was proud of. The name is a pun on Kanye's nickname "Ye".
