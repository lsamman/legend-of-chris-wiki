    Vinny B. James is the hero of The Epic of Vinny. He is a cashier at a Burger King in Boston. On the Fourth of July, 1995, he ends up in the The Backrooms, aboard the S.S. Vessel and in the AI Shadow Realm, where he lets Claude, King of AI out. He spends the next four months putting that right.

    Before the story

    Vinny lives in Boston and works the register at a Burger King. His British boss will not let him take a vacation day for Independence Day, 1995. He wears a watch from 5 Below, and he takes the customers seriously.

    Chapter 1

    A customer orders a Wendy's sandwich at his register and Vinny politely redirects them. He and Tyrone then enter the The Backrooms through the The Freezer, where Vinny hits Garret the Forklift Guy with the The Forklift and gets carried off by FRIEND.

    Chapter 2

    Vinny joins Captain Gaster's crew, insults Taking Back Sunday, and is stabbed by the Roaring Knight. He drowns, or nearly does, and wakes in the AI Shadow Realm. He tricks his way into Claude's castle by Lying (as Polydoros), loses at Smash, and opens the portal. The shadow figures reward him with a Shadow Cape.

    Chapter 3

    Vinny confronts Claude on Garth and is thrown into Wyoming. He recovers, finds a Dunkin, and assembles an alliance with Will Vella, Aesop Rock and Del the Funky Homosapien (The Dunkin Alliance).

    With the Dunkin Alliance he survives the Mystery Machine crash, is teleported to Texas against his wishes, and trains in the ways of Freedom for three months (The Freedom Road Trip). In Louisiana he is choked by Claude again, thrown into some GPUs, and loses a six-hour battle. After the Vellas win it for him, he spares Claude in exchange for Louisiana, then throws him into space anyway: "AMERICA, BITCH!!!!" (The End of the Age of Claude)

    Chapter 4

    Four days later Vinny is "back to his 9-5" at the Burger King. "No one gave him credit for stopping Claude from his rule." (Back to the 9-5)

    In the prologue

    A flash-forward shows Vinny fighting TON 618 in the Kanye System, backed by The VIGOROUS V, with Vinny's Vibrant Sword, Vinny's Titanium Armor and a cape of shadows. The power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese flows through him.

    Trivia

    • Vinny says "Aight, bet" every time he agrees to something dangerous. See "Aight, bet".
    • He claims he lost to Claude because he was "using the crappy controller".
    • Vinny can fly from the end of Chapter 2 onward. The Shadow Cape grants "strength and flight": he flies out of the portal, into Claude, out of a mountain, and into Claude again.
    • An earlier version of the prologue spelled his name "Vinney" once. The updated text fixes it.
