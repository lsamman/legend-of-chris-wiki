    Samwise

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Two men, one fusion, the father of Chris.

    Samwise
    ComponentsElon Musk + LeBron James
    FormedThe Merging
    ChildrenChris
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Characters

    Samwise is the fusion of Elon Musk and LeBron James, and the creator of Chris. Samwise's whole canonical career fits in two sentences: he is formed, and he creates Chris, who is then born in Newark.

    The paradox

    Because Chris balled into the sky alongside Musk and LeBron before they merged into Samwise, Samwise creates someone who already knew both halves of him. Editors usually treat this as evidence that the Cycle is a literal loop rather than a set of misprints.

    Trivia

    • Samwise appears directly after Frodo Baggins leaves the face of the universe, which in The Lord of the Rings is roughly where the real Samwise Gamgee comes in.
    • After Chris's birth Samwise is never mentioned again, and Musk and LeBron are referred to separately from then on.
