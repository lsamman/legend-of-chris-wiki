    The Verse-Wide Tour

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · The very first.

    The Verse-Wide Tour
    ArtistYuno Miles
    ScopeThe universe
    Attendance"Gangs from all over"
    LegacyCemented Yuno Miles as "a historically ass artist"
    Events

    The Verse-Wide Tour was the "very first verse-wide tour", undertaken by Yuno Miles after he became the last rapper following the Gawk-ination of 2039. According to Steve Jobs, "Gangs from all over bought tickets for him, and eventually the verse-wide tour cemented him as a historically ass artist. That's why his music is still prominent even today."

    It is unclear whether "verse-wide" means universe-wide or multiverse-wide, or is a pun on rap verses. Given that the Legend of Chris multiverse includes at least the America Universe and the Shadow Realm, the logistics would have been considerable.
