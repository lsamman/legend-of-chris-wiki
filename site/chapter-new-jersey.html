    Chapters

    Chapter Number New Jersey is the ninth and newest chapter of The Legend of Chris. For a while it was only a title. It now has a body: the gang reaches Earth, New Jersey, survives an ambush by Yeat and Quandale Dingle, finds Mike Schmidt in Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, and begins Night 1.

    The title

    The printed title is “Chaper Number New Jersey, the newest jersey, dare I say the most modern jersey I have seen yet, believe me I've seen alot of jerseys and none are as meta as this jersey”. It keeps the book's tradition of chapter titles that are really sentences (Chapter the Fourth, Dunder Mifflan, Chapter the Maybe Seventh). It is also the first chapter to give itself a number-shaped word (“Number”) and then decline to say which number.

    The spelling “Chaper” is kept in the printed title and is not a Wiki typo.

    Synopsis

    Morning on the iNav

    Plankton is woken by Ioannus Paranukus, who wants to do accounting as his career. Plankton describes a dream in which Travis Scott wanted squad wins for the boys. RFK asks how he slept: “Felt like a victory royal in Fortnite season 2.” Outside, the two Tesla 2028 roadsters are docked to the iNav. Steve Jobs announces Earth, New Jersey, and tells Siri to navigate to Mike Schmidt. A voice screams for Michael and Dorisdos.

    Salty Springs

    The iNav lands in Salty Springs on its ilegs, at a birthday party. Yeat and Quandale Dingle jump the gang with infinity blades and demand the iPhone 3G (The Salty Springs Ambush). Plankton shouts “USA POWER!” and beams Quandale into a vessel. Kanye fights Yeat on top of a Lambo Tonka until God puts a hole through Yeat and the Earth (The Smiting of Yeat).

    Freddy Fazbear's Pizza

    The gang arrives at about 11pm, having lost time scrolling reels in the Wawa parking lot. Plankton finds dead kids and possibly a Golden Freddy in the back rooms. In the office, a golden plaque promises Snapchat: Ghost Protocol to whoever spends five nights. Mike Schmidt appears, hands over a flashlight and a covid mask, flashes a peace sign and fades away. The doors close. Night 1.

    Who is on the ship

    The chapter opens with Plankton, RFK and Ioannus Paranukus aboard the iNav with Steve Jobs, Kanye West and ChatGPT. The last chapter left the New Crips in O'Block 2 and the New Apple Employees in a roadster, and the book does not say how the two groups met. Hank Hill, MF Vroom and Son are not mentioned in the chapter.

    Trivia

    • Ioannus Paranukus is also spelled “Ioann Paranukus” here, and gets an official catchphrase: “Man shut yo bitch ass up”.
    • The Verizon plan appears in its longest form yet: “The 3 year contract plan from Verizon for unlimited talk and text”.
    • The back rooms of the pizzeria are “not to be confused with the backrooms”.
    • Plankton makes a SnapCube reference and says so.
