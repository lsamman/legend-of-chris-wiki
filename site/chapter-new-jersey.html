    Chapters

    Chapter Number New Jersey is the ninth and newest chapter of The Legend of Chris. For a while it was only a title. It now has a body: Steve Jobs, Kanye West and ChatGPT reach Earth, New Jersey, survive an ambush by Yeat and Quandale Dingle, find Mike Schmidt in Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, and begin Night 1.

    The title

    The printed title is “Chaper Number New Jersey, the newest jersey, dare I say the most modern jersey I have seen yet, believe me I've seen alot of jerseys and none are as meta as this jersey”. It keeps the book's tradition of chapter titles that are really sentences (Chapter the Fourth, Dunder Mifflan, Chapter the Maybe Seventh). It is also the first chapter to give itself a number-shaped word (“Number”) and then decline to say which number.

    The spelling “Chaper” is kept in the printed title and is not a Wiki typo.

    Synopsis

    Morning on the iNav

    The chapter opens on Plankton, woken by hearing Ioannus Paranukus say he wants to do accounting as his career, “as a distant voice in his ear”. It then moves to the iNav, where Kanye walks the cabin “redbull-less and sleep deprived” and describes a dream in which Travis Scott wanted squad wins for the boys. Steve asks how he slept: “Felt like a victory royal in Fortnite season 2.” The two Tesla 2028 roadsters are docked outside. Steve announces Earth, New Jersey and tells Siri to navigate to Mike Schmidt. A voice screams for Michael and Dorisdos. “Strange..” says Kanye.

    Salty Springs

    The iNav lands in Salty Springs on its ilegs, at a birthday party. Yeat and Quandale Dingle jump the gang with infinity blades and demand the iPhone 3G (The Salty Springs Ambush). Steve shouts “AIRPOWER!”, turns into a Mac charging brick and beams Quandale into a vessel. Kanye fights Yeat on top of a Lambo Tonka until God puts a hole through Yeat and the Earth (The Smiting of Yeat).

    Freddy Fazbear's Pizza

    The gang arrives at about 11pm, having lost time scrolling reels in the Wawa parking lot. In the back rooms there are dead kids and possibly a Golden Freddy. In the office, a golden plaque promises Snapchat: Ghost Protocol to whoever spends five nights. Mike Schmidt appears, hands over a flashlight and a covid mask, flashes a peace sign and fades away. The doors close. Night 1.

    Who is on the ship

    The iNav carries Steve Jobs, Kanye West and ChatGPT, who left Chapter 4+.1+.1… bound for the New Jersey system. Hank Hill is not mentioned. The New Crips are not with them: the chapter's first sentence only shows Plankton hearing Ioannus's voice “as a distant voice in his ear”.

    Two lines in the pizzeria still name New Crips: Plankton looks in the back rooms and spots the plaque, and “Ioann Paranukus” shouts “IS IT EPSTIEN” at Mike. An earlier version of the chapter put Plankton, RFK and Ioannus aboard the iNav for the whole chapter; the author has since corrected the opening.

    Trivia

    • Ioannus Paranukus gets an official catchphrase, “Man shut yo bitch ass up”, but it is ChatGPT who says it here.
    • The Verizon plan appears in its longest form yet: “The 3 year contract plan from Verizon for unlimited talk and text”.
    • The back rooms of the pizzeria are “not to be confused with the backrooms”.
    • Steve Jobs makes a SnapCube reference and says so.
    • AIRPOWER is named after a real Apple product that was cancelled before it shipped.
