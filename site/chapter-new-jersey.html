    Chapters

    Chapter Number New Jersey is the ninth chapter heading of The Legend of Chris, added to the MASTER FILE in the October 2026 update. At the time of writing it consists of a title and nothing else. The text under it is blank, which makes it the shortest chapter in the canon by a margin that no future chapter can ever beat.

    Contents
    1. The title
    2. Setting the scene
    3. Trivia

    The title

    The printed title is “Chaper Number New Jersey, the newest jersey, dare I say the most modern jersey I have seen yet, believe me I've seen alot of jerseys and none are as meta as this jersey”. It keeps the book's tradition of chapter titles that are really sentences (Chapter the Fourth, Dunder Mifflan, Chapter the Maybe Seventh). It is also the first chapter to give itself a number-shaped word (“Number”) and then decline to say which number.

    The spelling “Chaper” is kept in the printed title and is not a Wiki typo.

    Setting the scene

    The previous chapter ends as the New Apple Employees climb into the last Tesla roadster and Steve Jobs announces: “Alright boys, its time to go to the New Jersey system”. That makes the New Jersey system the next destination, and presumably the setting of this chapter.

    Trivia

    • Unlike the other chapter titles, this heading is centred on the reading page.
    • By line count it is three lines long, all of them the title.
    • “Meta” appears in the title as a quality of a jersey. The book does not elaborate.
