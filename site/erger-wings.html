    Erger Wings

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · A Mountain Dew refinery and restaurant. Coming eventually.

    Erger Wings
    TypePlanned business (refinery and restaurant chain)
    FounderWalter White (planned)
    LocationDusty Depot
    ProductMountain Dew
    StatusNot yet opened
    Concepts & Lore

    Erger Wings is Walter White's dream business: a Mountain Dew refinery and restaurant, to be opened in Dusty Depot when he and Jesse "grow old", and later expanded into a chain. Walter shares the idea while sitting on a lawn chair looking up at the trees. Jesse's only response is to nod.

    Analysis

    The concept of refining Mountain Dew is consistent with later canon, in which the 360 Chambers contain pipes of Mountain Dew described as "an organic substance that has the power to make you super human, or super dead", and a door smothered in "mountain dew oil". This suggests Mountain Dew is, in-universe, a natural resource.

    The meaning of "Erger" is never explained. Fan theory: some readers believe it is a misspelling of "burger"; others insist it is a real word that Walter, a former chemistry teacher, simply knows and we don't.
