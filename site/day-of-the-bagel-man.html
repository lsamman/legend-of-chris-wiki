    The Day of the Bagel Man

    Events From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · The Dunkin Cult has formed.

    The Day of the Bagel Man
    WhenOctober 29th, 1995, at sunrise
    WhereLouisiana
    WhatVellatosis begins; the Dunkin Cult forms
    VictimClaude, King of AI
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The Day of the Bagel Man is the day Will Vella achieves Vellatosis. Having heard Claude, King of AI's explanation, Will starts shaking, grows "another eye, a leg, and an arm" and splits, again and again. "The Dunkin Cult has formed. The Day of the Bagel Man is today."

    What the Vellas do

    They "jump Claude's ass and beat the shit out of him, outnumbering him". The Bagel Man puts on a white cloak with the Dunkin Donuts logo. "THE KANYE SYSTEM RUNS ON DUNKIN!" They then destroy every data center in Louisiana.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
