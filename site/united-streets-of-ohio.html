    The United Streets of Ohio

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Worsening.

    The United Streets of Ohio
    Notable sitesThe Corner Store
    Notable residentsKanye West (in business)
    StatusWorsening
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Locations

    The United Streets of Ohio is introduced in one sentence: "However the situation in the the united streets of Ohio was worsening". The canon never says what the situation is or how it is getting worse. The scene that follows is Kanye West cheerfully launching Ye the Gas at a local corner store, which does not obviously make anything worse.

    Ohio is a long-running internet meme for strangeness ("only in Ohio"). The name may parody the United States, with "States" replaced by "Streets". It should not be confused with the United State of America.
