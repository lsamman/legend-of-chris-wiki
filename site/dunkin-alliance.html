    The Dunkin Alliance

    Events From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Four people, one table, one common enemy who likes Wordle. Disbanded, forever.

    The Dunkin Alliance is the team Vinny B. James assembles at the Dunkin after being thrown into Wyoming. It is made of Will Vella, Aesop Rock and Del.

    Contents
    1. Recruiting
    2. The campaign
    3. The end
    4. On the spin-off wiki

    Recruiting

    Vinny offers Will help finding the secret of Vellatosis if Will helps defeat Claude, King of AI ("Sounds baller"). Then he and Will sit at the rappers' table, and Vinny asks them for help against a super-AI "trying to take over the world with Data-centers and Robots". They are unmoved until he says, "He likes Wordle." Del: "We're in."

    The campaign

    The Alliance loses the Mystery Machine (The Mystery Machine Crash), learns to teleport, lands in Texas (The Teleport to Texas) and trains with the UHA for three months (The Freedom Road Trip). It then loses the Battle of Louisiana after six hours, and wins anyway through Vellatosis.

    The end

    "The Dunkin Alliance disbands, forever. They all call an Uber because they have no way to get back home." Will has already left with the Dunkin Cult; Aesop and Del return to Wyoming; Vinny returns to the register (Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World).

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
