    Popeyes

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Formerly a Walmart. Formerly not a nuke site.

    Popeyes
    LocationNadir side of Constantinople
    FormerlyAn abandoned Walmart
    Notable customerLightning McQueen
    Payment methodGlock 19
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Locations

    Popeyes is a fried chicken restaurant in Constantinople that used to be the second of the abandoned Walmarts. Lightning McQueen "ventured north to the other target and discovered it was actually now a Popeyes chicken location".

    The order

    McQueen used the drive-through and placed the order quoted above. Told to "Please pull up", he answered "Aight bet", then "pulled out his Glock 19 and demanded his order from the cashier at the register". The cashier replied "Alright here's your order sir", and McQueen said "Thank you". The exchange stayed polite.

    McQueen "dropped off his nuke after he ate his order and left". He was so unhurried that by the time he radioed Mr. Bean, Bean had also "just finished lunch".

    Trivia

    • "Extra DIEP" is never explained. It is generally assumed to be a typo, though for what is disputed.
    • Popeyes does not have a menu item numbered 45.
