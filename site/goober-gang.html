    The Goober Gang is a three-member strike team made up of Lightning McQueen, Agent 007 and Mr. Bean. It is the only faction in the canon whose full roster is printed in the text as a formal list, set off by dashed lines like a roll of honor:

    The Goober Gang -McQueen -007 -Mr.Bean
    Contents
    1. History
    2. Other uses of "goober"
    3. Fan theories

    History

    The gang formed in England when McQueen met his Parliament contacts at the Smoke and Vape Shop. Its single known mission was to take Constantinople away from the Barack Empire and restore Bernie Sanders. After planting four nukes and recovering a torsoless 007, the gang escaped through a wormhole "just north of Dababy's last known location". The narrator says this was "the last anyone heard from the goober gang for now", and the gang has not reappeared since.

    Other uses of "goober"

    The word Goober appears twice more in the same chapter:

    • The DOD fled the blue dust "in fear of being a goober".
    • After Hank Hill escaped in the crack van, "the goobers where gone", which means Hank, Jesse and White were also goobers, at least briefly.

    Scholars disagree on whether these are separate goobers or whether Goober is a state anyone can enter.

    Fan theories

    • Theory: "for now" means the gang will return at the Court for the final battle against Chris. Nothing in the text confirms this.
