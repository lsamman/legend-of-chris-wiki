    The Burger King (Boston)

    Locations From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Where the freezer is not just a freezer.

    The Burger King (Boston)
    TypeRestaurant
    CityBoston
    StaffVinny B. James, Tyrone, their British boss
    Notable featureA The Freezer with a door into the The Backrooms
    Best sellerDouble Whopper with Cheese
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 1: Beginnings, Chapter 3: The Age of Claude, Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World
    Locations

    The Burger King in Boston is where Vinny B. James and Tyrone work. It is also, by a freezer wall, an entrance to the The Backrooms.

    Contents
    1. On July 4th, 1995
    2. After the Age of Claude
    3. On the spin-off wiki

    On July 4th, 1995

    The shift covers a customer who wants a Wendy's sandwich, a shortage of Double Whoppers with Cheese, and a trip into the The Freezer. Vinny and Tyrone never come back to finish it (Independence Day, 1995, FRIEND's Abduction).

    After the Age of Claude

    In Chapter 3: The Age of Claude Vinny describes it to his team as "a Burger King that has an entrance to the Backrooms Dimension in the freezer". In Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World, on November 2nd, 1995, he is back at its cash register. The chapter is titled "The Burger King Dark World".

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
