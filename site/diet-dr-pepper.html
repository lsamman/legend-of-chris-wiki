    Diet Dr Pepper

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Dayum, this is ice cold.

    Diet Dr Pepper
    TypeSoft drink
    Served byThe Inav (hovering wall coaster)
    DrinkerKanye West
    TemperatureIce cold
    First appearanceThe Flashback Chapter
    Items & Technology

    Diet Dr Pepper is served on board the Inav. In response to "Hey Siri get me some diet dr.pepper", "a hovering coaster was deployed from the wall servicing Kanye a ice cold diet dr.pepper." Kanye reviewed it favourably. Steve Jobs replied: "Apple only does things right kanye."

    Notes

    • This is the only time the Inav's beverage system is used, and it is the canon's main evidence for Steve's claim that the Inav "just works".
    • Diet Dr Pepper joins Mountain Dew, Red Bull, G Fuel and Prime in the canon's very caffeinated drinks list.
