    The Soviet Union of North Korea

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Truth Social boomtown.

    The Soviet Union of North Korea
    Popular platformTruth Social
    Merging withThe Democratic Republic of the Chinese
    Notable residentsGeorge Constanta; briefly Lightning McQueen (possibly)
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Locations

    The Soviet Union of North Korea is a country where, during the War for Byzantine Mexico, “Truth social was at the time booming,” and which “was merging with the democratic Republic of the Chinese.”

    Possible later appearance

    In Chapter Triangle, the narration says “Meanwhile in the Soviet Union, lighting McQueen was getting sick of George Constanta,” after which McQueen flies to England. McQueen had moved in with Constanta after the attack on Moscow, which the book places in Italy. Editors disagree on whether “the Soviet Union” refers to this country, to a separate Soviet Union, or to Moscow.
