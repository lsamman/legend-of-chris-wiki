    The Multiverse

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Created for a movie remake. Things got out of hand.

    The Multiverse
    CreatorElon Mush
    PurposeRecreating Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse
    Known universesCringe, America, Minor Miner, Protestant, Universe Prime
    Political bodyUnited Multiverses
    NavigatorThe Inav
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Locations

    The Multiverse was created by Elon Mush “so he could recreate “Spider-man Across the spider verse”.” It has had a difficult history since then.

    History

    Mush's first by-product was the Cringe Universe, which led to the Dabbing of the Haters and the Great Oppression. The America Universe and Elon Musk then took over “over 2 universes,” which caused a civil war that “the bad ones won.” Mark Zuckerberg then sent every universe except his favourite to the Shadow Realm.

    Later references

    • When Kanye and ChatGPT drop their mixtape, “across all multiverses, a wormhole opened at once.” (Plural multiverses, so there may be more than one.)
    • The iPhone 3G Keynote is held in “Cupertino, California, United Multiverses.”
    • Steve Jobs says the Inav was contracted by the Department of Awesomeness as a “universal navigator and transporter for the newly found multiverse.” The word “newly” suggests that either the Multiverse was rediscovered after the Snap, or Apple was very late.
