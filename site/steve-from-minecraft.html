    Characters

    Steve from Minecraft is a character in The Legend of Chris and, alongside his partner Quagmire, one of the three known Keepers of the MySpace Relics. He is the default player character of the real-world video game Minecraft, and in the canon he appears to have retired from block-placing in favor of geopolitics.

    Biography

    In Chapter 2, Steve and Quagmire travel to the famed city of the Projects to meet Osama Bin Bom'in and "corrode-a-nate" (sic) an attack on "the italians of russia". The pair's initial search is described as "good", as they quickly find a guide, MF Vroom. Once the party reaches I-38 West, however, Osama exiles their guide to the COD lobbies, and Steve does not appear to object.

    Steve and Quagmire then sit down with Osama to discuss "which Toyota Corolla should be responsible for the intital bombing run" (see The Toyota Corolla Question). The target is the Italian city of Moscow, chosen because it hosted the September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing contest, which caused DQ to start beef on MySpace with McDonald's. As relic keepers, the trio "wasnt cool with this C tier beef" and resolved to "let DQ win already".

    The meeting ends with Lightning McQueen chosen for the run and Tyler from Uber booked as the getaway driver. Steve is not seen again after this meeting.

    Trivia

    • Steve is never given a last name. The wiki has chosen not to read anything into this, unlike in the case of Plankton.
    • His motive is entirely about restaurant rivalry on MySpace, which makes him the only character in the canon to go to war over a social media comment section.
