    The 360 Chambers

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · All the screams, none of the rooms.

    The 360 Chambers
    TypeCorridor complex
    LocationCall of Duty HQ
    AtmosphereSilent, crisp, "Erie"
    PlumbingTranslucent pipes of Mountain Dew
    Notable occupantMF Vroom (lost for days)
    ExitA door smothered in Dorito dust and Mountain Dew oil
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Locations

    The 360 Chambers are a network of corridors deep in Call of Duty HQ, past the battlefield. When the battle starts, someone shouts "This is our chance, quick to the 360 chambers!" and RFK and Plankton "ran down the adjacent hallway to meet their worst nightmare..". This is where they find MF Vroom.

    Contents
    1. Description
    2. Events
    3. Trivia

    Description

    Entering from the battlefield, "The red mist of blood slowly gave way to dead air. The air was crisp and clean, the chambers were silent and devoid of noise." The floor is "grated panels" and the walls are "filled with translucent pipes filled with a green liquid". Ioannus warns: "These pipes are filled with Mountain Dew. An organic substance that has the power to make you super human, or super dead."

    Deeper in, "the screams and slurs of xbox live lobbies" come "from every hallway", even though no rooms can be seen.

    Events

    A shadow runs across the hallways. Ioannus draws his Lightsaber, RFK draws his hantavirus vaccine, and Plankton draws his metal folding chair. The shadow turns out to be MF Vroom, "a fuckig car wearing a robe", who "escaped the COD lobbies but for days now ive been roaming these hallways trying to find a way out!"

    An "ethereal voice" says "Bruh just leave", and a door appears, "smothered in Dorito dust and mountain dew oil". Plankton: "Daum… Obama care did not reach this door". Outside it is "freaking stormy", "absolutely raining pepsi and dew".

    Trivia

    • The name refers to the Xbox 360.
    • Who the ethereal voice belongs to is never explained.
    • The Chambers are the only place in the canon that is silent.
