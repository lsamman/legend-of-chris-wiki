    Characters

    Will Vella is the colorful figure Vinny B. James meets in the Dunkin in Wyoming. Wearing a United Kingdom flag as a cape, Will is furious that there are no England-style donuts and announces a plan to colonize every coffee shop.

    At the Dunkin

    Will demands England-style donuts, and the Starwalker manager answers, "This is America." Will says, "You're getting colonized." Will vows that when Dunkin is taken over, every coffee shop will be colonized, and shouts "DO NOT QUESTION THE BAGEL MAN!!!!!" at the manager's question ("But aren't you no-"). Will's goal is to "create my Dunkin Cult" through Vellatosis, with an "army of Vella's".

    The deal

    Vinny offers to help find the secret of Vellatosis, which he calls "a game theory", if Will helps defeat Claude, King of AI. Will agrees: "Sounds baller." Will then sits at the table with Aesop Rock and Del (The Dunkin Alliance).
