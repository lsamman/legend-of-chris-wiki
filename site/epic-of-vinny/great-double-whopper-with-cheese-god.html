    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese God

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Characters · No one has found it. Everyone is looking.

    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese God
    GrantsThe power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese
    StatusNever found, in any galaxy
    Believed byEveryone in the Kanye System
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Characters

    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese god is the deity said to grant the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese. "No one in any galaxy" has found it.

    What is known

    Chapter 1: Beginnings says whoever is granted the Great Double Whopper with Cheese by the god will have its power. Tyrone calls the whole thing "just a myth! No one has ever seen" either the item or the god. Vinny B. James later corrects Captain Gaster: "Only The Great Double Whopper with Cheese god has The Great Double Whopper with Cheese, you idiot!"
