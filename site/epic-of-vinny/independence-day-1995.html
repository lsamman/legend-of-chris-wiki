    Independence Day, 1995

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · The day it all starts. The boss said no.

    Independence Day, 1995
    DateJuly 4th, 1995
    PlaceBoston, Garth
    Main figureVinny B. James
    Everything else that dayThe Forklift Incident, The Cat in the Box, FRIEND's Abduction, and more
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Events

    Independence Day, 1995 is the date the story's main timeline begins. The text says "the year is 1995, July 4th", and that Vinny B. James's British boss refuses to give him the day off.

    The day

    Vinny's shift starts normally and then passes through the The Freezer into the The Backrooms. The rest of the story so far, through Chapter 3: The Age of Claude, appears to take place in the hours and days after: the ship, the drowning, the Shadow Realm, and Wyoming. The text only dates the start.
