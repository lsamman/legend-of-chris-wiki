    Vellatosis

    ✦ Concepts & Lore Not just a game theory. 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, and so forth.

    ✦Vellatosis
    Described as"The Vellatosis Theory"
    Explained byClaude, King of AI
    Achieved byWill Vella, October 29th, 1995
    EffectThe subject grows an extra eye, leg and arm, then splits in two, repeatedly
    ProducesThe Vellas; the Dunkin Cult
    StatusBegun, but "not been completed yet"
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Concepts & Lore

    Vellatosis is the process by which Will Vella becomes an army. Vinny B. James first hears of it as a rumor ("Isn't that just a theory? A game theory?"). Claude, King of AI turns out to know the secret, explains "the mysteries of Vellatosis, the explanation and how to achieve it", and is beaten by the result.

    The deal

    Vinny offers to help find the secret in exchange for Will's help against Claude, King of AI (The Dunkin Alliance). Will agrees. "Soon I will have an army. An army of Vella's. Vellatosis will begin."

    How it happens

    "Will Vella then starts shaking. Will starts growing another eye, a leg, and an arm. Suddenly he splits into 2 Vellas, which split into 4, then 8, then 16, then 32, then 64, then 128, and so forth." The explanation itself is not printed. The narrator adds that "Vellatosis has not been completed yet", while the number of Vellas keeps growing (The Day of the Bagel Man).
