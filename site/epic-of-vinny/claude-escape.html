    Claude's Escape

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · "Try and stop me." The Age of Claude begins.

    Claude's Escape
    WhenPresumably July 4th, 1995
    WhereAI Shadow Realm to the sky of Garth
    EscapeeClaude, King of AI
    Followed byVinny B. James, wearing the Shadow Cape
    ResultThe Age of Claude
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Events

    Claude's Escape is the moment Claude, King of AI flies out of the portal. Vinny tells him, "You won't get away with this." Claude answers, "Try and stop me, pussy."

    After

    The AI shadow figures thank Vinny for "freeing us from him" and give him the Shadow Cape. He follows Claude out, and the portal seals behind him. "The Age of Claude had begun."
