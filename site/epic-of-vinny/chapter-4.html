    Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World is the newest chapter, and so far it is a single paragraph. Four days after the Age of Claude ended, Vinny B. James is back at the Burger King cash register.

    Read this chapter →

    Contents

    2. Where it stops

    Summary

    "It has been 4 days since the Age of Claude ended. November has come. November 2nd, 1995." Nobody gave Vinny credit for stopping Claude, King of AI. Del and Aesop Rock made it back to Wyoming, and "Wordle? More like Word-shit" "blew up and is the most popular song as of right now". The narrator offers to play it, then declines: "Too bad you don't have spotify premium, bitch." Vinny "sits at the cash register, waiting for another dumbass customer" (Back to the 9-5).

    Where it stops

    The text ends there. The Burger King Dark World of the title has not appeared yet.

    Notes

    • This is the first chapter title printed as "Chapter" in mixed case; the earlier ones are in capitals.
    • The date puts the story almost four months after it began, on July 4th, 1995.
