    Characters

    Aesop Rock is a rapper from the real world who appears in Chapter 3: The Age of Claude at a Dunkin in Wyoming, writing a diss track with Del. He is one of the allies Vinny B. James recruits against Claude, King of AI.

    Appearances

    Aesop tells Vinny and Will that "these weird birds ain't being very aware of our locked in galaxy." Vinny asks, "What the fuck does that mean, Ian Bavitz?" and Del translates it as "These strangers aren't aware of their personal space." Told about Claude's plan for data centers and robots, Aesop says, "Technology. Now that is a powerful cat." After hearing that Claude likes Wordle, he and Del join: "We're in."

    The rest of Chapter 3

    Aesop speaks only in Aesopian, which Del and John Freedom can follow. He uses both of his teleports: one to take the team to Texas instead of Boston (The Teleport to Texas), and the last to take them to Claude, King of AI. He fights in the Battle of Louisiana. By Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World he is back in Wyoming with a hit song.

    Trivia

    • Aesop Rock is a real person. The character here is a fictional cameo in a fan story.
