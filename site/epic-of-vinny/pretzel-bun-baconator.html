    Pretzel-Bun Baconator

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Items · That's Wendy's thing.

    Pretzel-Bun Baconator
    TypeWendy's sandwich (real)
    Ordered byThe Random Customer
    Ordered atBurger King, Boston
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Items

    The Pretzel-Bun Baconator is the Wendy's sandwich that a customer tries to order at the wrong restaurant. Vinny B. James says, "Sir, this is a Burger King." "So?" "That's Wendy's thing."

    Outcome

    The customer asks Vinny to pick something. Vinny suggests a Double Whopper with Cheese. The customer leaves, saying that "you guys don't have what I want".
