    Garret the Forklift Guy

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Characters · On break. No-clipped.

    Garret the Forklift Guy
    OccupationForklift operator, The Backrooms storage room
    StatusNo-clipped to somewhere unknown
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Characters

    Garret is the forklift guy in the The Backrooms storage room behind the The Freezer. When Vinny B. James and Tyrone ask for help moving Double Whoppers with Cheese, he says he is on break, then lets them use the The Forklift. Vinny immediately hits him with it.

    The forklift incident

    After Vinny spins out, Garret "goes flying into a wall and no-clips to somewhere" (see No-clipping and The Forklift Incident). Vinny's reaction is, "Aw shit. I forgot I'm not forklift certified." Nothing more is said about where Garret ended up.
