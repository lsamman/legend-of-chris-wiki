    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    Normal_Parrot is Captain Gaster's parrot and a member of the S.S. Vessel crew. The name is written with an underscore, like a username.

    Appearances

    Normal_Parrot is only named in the crew introduction in Chapter 2: The Weird Route. It has no lines and takes no part in the action so far.
