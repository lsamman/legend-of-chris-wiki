    Locations

    Boston is Vinny B. James's home city. The narrator announces it with thirteen exclamation marks. Vinny lives there and works at a Burger King.

    In the story

    The story opens in Boston on July 4th, 1995 (Independence Day, 1995). Independence Day is a pointed day to be told he cannot take off: his British boss will not let him have a vacation day.
