    Vinny's Boss

    ☻ Characters British. Will not give anyone the Fourth of July off.

    ☻Vinny's Boss
    WorkplaceBurger King, Boston
    NationalityBritish
    Known as"The big brit boss" (by Tyrone)
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Characters

    Vinny's boss is the British manager of the Burger King where Vinny B. James works. The boss will not let Vinny take a vacation day for Independence Day, which is a significant decision on July 4th, 1995.

    Resignation by message

    After the Roaring Knight sends Vinny overboard, Tyrone shouts, "Tell the big brit boss that I quit, Vinny! I'm with Gaster's Crew now!" The boss is never seen on the page.
