    Concepts & Lore

    The Very Violent and Vivid Prophecy (the Final Prophecy) is the line that opens Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy. The text promises it "will come to pass" because Vinny B. James "shall not be in Vain". Its Vs are why the group around him is called The VIGOROUS V.

    Meaning

    The prophecy is not explained. It is paired with the fight against TON 618, which is the subject of the prologue (The Final Battle with TON 618). It is separate from The Great Double Whopper with Cheese War, the other prophecy in the text.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The Prophecy: The Legend Of Chris has its own Prophecy: a phone with six apps and a Verizon plan, filled with DETERMINATION.
