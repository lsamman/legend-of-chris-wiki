    Little Saint James Island

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · A real island with a small building on it. The crew left quickly.

    Little Saint James Island
    TypeIsland
    SeaThe Little Saint James Sea
    Visited byNobody (the crew turn away)
    Real-world noteThe name is a real island's; it is not described in detail
    First sightingChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Locations

    Little Saint James Island is sighted by the S.S. Vessel in Chapter 2: The Weird Route. The crew see "a small building" through Captain Gaster's spyglass and speed away "after a quick escape from being extremely political".

    The theory

    Furious, Vinny B. James asks what Gaster was thinking. Gaster answers that the island's secret might have been that it held The Great Double Whopper with Cheese. Vinny says that only the god has it (The Little Saint James Detour). Later, when Vinny is knocked overboard, the island is one of his two choices: swim for it or drown. He chooses to drown.
