    The 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique

    ✦ Concepts & Lore Peace sign. Think of a place. Twice only.

    ✦The 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique
    HowPut up a peace sign and think of where you want to go
    LimitTwice per person, because Scott the Woz put a limit on it
    Discovered throughA secret bug in Wii Play
    Used byScott the Woz (once), Aesop Rock (twice)
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Concepts & Lore

    The 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique lets a person teleport, with passengers, by making a peace sign and thinking of a destination. Each person can use it twice. The Dunkin Alliance learns it from a YouTube tutorial by Scott the Woz.

    Known uses

    • Scott the Woz: from his chair to the chair next to him, as a demonstration.
    • Aesop Rock, first use: the whole Alliance to Austin, Texas instead of Boston (The Teleport to Texas).
    • Aesop Rock, last use: the whole Alliance to Claude, King of AI in Louisiana.

    Unused teleports

    Vinny B. James, Will Vella and Del all watched the tutorial (Del twice) and have not used the technique on the page. After the battle, everyone calls an Uber.

    • Chapter 3: The Age of Claude
