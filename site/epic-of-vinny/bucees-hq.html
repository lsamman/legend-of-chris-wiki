    Buc-ee's HQ

    ⌖ Locations The most American place on Garth.

    ⌖Buc-ee's HQ
    InAustin, Texas
    Home ofThe United Homies of America
    Contains"The most American things to ever see"; a flight of stairs; the American Garage
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    Buc-ee's HQ is the headquarters of The United Homies of America. Will Vella recognizes it on sight: "Holy shit, it's Buc-ee's HQ!! The most American place on Garth!!!"

    Inside

    "Inside are the most American things to ever see. Vinny sheds a tear from how beautiful it is. Will Vella sheds a tear because Will is British." The UHA make their entrance down a flight of stairs, and the building's American Garage holds the UHA Ford-F150.
