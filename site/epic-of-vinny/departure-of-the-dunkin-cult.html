    The Departure of the Dunkin Cult

    ✸ Events They will return again.

    ✸The Departure of the Dunkin Cult
    WhenOctober 29th, 1995
    ShipThe Dunkin Cult Cruiser
    DestinationThe Dunder Mifflin Galaxy
    GoalFind the MacBook Charger; "this conquest of Dunkin"
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The departure of the Dunkin Cult follows the destruction of the data centers. The Bagel Man addresses the Vellas from the middle of the Dunkin Cult Cruiser, and they "fly off into space, disappearing into the Universe. They will return again."

    The speech

    "VELLAS OF THE DUNKIN CULT! WE MUST FIND OUR NEMESIS, THE MACBOOK CHARGER! IN THE DUNDER MIFFLIN GALAXY, THE UNITED HOMIES OF AMERICA ARE WAITING FOR US! WE WILL NEED THEIR HELP IN THIS CONQUEST OF DUNKIN!"
