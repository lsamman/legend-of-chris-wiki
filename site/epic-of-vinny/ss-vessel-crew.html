    Factions

    The S.S. Vessel crew are the people (and a parrot) on Captain Gaster's ship. They are on a mission to find The Great Double Whopper with Cheese, and Gaster intends to use its power to rule the Kanye System.

    Roster

    The narrator lists the crew in Chapter 2: The Weird Route: FRIEND (the foodbringer), ERAM (the sail), Normal_Parrot (Gaster's parrot) and The Roaring Knight (professional door dasher). Vinny B. James and Tyrone sign on after waking aboard.

    Fate

    Vinny leaves the crew when the Roaring Knight knocks him overboard. Tyrone stays aboard, having asked Vinny to pass on his resignation to their boss.
