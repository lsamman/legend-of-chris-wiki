    ERAM

    ☻ Characters The sail.

    ☻ERAM
    Role"The sail", S.S. Vessel crew
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    ERAM is listed by Captain Gaster as part of his crew, with the description "(The sail)". ERAM is not given any lines.

    Crew introduction

    When Vinny B. James and Tyrone wake on the S.S. Vessel, Gaster points at the crew and the narrator lists them: FRIEND (the foodbringer), ERAM (the sail), Normal_Parrot (Gaster's parrot) and The Roaring Knight (professional door dasher). The text does not say whether the sail is a person, a part of the ship, or both.
