    The Roaring Knight's Strike

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · Insult the band, get the BLACK KNIFE. Drown. Not quite.

    The Roaring Knight's Strike
    WhenPresumably July 4th, 1995
    WhereThe S.S. Vessel, then the sea
    AttackerThe Roaring Knight
    VictimVinny B. James
    WeaponThe BLACK KNIFE
    Time to unconsciousness1 minute 24 seconds
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Events

    The Roaring Knight's Strike is when Roary hits Vinny B. James with the BLACK KNIFE and he "falls off the S.S. Vessel and into the ocean". Vinny can swim to the island or drown. "Guess I'll die," he says, and dives.

    Drowning

    "He starts losing consciousness after a minute and twenty-four seconds. Is it so joever for Vinny?" The narrator immediately answers: "Nah, we're so BARACK." Shadows from the sea carry him to the AI Shadow Realm.

    Before he falls, Tyrone shouts, "Tell the big brit boss that I quit, Vinny!"
