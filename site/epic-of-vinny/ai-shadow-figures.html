    The AI Shadow Figures

    ⚑ Factions The population of the Shadow Realm. Cheerers. Gift-givers.

    ⚑The AI Shadow Figures

    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Factions

    The AI Shadow Figures are the shadowy inhabitants of the AI Shadow Realm. They cheer on the Smash match, and after Claude, King of AI leaves, they thank Vinny B. James for "freeing us from him" and give him a Shadow Cape.

    Behavior

    Some are rude (the first one Vinny meets); some are polite and deceived (the butler). As a group they gather around the Smash match, then give Vinny "strength and flight" as a reward.
