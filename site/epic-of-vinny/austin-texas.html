    ⌖Austin, Texas
    OnGarth, United States of America

    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    Austin, Texas is where the Dunkin Alliance lands when Aesop Rock cuts off Vinny B. James's plan to go to Boston and teleports everyone somewhere else (The Teleport to Texas).

    Arrival

    Vinny: "Aesop, did you seriously use one of your 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique to bring us to Texas?!" Aesop: "Firewall in the attic, boom shoes on a fresh night." In front of them stands Buc-ee's HQ.
