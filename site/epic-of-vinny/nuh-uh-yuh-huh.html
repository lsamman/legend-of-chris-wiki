    "Nuh uh" / "Yuh huh"

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Five rounds. No winner. Moves on.

    "Nuh uh" / "Yuh huh"
    BetweenVinny B. James and Tyrone
    Rounds5 ("Nuh uh" and "Yuh huh", alternating)
    Resolved byTyrone: "I think there might be more in storage."
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Concepts & Lore

    "Nuh uh" / "Yuh huh" is the argument Vinny and Tyrone have in Chapter 1: Beginnings about whether the restaurant has run out of Double Whoppers with Cheese. It ends when Tyrone suggests the storage room.

    The exchange

    "Vinny we ran out of Double Whoppers with Cheese." "Nuh uh." "Yuh huh." The two repeat the pair five times, and then Tyrone says, "I think there might be more in storage." Vinny answers, "Aight, bet." ("Aight, bet")

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate: The Legend Of Chris has a 303-utterance Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate between Bon Jovi and RFK.
