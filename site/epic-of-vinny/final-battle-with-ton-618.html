    The Final Battle with TON 618 is the fight the prologue begins and Chapter 1 abandons. Vinny B. James "stares at the TON 618, emitting a blue glow" and says, "Bring it on, you fucking bastard." "The battle begins."

    What is known

    Vinny has a vibrant sword, titanium armor, a cape of shadows, the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese, and DETERMINATION. Behind him is The VIGOROUS V. The outcome is not given.
