    The AI Shadow Figure
    TypeAI shadow figure
    LocationAI Shadow Realm
    Greeting"Yo pause."
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    The AI Shadow Figure is the first resident of the AI Shadow Realm that Vinny B. James meets after waking up soaking wet. It is hostile and unhelpful, and vanishes after a short, rude exchange.

    The meeting

    "Yo pause," the figure says. Vinny asks what it is doing there. It replies that it is an AI shadow figure, "you fucking dumbass", and asks why Vinny thinks it is there. After Vinny says it does not have to be rude, the figure says something worse and disappears.

    Other shadow figures

    The realm has many more figures; see The AI Shadow Figures for the group and The AI Shadow Butler for the one in Claude's castle.
