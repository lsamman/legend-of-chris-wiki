    UNC Standard Time

    ✦ Concepts & Lore It's 2:24am.

    ✦UNC Standard Time
    Reported byWill Vella
    Reading2:24am, July 5th, 1995
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Concepts & Lore

    UNC standard time is the time zone Will Vella uses when Vinny B. James asks for the time after the crash: "It's 2:24am UNC standard time."

    In the story

    It is the only clock reading in the story so far, apart from Vinny's watch from 5 Below.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • Unc Standard Time: The Legend Of Chris also keeps its clocks in Unc Standard Time.
