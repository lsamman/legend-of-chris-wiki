    The Clash Over the USA

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · Stoppable force. Immovable object. Wyoming.

    The Clash Over the USA
    WhenPresumably July 4th–5th, 1995
    WhereThe sky over the United States, then Wyoming
    FightersVinny B. James and Claude, King of AI
    OutcomeVinny is thrown into a mountain in Wyoming
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The Clash Over the USA is the first round of Vinny B. James against Claude, King of AI on Garth. Vinny flies into Claude "at full speed", becoming "the stoppable force against an immovable object". Claude chokes him and throws him into Wyoming.

    Before and after

    Claude has just flown into the United States, remarking that it "needs more data centers". Vinny crashes into a mountain and lies in pain for "about 6 hours and 8 minutes", until the sun goes down. He then goes in search of Dunkin (The Dunkin Alliance).
