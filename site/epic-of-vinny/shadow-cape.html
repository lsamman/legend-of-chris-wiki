    The Shadow Cape

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Items · Strength and flight. Sewn from literal shadows.

    The Shadow Cape
    Given byThe AI shadow figures
    OwnerVinny B. James
    PowersStrength and flight
    Material (prologue)"Sewn together with literal shadows"
    First appearanceChapter ?: The Final Prophecy
    Items

    The Shadow Cape is the cape the AI shadow figures give Vinny B. James in Chapter 2: The Weird Route: "It will grant you strength and flight." Vinny's reply is "Lmao thanks."

    Use

    Vinny uses it to fly out of the portal and later to fly into Claude, King of AI over Garth (The Clash Over the USA) and out of the Wyoming mountain. In Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy, he wears a cape "sewn together with literal shadows". The two are presumably the same cape, but the text does not say so.
