    The Random Customer

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Characters · A suspicious Wendy's enjoyer in a Burger King.

    Characters

    The Random Customer is the first person Vinny B. James serves in the story. They "suspiciously" like Wendy's, and ask for a Pretzel-Bun Baconator, a Wendy's sandwich, at a Burger King.

    The order

    The customer starts a sentence with "can I get" and a very long hum of the letter h before the sandwich name. Vinny points out that the place is a Burger King and that the Baconator is "Wendy's thing." The customer asks Vinny to choose, hears the Double Whopper with Cheese, and says, "No, I think I'll just leave. You guys don't have what I want."
