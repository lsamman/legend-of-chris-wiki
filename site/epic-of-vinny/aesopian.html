    Aesopian

    ✦ Concepts & Lore The language Aesop Rock speaks.

    ✦Aesopian
    Spoken byAesop Rock, John Freedom
    Translated byDel
    Understood by normal mindsNo
    First namedChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Concepts & Lore

    Aesopian "is the language Aesop Rock speaks. To the normal mind, people don't really understand what the fuck he's talking about."

    Phrasebook

    • "These weird birds ain't being very aware of our locked in galaxy." Del's translation: "These strangers aren't aware of their personal space."
    • "Technology. Now that is a powerful cat." Said of Claude, King of AI.
    • "No eyes no tapes no Animals." Said after the crash. Untranslated.
    • "We don't need those thingamagigs when we have the understoodation of transportation. Peace."
    • "Firewall in the attic, boom shoes on a fresh night." Said in Texas. Untranslated.
    • "Everyday is tomorrow. This is something I'm willing to die for."

    Second speaker

    John Freedom understands Aesop without help. "Wait, you speak Aesopian?" "Yes, Freedom is the only thing you need."
