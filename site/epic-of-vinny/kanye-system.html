    The Kanye System

    ⌖ Locations Home of Garth, Burger King, and one very large light.

    The Kanye System is the system containing Garth, and the setting of The Epic of Vinny. It is ruled by TON 618, which conquered it after taking the shape of a giant man. In it, "someone orders a Double Whopper with Cheese" 98.578462849224% of the time.

    Burger King

    Chapter 1: Beginnings says that everyone in the Kanye System loves Burger King, because of the prophecy of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese War. Double Whoppers with Cheese dominate the menu. Captain Gaster hopes to rule the whole system with the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese.

    The prologue

    The final fight of Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy takes place in the Kanye System "next to the TON 618" (The Final Battle with TON 618).

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The Kanye System: In The Legend Of Chris the system sits inside the Reformed Travis Scott Sector. The Department of Defense is sent there to contain Hank Hill.
