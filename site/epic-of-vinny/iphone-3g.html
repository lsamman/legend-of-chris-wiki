    The iPhone 3G

    ◆ Items Will Vella's phone. Plays one YouTube tutorial, twice.

    ◆The iPhone 3G
    OwnerWill Vella
    Used forA YouTube tutorial by Scott the Woz
    Year in the story1995
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Items

    The iPhone 3G (written "Iphone 3G") is the phone Will Vella pulls out after the Mystery Machine blows up: "I gotchu Vinny." Will uses it to play the tutorial on the 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique.

    In the story

    Will plays the video, puts the phone away and says "Yeah." Del was not paying attention, so Will pulls it out and plays the video again.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • iPhone 3G: The iPhone 3G is the sacred phone of The Legend Of Chris, the one the Prophecy is about.
