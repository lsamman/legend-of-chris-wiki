    Louisiana

    ⌖ Locations Louisiana is a Data Center.

    ⌖Louisiana
    OnGarth, United States of America
    Briefly owned byFrance (The Reversal of the Louisiana Purchase)
    Site ofThe Great Data Center; the Battle of Louisiana
    StatusBought back for America by Claude, King of AI
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    Louisiana is where Claude, King of AI builds the Great Data Center, and where the Age of Claude ends. When Claude lifts Vinny B. James through the ceiling to look around, "the only thing he sees is data centers. All of Louisiana is Data Centers. Louisiana is a Data Center."

    The purchase, reversed

    With a snap of his fingers Claude reverses the Louisiana Purchase: "France now owns all the land America bought from France."

    After the battle

    The Vellas destroy every data center. The defeated Claude offers, "Spare me, and I'll buy Louisiana in America's name." "frfr?" "yo frfr." "bet." Louisiana "then becomes the U.S. once again".
