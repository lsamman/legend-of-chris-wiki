    Factions

    The VIGOROUS V is the name the narrator gives to Vinny B. James and "his loyal friends", who "have stayed by his side for the entirety of his adventure". They stand behind him as he faces TON 618 in the prologue.

    Membership

    The narrator says, "No spoilers for those on the team though." The members are not named. The allies the story has introduced so far have all gone their own way: Tyrone joined Gaster's crew, the Dunkin Alliance "disbands, forever", and Will Vella left Garth with the Dunkin Cult. Who ends up standing behind Vinny is still open.

    Name

    "Vigorous" fits the story's habit of naming almost everything with a V (see The Very Violent and Vivid Prophecy).
