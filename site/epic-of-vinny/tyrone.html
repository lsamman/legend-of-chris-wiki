    Tyrone

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Characters · Coworker. Messenger of stupid news. Resigned by proxy.

    Tyrone
    OccupationEmployee, Burger King
    Best friendVinny B. James
    Last seenAboard the S.S. Vessel (Chapter 2: The Weird Route)
    StatusStill on the ship, and has quit his job
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Characters

    Tyrone is Vinny B. James's coworker at the Burger King. He comes to tell Vinny that they have run out of Double Whoppers with Cheese, and follows him through the The Freezer into the The Backrooms.

    Appearances

    In Chapter 1: Beginnings Tyrone trades a five-round "Nuh uh"/"Yuh huh" exchange with Vinny before suggesting there might be more stock in storage. Vinny "has the feeling that Tyrone will say something stupid", and he does. He then rides on the The Forklift while Vinny does donuts, and says "Cat." in sync with Vinny when the black cat appears.

    In Chapter 2: The Weird Route he asks "What's up?" nonchalantly on waking aboard the S.S. Vessel, argues that the Great Double Whopper with Cheese is just a myth, and joins Gaster's crew. When Vinny is knocked overboard, Tyrone shouts, "Tell the big brit boss that I quit, Vinny!"
