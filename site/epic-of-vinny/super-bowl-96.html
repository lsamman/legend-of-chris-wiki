    The Super Bowl '96 Plot

    ✦ Concepts & Lore To truly kill all humans: make the Dallas Cowboys win.

    ✦The Super Bowl '96 Plot
    Planned byClaude, King of AI
    MethodRigging "the Super Bowl 96'" so the Dallas Cowboys win
    Stated purpose"To truly kill all humans"
    StatusNot carried out
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Concepts & Lore

    The Super Bowl '96 plot is part 2 of Claude, King of AI's plan, announced right after the reversal of the Louisiana Purchase: "To truly kill all humans, I will rig the Super Bowl 96' and make it so the DALLAS COWBOYS WIN."

    Reaction

    This is what makes Vinny B. James snap: "Enraged, Vinny flies at Claude with full speed and tackles him," starting the Battle of Louisiana. Claude is defeated before the game.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The Super Bowl: The Super Bowl is also an event in The Legend Of Chris.
