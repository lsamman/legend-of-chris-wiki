    John Freedom

    ☻ Characters Leader of the United Homies of America. Running for president in 2040.

    ☻John Freedom
    RoleLeader, The United Homies of America
    Known asA U.S. based YouTuber and livestreamer with a strong patriotic theme
    BaseBuc-ee's HQ, Austin, Texas
    VehicleThe UHA Ford-F150
    LanguagesEnglish, Aesopian
    FutureWill run for president in the year 2040
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Characters

    John Freedom is the leader of The United Homies of America. The narrator calls him "the freedom America needs to help the Dunkin Alliance". He trains Vinny B. James's team in the ways of Freedom over three months on the road.

    Contents
    1. Entrance
    2. Speaking Aesopian
    3. The road trip

    Entrance

    He arrives to a shout of "BY THE SHEER POWER OF FREEDOM!!!!", a screeching bald eagle, gunshots and a waving American flag, walking down a flight of stairs with his homies. He asks why the visitors have come to "this AMERICAN home, freedom lovers".

    Speaking Aesopian

    When Aesop Rock says "Technology. Now that is a powerful cat. You can get a robot limb for your blown off limb", John understands at once: "Oh shit, Claude has returned?" He is the only other known speaker of Aesopian. His explanation: "Freedom is the only thing you need."

    The road trip

    John drives everyone down Route 66 in the Ford-F150 (The Freedom Road Trip). On October 28th he drops the Alliance in Louisiana, says "Your buddy John Freedom will always be here for America, folks", apologizes for Uncle Sam ("he's a little TOO American"), and tells the Bagel Man he will see them in the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy. Then he pushes a button and the truck floats straight up.
