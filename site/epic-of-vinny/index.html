    Featured article

    Vinny B. James

    Vinny B. James is the hero of The Epic of Vinny. He is a cashier at a Burger King in Boston. On the Fourth of July, 1995, he ends up in the The Backrooms, aboard the S.S. Vessel and in the AI Shadow Realm, where he lets Claude, King of AI out. He spends the next four months putting that right.

    Did you know…

    • Vinny B. James: An earlier version of the prologue spelled his name "Vinney" once. The updated text fixes it.
    • Vinny B. James: Vinny can fly from the end of Chapter 2 onward. The Shadow Cape grants "strength and flight": he flies out of the portal, into Claude, out of a mountain, and into Claude again.
    • Claude, King of AI: Claude is introduced as being married to nobody: "Hey, you aren't my wife. I don't have one."
    • Captain Wing Ding Gaster: His line of symbols is printed in the Wingdings typeface. One of its characters shows up as an empty box in the PDF itself.
    • Vinny B. James: He claims he lost to Claude because he was "using the crappy controller".

    This story is set on Garth, a planet in the Kanye System, in the same multiverse as The Legend Of Chris. Elements cross over: The Kanye System, TON 618 (the story says it is the same one), the Shadow Realm, the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy, the iPhone 3G, Kanye West and ChatGPT.
