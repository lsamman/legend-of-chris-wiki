    Featured article

    Vinny B. James

    Vinny B. James is the hero of The Epic of Vinny. He is a cashier at a Burger King in Boston who, over one very long Fourth of July, ends up in the The Backrooms, aboard the S.S. Vessel, in the AI Shadow Realm and, finally, in Wyoming.

    Did you know…

    • Chapter 2: The Weird Route: The story includes retro RPG-style text boxes with a green-faced speaker. One announces that The Roaring Knight is "Roary Oleary", which echoes the author's surname.
    • Vinny B. James: He claims he lost to Claude because he was "using the crappy controller".
    • Claude, King of AI: The doors of his castle are made entirely of AI.
    • Vinny B. James: Vinny says "Aight, bet" every time he agrees to something dangerous. See "Aight, bet".
    • Captain Wing Ding Gaster: His name comes from a character in the video game Undertale. In this story he is a loud pirate captain.
    • Chapter 1: Beginnings: A customer's order is stretched across four lines of the letter "h" before it reaches "Pretzel-Bun Baconator".
