    The BLACK KNIFE

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Items · Capital letters. Hits for the ship's honor.

    The BLACK KNIFE
    OwnerThe Roaring Knight
    Used onVinny B. James
    EffectKnocks him off the ship
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Items

    The BLACK KNIFE is the weapon Roary, The Roaring Knight, uses to hit Vinny B. James. The narrator always writes it in capitals.

    The strike

    After Vinny calls Taking Back Sunday stupid, Roary hits him with the BLACK KNIFE. Vinny "falls off the S.S. Vessel and into the ocean" (The Roaring Knight's Strike).

    Trivia

    • The name matches a weapon from the video game Deltarune.
