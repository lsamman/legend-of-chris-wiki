    The Little Saint James Detour

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · A quick escape from being extremely political.

    The Little Saint James Detour
    WhenPresumably July 4th, 1995
    WhereThe Little Saint James Sea
    InvolvedThe S.S. Vessel crew, Vinny B. James, Tyrone
    CauseGaster's theory that the island held the Great Double Whopper with Cheese
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Events

    The Little Saint James Detour is the ship's brief approach to an island. Gaster sights a small building through his Captain Gaster's Spyglass, and the S.S. Vessel immediately speeds away.

    The argument

    Vinny asks, "What were you thinking, you bastard?!" Gaster says the island's secret might have been that it had The Great Double Whopper with Cheese. Vinny says only the god has it, and that Gaster is "almost as stupid as Taking Back Sunday". That insult leads straight to The Roaring Knight's Strike.
