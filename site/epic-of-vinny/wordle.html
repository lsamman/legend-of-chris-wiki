    Wordle

    ◆ Items Claude likes it. Aesop and Del failed it. Everyone is in.

    ◆Wordle
    TypeWord game (real)
    Connected toClaude, King of AI (a fan), Aesop Rock and Del (failed it)
    Role in the storyThe reason the rappers join
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Items

    Wordle is the word game that Aesop Rock and Del failed "miserably" and wrote a diss track about. It is also the thing that Claude, King of AI likes, according to Vinny B. James, which is enough to recruit them.

    The pitch

    Vinny explains that Claude is a super-AI aiming to take over the world with data centers and robots. The rappers are unmoved until Vinny adds, "He likes Wordle." Del answers: "We're in." (The Dunkin Alliance)

    In Louisiana

    When the Dunkin Alliance teleports to Claude, King of AI, "he is also currently playing Wordle", which confirms what Vinny B. James told the rappers.
