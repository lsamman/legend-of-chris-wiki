    The Smash Match

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · Vinny (Greninja) vs. Claude (Mr. Game & Watch). Controller dispute.

    The Smash Match
    WhenPresumably July 4th, 1995
    WhereClaude's castle, AI Shadow Realm
    GameSuper Smash Bros. for the Wii U
    StakesClaude wins: Vinny frees him. Vinny wins: Claude leaves.
    WinnerClaude, King of AI (side smash in sudden death)
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Events

    The Smash Match is the match Claude, King of AI proposes to settle whether he is freed. Vinny B. James plays Greninja, Claude plays Mr. Game & Watch, and the AI shadow figures cheer. It goes to sudden death, where Claude side-smashes Vinny off the stage.

    Aftermath

    Vinny complains that "I was using the crappy controller!" Claude repeats "Copium" many times. Vinny honors the deal and asks how to open the portal (The Opening of the Portal).
