    The Burger King (Boston)

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · Where the freezer is not just a freezer.

    The Burger King (Boston)
    TypeRestaurant
    CityBoston
    StaffVinny B. James, Tyrone, their British boss
    Notable featureA The Freezer with a door into the The Backrooms
    Best sellerDouble Whopper with Cheese
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Locations

    The Burger King in Boston is where Vinny B. James and Tyrone work. It is also, by a freezer wall, an entrance to the The Backrooms.

    On July 4th, 1995

    The shift covers a customer who wants a Wendy's sandwich, a shortage of Double Whoppers with Cheese, and a trip into the The Freezer. Vinny and Tyrone never come back to finish it (Independence Day, 1995, FRIEND's Abduction).
