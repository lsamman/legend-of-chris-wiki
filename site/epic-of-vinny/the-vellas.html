    The Vellas

    ☻ Characters 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, and so forth.

    ☻The Vellas
    OriginVellatosis: Will Vella splitting
    NumberDoubling; still growing when last seen

    LeaderThe Bagel Man
    Battle cry"THE KANYE SYSTEM RUNS ON DUNKIN!"
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Characters

    The Vellas are the copies of Will Vella produced by Vellatosis. Will grows another eye, a leg and an arm, splits into 2 Vellas, "which split into 4, then 8, then 16, then 32, then 64, then 128, and so forth".

    Deeds

    The Vellas "jump Claude's ass and beat the shit out of him, outnumbering him", then destroy every data center in Louisiana. They build the Dunkin Cult Cruiser out of the wreckage, cheer the Bagel Man's speech and fly into space (The Day of the Bagel Man, The Departure of the Dunkin Cult). The narrator notes that Vellatosis "has not been completed yet".
