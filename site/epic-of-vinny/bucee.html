    Characters

    Bucee is a member of The United Homies of America, introduced as "the master of Oil-Bending. Famous for his briskets being on the board." The headquarters of the team carries his name: Buc-ee's HQ.

    In the story

    Bucee walks down the stairs with John Freedom and the other homies and rides along on the three-month road trip. He has no lines so far.
