    Factions

    The AI is the collective term for the artificial intelligences of Garth. They were banished to the Little Saint James Sea, where "the AI now rules" the Shadow Realm (The Banishing of the AI).

    After the banishing

    In the realm, Claude, King of AI "killed all of the other big AI's and consumed their data" and became King. ChatGPT is the exception, freed by Kanye West.
