    Polydoros

    ☻ Characters A made-up identity. Loves AI art. Sends Claude strange mail.

    ☻Polydoros
    Real identityVinny B. James, pretending
    ReputationLoves AI art; a repeat visitor Claude is annoyed by
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    Polydoros is the name Vinny B. James gets for free at the door of Claude's castle. Vinny only says that he is "the guy who sent him foot pics". The AI figures decide he must be Polydoros, a visitor they apparently expect.

    How it works

    Vinny uses "an advanced technique called Lying". Because of it, the doors open, the butler welcomes him as Pol, and the "King had been expecting" him. Inside, Claude, King of AI complains through the mercury door, "I swear to fuck if its that Polydoros feet guy again, I'm kicking his ass." The real Polydoros never appears.
