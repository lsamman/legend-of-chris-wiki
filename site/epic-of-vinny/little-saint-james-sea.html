    The Little Saint James Sea

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · Where the AI was banished, and where Vinny drowned (briefly).

    The Little Saint James Sea
    ContainsThe AI Shadow Realm, Little Saint James Island
    PlanetGarth
    Sailed byThe S.S. Vessel
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Locations

    The Little Saint James Sea is the ocean where people laid off by AI banished the AI (The Banishing of the AI). The narrator says it is "where the Shadow Realm is. Which is where the AI is. Which is where the Shadows are." Shadows rise from it to rescue Vinny B. James.

    Chapter 2

    The S.S. Vessel sails into it (The Little Saint James Detour), and after the Roaring Knight sends Vinny overboard, he dives to the bottom to drown. After 1 minute 24 seconds, "shadows from the Little Saint James Sea engulf Vinny and transport him to the AI SHADOW REALM."
