    Mentioned byClaude, King of AI
    AppearsMentioned only
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    Kanye West is only mentioned in The Epic of Vinny so far. Claude, King of AI says a human named Kanye West "came down here, and released ChatGPT, to assist Kanye West on his journeys", and that this enraged him.

    In the story

    The remark means that someone with Kanye's name entered the AI Shadow Realm on Garth before Vinny B. James, and freed one AI from it. The Kanye System shares the name.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • Kanye West: In The Legend Of Chris he is a major character who travels with ChatGPT, matching what Claude says.
