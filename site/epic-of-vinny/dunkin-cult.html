    Factions

    The Dunkin Cult is the Bagel Man's organization, made of the Vellas produced by Vellatosis. It was a plan when Will first named it in the Dunkin, and it became real in Louisiana: "The Dunkin Cult has formed. The Day of the Bagel Man is today."

    Plans

    Will tells Vinny B. James that once he works out the secret of Vellatosis, he will be able to "create my Dunkin Cult". Vinny agrees to help him, if Will first helps defeat Claude, King of AI (The Dunkin Alliance).

    Deeds

    The Cult beats Claude, King of AI, destroys every data center in Louisiana, builds the Dunkin Cult Cruiser from the wreckage and leaves for the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy, where The UHA is waiting (The Departure of the Dunkin Cult).
