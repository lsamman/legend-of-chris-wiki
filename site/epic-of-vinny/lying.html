    Lying

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Concepts & Lore · "An advanced technique." Very strongly used in acts of war.

    Lying
    Used byVinny B. James
    ContextGetting into Claude's castle
    ResultVinny is mistaken for Polydoros
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Concepts & Lore

    Lying is called "an advanced technique" by the narrator when Vinny B. James tells the castle's doorkeeper that he is the person who sent Claude "foot pics". The narrator adds, "It's very strongly used in acts of war. Even though Vinny isn't at war right now."

    Result

    The lie opens the castle to Vinny, who is welcomed as Polydoros. It holds up with the butler and almost fails at the throne room door.
