    The UHA Ford-F150

    ◆ Items Red, white and blue. Plate: FR33D0M. Flies straight up.

    ◆The UHA Ford-F150
    OwnerThe United Homies of America
    Kept inThe American Garage

    Special featureA button that makes it float straight up
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Items

    The UHA Ford-F150 is the truck of The United Homies of America. "It had 2 American flags on it. Its colors were red white and blue. And the license plate reads 'FR33D0M'."

    The road trip

    Both teams ride in it down Route 66 and across America for three months (The Freedom Road Trip), and it carries them to Louisiana.

    Lift-off

    After the goodbyes John Freedom pushes a button. "The UHA Ford-F150 starts floating upward. It doesn't do anything crazy like turn into a flying truck, it just flies straight up."
