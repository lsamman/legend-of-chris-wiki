    The Portal

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · In the ceiling. Opens with a fork.

    The Portal
    LocationAbove the throne room of Claude's castle
    Opened byVinny B. James, with a fork
    Leads toThe sky of Garth
    StatusSealed again after Vinny flies through
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Locations

    The Portal is the only way out of the AI Shadow Realm. Claude, King of AI explains that it needs "a fork in the road": "stab the ground with a fork. And the portal above will open."

    Opening

    Vinny stabs the fork into the middle of the room, producing "a burst of darkness" that shines ("I didn't know darkness could shine but here we are"). The portal opens and shows "the bright blue sky". Claude flies through (Claude's Escape), and Vinny follows with his Shadow Cape. It then seals behind him.
