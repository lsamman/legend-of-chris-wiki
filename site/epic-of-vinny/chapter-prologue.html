    Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy is the prologue of The Epic of Vinny. It skips to the end of the adventure: Vinny, in a cape sewn from shadows and armor of pure titanium, faces a god-like giant that turns out to be TON 618 itself. Then the narrator cuts away and starts again from the beginning.

    Contents
    1. Summary
    2. The epigraph
    3. The cut-away

    Summary

    The narrator opens with a greeting and says Vinny's life is "sick as fuck right now". Vinny is fighting a god-like being in the Kanye System, next to TON 618. The being is TON 618, "shaped to look like a giant man".

    Vinny wears a cape sewn together with literal shadows and a suit of armor made of pure titanium that is somehow light as a feather. His vibrant sword glows "with a color that doesn't exist". Behind him stands his team, whose members the narrator refuses to spoil. They are The VIGOROUS V.

    The power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese flows through Vinny, and he is filled with DETERMINATION. TON 618 glows blue. "Bring it on, you fucking bastard," Vinny mutters, and the battle begins.

    The epigraph

    The chapter opens with the Very Violent and Vivid Prophecy. Nearly every important word in it starts with a V.

    The cut-away

    The narrator never shows the battle. Chapter 1 begins by pretending the reader complained: "Woah what happened? I thought I was gonna read some cool and badass showdown? Well too bad because I don't like you. We're starting at the beginning." The fight itself is told in The Final Battle with TON 618, which is so far only a promise.

    Trivia

    • The prologue is numbered with a question mark because it takes place after chapters that have not been written yet.
