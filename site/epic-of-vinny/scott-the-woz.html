    Scott the Woz

    ☻ Characters Found a teleportation bug in Wii Play. Please buy Wii Play.

    ☻Scott the Woz
    SeenIn a YouTube tutorial on Will Vella's iPhone 3G
    DiscoveredThe 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique
    Source of powerA secret bug in the code of Wii Play
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Characters

    Scott the Woz appears on screen in the YouTube tutorial "How to use the 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique". He explains the technique, demonstrates it by fading out and back into the chair next to him, and asks viewers to buy a copy of Wii Play.

    The tutorial

    "You see, you just put a peace sign and think of where you want to go, and you instantly teleport there. But you can only use it twice, ok? Twice. Why? Because I put a limit on it. Why? I don't know, sue me. I should stop ending sentences with that." He notes that he just spent one of his own two teleports on the demonstration, a secret he could have kept.

    Played twice

    Del was not paying attention, so Will plays the whole video again. The story prints the full tutorial a second time.
