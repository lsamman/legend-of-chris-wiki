    The Fork in the Road

    ◆ Items A fork, at a fork in the road. The story knows.

    ◆The Fork in the Road
    FoundAt a fork in the road in the AI Shadow Realm
    FinderVinny B. James
    UseOpens the portal when stabbed into the ground
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Items

    The Fork in the Road is a regular fork that Vinny B. James finds at a fork in the road on the way to Claude's castle. "Vinny picks up the fork and saves it for later. Who knows, maybe you'll need a fork."

    The portal

    Claude, King of AI says the only way to open the portal is with "a fork in the road", which he has never found. Vinny produces it, and both Claude and Vinny comment on the pun. Vinny notes that he earlier got out of a pickle, and that if he is in a story "the creator is making terrible puns". A screwdriver meme is promised in the text, in brackets (The Opening of the Portal).

    Dark Fountain

    Vinny asks whether stabbing the ground would make "a Dark Fountain". Claude says no: it is "dark as shit already down here".
