    Concepts & Lore

    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese War is a prophesied "multiversal war" that "will soon come to pass" as of July 4th, 1995. It is the reason "everyone loves Burger King in the Kanye System".

    What is known

    Chapter 1: Beginnings says there are "tales and prophecies" about the war. The details are not given. The Great Double Whopper with Cheese is both its namesake and its prize, since whoever holds it has its power. The prologue's fight with TON 618 may be a battle of this war (The Final Battle with TON 618).
