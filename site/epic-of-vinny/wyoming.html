    Wyoming

    ⌖ Locations Has exactly three Dunkins.

    ⌖Wyoming
    CountryUnited States of America
    PlanetGarth
    Dunkins3, per Chapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Notable siteThe Dunkin, a mountain
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    Wyoming is where Claude, King of AI throws Vinny B. James at full force (The Clash Over the USA). Vinny crashes into a mountain and lies there "in pain for about 6 hours and 8 minutes" until sundown.

    The mountain

    Vinny is "genuinely not balling frfr", then decides what he wants is Dunkin Donuts. He flies out of the mountain and finds one in the state "even though Wyoming only has 3".

    After the Age of Claude

    Del and Aesop Rock "eventually got back to Wyoming", where their song became the most popular song of the moment (Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World).
