    The S.S. Vessel

    ⌖ Locations A pirate-style ship. Fast. Sings shanties.

    ⌖The S.S. Vessel
    TypeShip
    CaptainCaptain Wing Ding Gaster
    CrewThe S.S. Vessel crew
    MissionFind The Great Double Whopper with Cheese
    PassengersVinny B. James (until overboard), Tyrone
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Locations

    The S.S. Vessel is the ship where Vinny B. James and Tyrone wake up after FRIEND carries them off. Captain Gaster sails it in search of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese.

    Voyage

    The crew sing shanties and drink rum & coke. After an island is sighted, the ship "speeds away", and speeds off again after the Roaring Knight knocks Vinny overboard. Tyrone stays aboard.
