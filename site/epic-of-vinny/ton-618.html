    Characters

    TON 618 is the ruler of the Kanye System. After it "became shaped like a giant man" it "soon realized it had great power", its light burned "into a bright blue", and it conquered the whole system. In the prologue it is the god-like being Vinny B. James fights.

    History

    Many say TON 618 "consumed the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese to conquer all things", and many say otherwise. Afterwards it was at rest, waiting to wake again (TON 618's Conquest of the Kanye System).

    The prologue

    In Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy, TON 618 is awake and glowing blue. The prologue puts Vinny "in the Kanye System, next to the TON 618", fighting a god-like giant that "IS the TON 618". The fight itself is the subject of The Final Battle with TON 618.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • TON 618: In The Legend Of Chris, TON 618 is a monolithic bio-robotic weapon with a red eye, built by Osama Bin Bom'in. Whether it is the same entity is not stated.
