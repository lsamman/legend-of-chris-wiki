    The United States of America

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · Needs more data centers. And donuts, per Will.

    The United States of America
    PlanetGarth
    Cities and statesBoston, Wyoming
    Claude's planMore data centers
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings (implied by Boston); named in Chapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    The United States of America is the country on Garth where Vinny B. James lives. Claude, King of AI flies down into it in Chapter 3: The Age of Claude and concludes, "Needs more data centers."

    In the story

    Vinny's Boston, the Dunkin in Wyoming, and the setting of The Clash Over the USA are all within it. The Starwalker manager reminds Will of it: "This is America."

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The United State of America: In The Legend Of Chris the country is spelled "The United State of America" and is home to the Projects.
