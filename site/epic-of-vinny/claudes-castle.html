    Claude's Castle

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · AI doors, AI paintings, a door of liquid mercury.

    Claude's Castle
    LocationAI Shadow Realm
    OwnerClaude, King of AI
    FeaturesDoors made of AI, a hallway of AI paintings, a giant door of liquid mercury, a throne room
    StaffAn AI butler and others
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Locations

    Claude's Castle stands in the distance of the AI Shadow Realm, and is where Claude, King of AI sits on his throne. Vinny B. James gets in by claiming to be Polydoros.

    The approach

    Vinny knocks on the AI doors. A voice yells, "I don't care who the IRS sends, I'm not paying taxes." Vinny answers, "I'm not in the IRS, I love gambling," and the voice replies, "Type shit." The doors open on a long hallway with an AI shadow butler and "lots of AI paintings".

    The throne room

    The throne room is behind "a giant door made of liquid mercury". When Vinny knocks there, the voice that asks who it is speaks "in a high voice". Vinny says he is Claude's wife and orders him to "OPEN THE DOOR, CLANKER!!!!!!!!!!" The door opens on a figure with orange eyes on a throne.
