    Chapters

    Chapter 2: The Weird Route is the longest chapter so far. Vinny B. James and Tyrone join the crew of the S.S. Vessel, Vinny is knocked overboard, and he wakes in the AI Shadow Realm. There he meets Claude, King of AI, loses a game of Smash, and opens the portal that lets the AI king out.

    Summary

    Aboard the ship. Vinny and Tyrone wake on the S.S. Vessel, where Captain Wing Ding Gaster is screaming at FRIEND for bringing humans instead of Double Whoppers with Cheese. The crew is FRIEND, ERAM, Normal_Parrot and The Roaring Knight. Their mission is to find The Great Double Whopper with Cheese and rule the Kanye System. Vinny and Tyrone sign on.

    The crew sing shanties and drink rum & coke. They sight an island and flee it (The Little Saint James Detour). Vinny insults Taking Back Sunday, and the Roaring Knight stabs him with the BLACK KNIFE, sending him overboard (The Roaring Knight's Strike). Tyrone asks Vinny to tell the boss he quits.

    In the Shadow Realm. Vinny dives to the bottom of the ocean to die. After 1 minute 24 seconds, shadows from the sea carry him to the AI Shadow Realm. He finds a fork in the road and keeps it, reaches Claude's castle by posing as Polydoros, and meets Claude, King of AI, the King of AI.

    Claude wants the portal opened so he can destroy the human world. They settle it with a game of Super Smash Bros.; Vinny loses. He opens the portal with the fork (The Opening of the Portal), and Claude flies out (Claude's Escape). The AI shadow figures give Vinny a Shadow Cape. The chapter ends: "The Age of Claude had begun."

    Notes

    Characters in this chapter comment on the story's puns. Claude points out that a fork in the road is "a really bad pun", and Vinny agrees that if he is in a story, "the creator is making terrible puns".

    Trivia

    • The story includes retro RPG-style text boxes with a green-faced speaker. One announces that The Roaring Knight is "Roary Oleary", which echoes the author's surname.
