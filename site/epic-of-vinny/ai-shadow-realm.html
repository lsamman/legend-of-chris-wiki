    The AI Shadow Realm

    ⌖ Locations Where the AI went. Where Claude rules. Dark as shit already.

    ⌖The AI Shadow Realm
    LocationUnder the Little Saint James Sea, Garth
    RulerClaude, King of AI
    ResidentsAI shadow figures
    Notable siteClaude's castle
    ExitA portal, opened with a fork
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Locations

    The AI Shadow Realm is where all the AI of Garth was banished (The Banishing of the AI). It is full of shadows and ruled by Claude, King of AI, King of AI. It is so dark that a Dark Fountain would make no difference.

    Geography

    Vinny B. James arrives wet, meets a rude AI shadow figure, and finds a fork in the road and an AI castle in the distance (Claude's castle). The castle's doors are "made entirely out of AI".

    The portal

    A portal in the ceiling lets someone out, but only if a fork in the road is stabbed into the ground (The Opening of the Portal). It seals again when Vinny leaves. He and Claude see "the bright blue sky" of Garth through it.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • The Shadow Realm: In The Legend Of Chris, the Shadow Realm is where Zuckerberg's Snap sends every universe but one. The text of The Epic of Vinny does not say whether they are the same place.
