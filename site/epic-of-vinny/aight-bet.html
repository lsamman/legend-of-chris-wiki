    "Aight, bet"

    ✦ Concepts & Lore Vinny's answer to dangerous ideas.

    ✦"Aight, bet"
    Said byVinny B. James
    MeaningAgreement
    Uses so farTwice (the storage room, the portal)
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Concepts & Lore

    "Aight, bet" is Vinny B. James's agreement phrase. He says it when Tyrone suggests checking storage (Chapter 1: Beginnings), and again when Claude, King of AI tells him how to open the portal (Chapter 2: The Weird Route).

    Uses

    Each use is followed by a major escalation. The first leads into the The Backrooms. The second leads to the portal and Claude's escape.

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • "Aight Bet": In The Legend Of Chris, "Aight Bet" is Plankton's answer to almost everything.
