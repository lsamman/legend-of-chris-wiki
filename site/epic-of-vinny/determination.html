    Concepts & Lore

    DETERMINATION is what Vinny B. James is "filled with" in the prologue, "to take this motherfucker DOWN", as he faces TON 618. It is always written in capital letters.

    Use

    It appears alongside "the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese", which also flows through Vinny. Together they are what makes him ready to fight (Chapter ?: The Final Prophecy).
