    The Opening of the Portal

    ✸ Events One fork. One burst of darkness that shines.

    ✸The Opening of the Portal
    WhenJuly 4th, 1995
    WhereThe throne room of Claude's castle
    Done byVinny B. James
    ToolThe Fork in the Road
    ResultClaude, King of AI can leave
    First mentionChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Events

    The Opening of the Portal is how Vinny B. James honors his Smash bet: he stabs the fork into the middle of the room, and "a burst of darkness shines in the room". The portal above opens, and he and Claude, King of AI see "the bright blue sky".

    The conversation

    Before the stab, Claude says Vinny needs "a fork in the road", Vinny produces it, and they discuss the pun. Vinny asks whether it will make a Dark Fountain; Claude says no. Vinny: "Aight bet." ("Aight, bet")
