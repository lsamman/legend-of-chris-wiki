    The Freedom Road Trip

    ✸ Events Three months of monuments, football and hot dogs.

    ✸The Freedom Road Trip
    WhenJuly to October 28th, 1995
    RouteRoute 66, then "across America"
    TravellersThe Dunkin Alliance and The UHA
    VehicleThe UHA Ford-F150
    EndsLouisiana
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The Freedom road trip is the three-month training of the Dunkin Alliance by The UHA. "They visit monuments, memorial sights, and cities. They went to various football games and hot dog eating contests."

    Outcome

    "3 months have passed and the day is October 28th. The Dunkin Alliance felt like America was running on them." Meanwhile Claude, King of AI streams his plans (Claude's Livestreams), so the UHA drives the Alliance to Louisiana.
