    The Double Whopper with Cheese is the most-ordered item in the Kanye System. Literally "98.578462849224% of the time, someone orders a Double Whopper with Cheese", because of the prophecy of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese War.

    In the story

    Vinny B. James recommends one to the random customer. Tyrone announces they have run out, and the The Backrooms storage room turns out to be full of them. FRIEND is sent to fetch some for Captain Gaster and returns with humans and a soggy Double Whopper without Cheese.

    Not to be confused with

    The Great Double Whopper with Cheese, the legendary power, which nobody has found.
