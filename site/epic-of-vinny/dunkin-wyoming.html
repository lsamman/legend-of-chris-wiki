    The Dunkin (Wyoming)

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · One of three. Currently hosting a Wordle diss track.

    The Dunkin (Wyoming)
    LocationWyoming
    PatronsAesop Rock, Del the Funky Homosapien, Will Vella, Vinny B. James
    EmployeeThe Starwalker manager
    EventThe Dunkin Alliance
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    The Dunkin is the coffee shop in Wyoming where Vinny B. James goes after his fight with Claude, King of AI. He orders "a large iced latte with extra latte" and ends up forming a team.

    Who is there

    Aesop Rock and Del are writing a diss track about Wordle at a table. Will Vella is angry about the lack of England-style donuts and plans to rule Dunkin. The Starwalker manager tells Will, "This is America." Vinny B. James, Will, Aesop Rock and Del end up at the same table (The Dunkin Alliance).
