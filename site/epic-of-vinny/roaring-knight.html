    The Roaring Knight

    ☻ Characters Professional door dasher. Defender of Taking Back Sunday.

    ☻The Roaring Knight
    Also known asRoary; Roary Knight
    Role"Professional door dasher", S.S. Vessel crew
    WeaponThe BLACK KNIFE
    Weak pointInsults to Taking Back Sunday
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Characters

    The Roaring Knight (called Roary) is a S.S. Vessel crew member and the narrator's "professional door dasher". They knock Vinny B. James off the ship with the BLACK KNIFE after he insults the band Taking Back Sunday.

    The strike

    Gaster has just claimed the island's secret might be the Great Double Whopper with Cheese. Vinny shouts that only the god has it and that Gaster is "almost as stupid as Taking Back Sunday". The Roaring Knight "stared at Vinny with pure rage", roared and charged, and hit him with the BLACK KNIFE (The Roaring Knight's Strike). Roary's only line is a flat "FUCK OFF" as the ship speeds away.

    Trivia

    • A text box in the story announces: "OH YEAH, THE ROARING KNIGHT IS ROARY OLEARY LMAO". The name ROARY OLEARY is printed in yellow.
    • The updated text adds that Roary "loves Taking Back Sunday", which explains the reaction.
