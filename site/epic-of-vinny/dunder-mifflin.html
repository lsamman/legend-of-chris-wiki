    The Dunder Mifflin Galaxy

    ⌖ Locations Where the MacBook Charger is. Where everyone will meet again.

    ⌖The Dunder Mifflin Galaxy
    Reached byThe Dunkin Cult Cruiser; the UHA Ford-F150 (presumably)
    HoldsThe MacBook Charger
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    The Dunder Mifflin Galaxy is where Will Vella expects to find the MacBook Charger. John Freedom's goodbye is "See you in the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy, Bagel Man," and the Bagel Man tells the Vellas that The UHA "are waiting for us" there.

    Reaction

    When Will first mentions flying out to it, three unnamed voices respond: "what." "what." "what."

    In The Legend Of Chris

    • Dunder Mifflin (Galaxy): The Dunder Mifflin Galaxy is a major location in The Legend Of Chris: a galaxy wrapped in an office building.
