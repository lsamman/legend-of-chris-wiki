    The Forklift

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Items · Nobody on this page is forklift certified.

    The Forklift
    OwnerGarret the Forklift Guy
    LocationThe Backrooms
    DriversVinny B. James, Tyrone (taking turns)
    Certified driversGarret the Forklift Guy only
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Items

    The Forklift belongs to Garret the Forklift Guy, who is on break in the The Backrooms. He tells Vinny B. James and Tyrone they can use it. Vinny drives and does donuts until he hits Garret with it.

    Use

    Vinny grabs the keys, turns the engine on, and says "Hell yeah, I call driving it." After the crash he and Tyrone "take turns playing with the forklift instead of doing their fucking job" (The Forklift Incident).
