    Chapter 3: The Age of Claude

    § Chapters Wyoming, Dunkin, Texas, three months of Freedom, and the day Louisiana became a data center.

    Chapters

    Chapter 3: The Age of Claude is the longest chapter and covers almost four months. Claude, King of AI throws Vinny B. James into Wyoming, Vinny builds the Dunkin Alliance in a Dunkin, the team trains with The United Homies of America, and the Age of Claude ends in Louisiana when Will Vella achieves Vellatosis.

    Wyoming

    Claude looks down at Garth, disgusted by how "infested with humans" it is, and flies into the United States of America, noting it "needs more data centers". Vinny flies into him at full speed. Claude chokes him, insults his Smash main, and throws him into Wyoming (The Clash Over the USA).

    Vinny lies in a mountain for 6 hours and 8 minutes, then craves Dunkin Donuts and finds one of Wyoming's three. Inside are Aesop Rock and Del the Funky Homosapien writing a diss track about failing Wordle ("Wordle? More like Word-shit"), and Will Vella, who is angry about the lack of English-style donuts. The Starwalker manager tells Will, "This is America." Will declares himself the Bagel Man. Vinny trades help with Vellatosis for help against Claude, and the rappers join because Claude likes Wordle (The Dunkin Alliance).

    The crash and the tutorial

    The four leave in the Mystery Machine, which Del stole from Scooby-Doo and Shaggy. On July 5th Del crashes it while high, and it blows up (The Mystery Machine Crash). Will plays a YouTube tutorial on the iPhone 3G in which Scott the Woz teaches the 2-time 2-fingered Teleportation Technique. Del was not paying attention, so the whole tutorial plays twice.

    Texas and Freedom

    Vinny wants to gear up in the Backrooms behind the freezer in Boston. Aesop teleports everyone to Austin, Texas instead (The Teleport to Texas), in front of Buc-ee's HQ, home of The United Homies of America: Bucee, Kilroy, Uncle Sam and their leader John Freedom. John speaks Aesopian, learns that Claude has returned, and takes both teams down Route 66 in the UHA Ford-F150. Three months of monuments, football games and hot dog eating contests follow (The Freedom Road Trip). Meanwhile Claude streams his plans for hours a day and announces the Great Data Center (Claude's Livestreams).

    Louisiana

    On October 28th the UHA drops the Alliance in Louisiana and floats away. Aesop's last teleport takes them to Claude, who is playing Wordle in a data center. Claude shows Vinny a Louisiana made entirely of data centers, reverses the Louisiana Purchase with a snap, and announces the Super Bowl '96 plot. Vinny tackles him. The battle lasts six hours and the Alliance loses.

    At sunrise Will asks how one achieves Vellatosis. Claude explains it. Will splits into 2 Vellas, then 4, then 8, and so forth (The Day of the Bagel Man). The Vellas beat Claude, destroy every data center, build the Dunkin Cult Cruiser and leave for the Dunder Mifflin Galaxy to find the MacBook Charger (The Departure of the Dunkin Cult). Claude buys Louisiana back for America, and Vinny chucks him into space (The End of the Age of Claude). The Alliance disbands and calls an Uber.

    Notes

    • The chapter title is printed in three colors: "CHAPTER 3:" in blue, "The Age of" in black, and "Claude" in orange.
    • Claude, King of AI's lines are set in Arial; the rest of the story is in Comic Sans.
    • The Scott the Woz tutorial is printed in full twice.
    • Three unnamed voices answer Will's Dunder Mifflin plan with "what." "what." "what."
