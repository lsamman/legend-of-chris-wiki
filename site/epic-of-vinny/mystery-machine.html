    The Mystery Machine

    ◆ Items Stolen from Scooby-Doo and Shaggy for literally no reason.

    ◆The Mystery Machine
    Stolen byDel
    FromScooby-Doo and Shaggy
    Used byThe Dunkin Alliance
    StatusCrashed, then blown up
    First appearanceChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Items

    The Mystery Machine is the van the Dunkin Alliance leaves the Dunkin in. Del "stole [it] from Scooby-Doo and Shaggy for literally no reason".

    Fate

    They set off for Boston. "Surely nothing will go wrong." On July 5th Del, who smoked weed during the drive, crashes it. Vinny B. James sits on a rock and mutters, "Well, it could be worse." The Mystery Machine then blows up (The Mystery Machine Crash).
