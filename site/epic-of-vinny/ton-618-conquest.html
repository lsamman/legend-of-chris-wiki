    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Events · Giant man. Blue light. Then a long nap.

    Events

    TON 618's Conquest of the Kanye System is the backstory event in which TON 618 "became shaped like a giant man", "realized it had great power", turned bright blue, and "conquered the Kanye System".

    Cause and aftermath

    "Many say that the TON 618 consumed the power of The Great Double Whopper with Cheese to conquer all things. Many say otherwise." Since the conquest, TON 618 "has been at rest, waiting to wake again." In the prologue it has.
