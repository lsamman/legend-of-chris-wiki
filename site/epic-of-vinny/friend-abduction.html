    FRIEND's Abduction

    ✸ Events Wrong delivery, correct wall.

    ✸FRIEND's Abduction
    WhenJuly 4th, 1995
    WhereThe The Backrooms
    TakenVinny B. James and Tyrone
    Taken byFRIEND
    DestinationThe S.S. Vessel
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Events

    FRIEND's Abduction is how Vinny B. James and Tyrone leave the The Backrooms. FRIEND appears behind them, grabs both, "runs throughout the backrooms before no-clipping into a wall", and delivers them to Captain Gaster.

    Why

    FRIEND had been sent for Double Whoppers with Cheese. Gaster explains that he wanted "DOUBLE WHOPPERS WITH CHEESE, NOT HUMANS!!!!", which FRIEND answers with a soggy Double Whopper without Cheese (Chapter 2: The Weird Route).
