    Super Smash Bros. for the Wii U

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Items · The game that decided the fate of the world.

    TypeVideo game (real)
    Played byVinny B. James (Greninja) and Claude, King of AI (Mr. Game & Watch)
    StakesClaude wins: Vinny frees him. Vinny wins: Claude leaves.
    WinnerClaude
    First appearanceChapter 2: The Weird Route
    Items

    Super Smash Bros. for the Wii U is the game Claude, King of AI picks to settle their bargain. The winner of the match gets his way. Claude wins, and the portal gets opened.

    Details

    The text includes the words "(pause)" where the characters' moves happen. Vinny's complaint after losing is that "I was using the crappy controller!" Claude answers with a long chant of "Copium". In Chapter 3: The Age of Claude, Claude whispers that Vinny's Smash main "sucks ass" while choking him.
