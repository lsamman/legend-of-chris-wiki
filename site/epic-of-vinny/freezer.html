    The Freezer

    From The Epic of Vinny Wiki · Locations · Cold. Cleaning duty. Wall.

    The Freezer
    LocationBurger King, Boston
    Leads toThe The Backrooms, through the wall
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    Locations

    The Freezer at the Burger King is on Vinny B. James's list of chores. When Vinny and Tyrone open it, they "walk into the wall and enter the backrooms".

    Use as a door

    The text does not say how the wall works. It is the first of several walls that characters pass through in Chapter 1: Beginnings (see No-clipping).
