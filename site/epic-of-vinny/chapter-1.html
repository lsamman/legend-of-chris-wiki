    Chapters

    Chapter 1: Beginnings introduces the world of Garth in 1995: a system where everyone loves Burger King, a sleeping god-giant, and an AI banished to a sea. Then it follows Vinny B. James through his Fourth of July shift, which ends with him being carried into a wall by something called FRIEND.

    Summary

    The world. It is July 4th, 1995. Tales and prophecies speak of a multiversal war, The Great Double Whopper with Cheese War. That is why everyone in the Kanye System loves Burger King: 98.578462849224% of the time, somebody orders a Double Whopper with Cheese. No one has ever found The Great Double Whopper with Cheese god or The Great Double Whopper with Cheese itself.

    TON 618 rules the system, having taken the shape of a giant man and conquered it. Since then it has been at rest, "waiting to wake again". Elsewhere, people laid off because of AI banished the AI to the Little Saint James Sea, where it now rules the Shadow Realm.

    The shift. Vinny B. James lives in Boston and works at a Burger King. His British boss refuses him a vacation day for Independence Day. A random customer who strangely loves Wendy's orders a Pretzel-Bun Baconator and leaves. Tyrone says they have run out of Double Whoppers with Cheese, they trade five rounds of "Nuh uh" and "Yuh huh", and the two walk through the The Freezer wall into the The Backrooms.

    There they find a room packed with Double Whoppers with Cheese and Garret the Forklift Guy on his break. Vinny drives the The Forklift and knocks Garret into a wall (The Forklift Incident). A black cat with yellow and pink eyes appears in a box and disappears (The Cat in the Box). Then FRIEND appears behind them and carries both away (FRIEND's Abduction).

    Notes

    The chapter ends mid-shift. Vinny and Tyrone never clock out, which becomes a running problem in Chapter 2: The Weird Route.

    Revisions

    The updated text adds several details: the prophecies were "foretold years and years ago"; the Great Double Whopper with Cheese gives "immense strength and defense. And luck for gambling"; TON 618 "has ruled the Kanye System for millions of years" and "is the same TON 618 in the Legend of Chris"; the AI was banished "in the year 1972"; and the black cat in the box is now identified: "It's FRIEND. FRIEND laughs and disappears."

    Trivia

    • A customer's order is stretched across four lines of the letter "h" before it reaches "Pretzel-Bun Baconator".
    • Vinny's forklift whoop is a long string of "O"s with the word "gambling" hidden in the middle.
