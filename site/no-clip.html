    No-clipping

    Concepts & Lore From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Walking through walls, on purpose or otherwise.

    No-clipping
    Done byGarret the Forklift Guy, FRIEND, and Vinny B. James and Tyrone (through the freezer wall)
    OriginVideo game term
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 1: Beginnings
    Concepts & Lore

    No-clipping is how characters in Chapter 1: Beginnings pass through walls: Vinny B. James and Tyrone "walk into the wall" of the The Freezer, Garret the Forklift Guy "no-clips to somewhere" when hit by a forklift, and FRIEND carries them "before no-clipping into a wall".

    Uses

    It is used as transport in the The Backrooms (FRIEND's Abduction) and as an accident (The Forklift Incident). The story never says where the walls lead.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
