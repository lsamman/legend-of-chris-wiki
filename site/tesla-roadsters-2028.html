    Tesla 2028 Roadsters

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Two, from Elon.

    Tesla 2028 Roadsters
    MakerTesla / SpaceX
    Sent byElon Musk, via text
    Quantity2
    Used byThe New Apple Employees; Walter and Jesse
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    The Tesla 2028 Roadsters are two vehicles that Scott has en route from Elon Musk: “I have 2028 Tesla Roaders enroute to our location from musk, I just texted him their on their way.” They “docked to the border” as “2 SpaceX Tesla 2028 roadsters”.

    Elon

    The gang is stunned: “wait your in contact with Elon?” Scott: “Yeah who do you think peer reviews his ideas, he’s pretty chill if you get to know him”. Asked whether they will summon him when they roll back to 3G, he says Elon “just plays ball for the love of the game and because he has connections, he lowkirkgenuinely doesn’t care too much”.

    This is the first on-page evidence that Elon Musk can be reached by text. The Wiki notes it is not Elon Mush.

    Use

    One roadster takes Walter and Jesse with the Wii box to Ohio. The other takes the remaining four: Steve, Kanye, ChatGPT and Hank. Steve is notified of a purchase in the same scene; see Kromer.
