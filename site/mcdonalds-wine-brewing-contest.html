    September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing Contest

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · The vintage that started a war.

    September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing Contest
    DateSeptember 2002
    Host cityMoscow (Italian City)
    OrganizerPresumably McDonald's
    ConsequenceThe DQ–McDonald's MySpace Beef
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Events

    The September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing Contest was a competition held in Moscow, "the Italian city". The book only mentions it as background: Moscow "was recently wrapped in controversy for hosting the September 2002 McDonalds Wine Brewing contest. This Which resulted in DQ starting beef on MySpace with Mac Donald's."

    Analysis

    The canon does not say who won, what was brewed, or why DQ was upset. Leading theories include:

    • DQ was not invited.
    • DQ entered a Blizzard and was disqualified because it was not wine.
    • Wine is not normally brewed, and DQ pointed this out.

    None of these is confirmed.

    The contest indirectly caused the Attack on Moscow, making it one of the most consequential beverage events in the canon.

    Trivia

    • "Recently" refers to a 2002 contest. Since MySpace launched in 2003, the beef may have been waiting a year for a platform to happen on.
