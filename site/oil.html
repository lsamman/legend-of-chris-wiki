    Oil

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · The resource behind more of the plot than you'd think.

    Oil
    TypeNatural resource
    Notable gradesPrime US oil; mountain dew oil
    Known reservesThe Yeezy Reserve
    Sought byHank Hill, Jesse Pinkman, Walter White
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Items & Technology

    Oil is a resource that appears throughout the later canon. It is used as a weapon of urban flooding, as a currency for security services, and as a motivation for overthrowing Chris.

    Appearances

    Constantinople

    In Chapter Triangle, Mr. Bean explains that the plan to defeat Barack Obama involves breaking "the main oil lines to the city causing flooding of the city, once the city is one big oil pool we lure Obamna into the center and light the nukes". The oil part of the plan is not mentioned again during the Fall of Constantinople.

    Dusty Depot

    In the The Flashback Chapter, Hank's crew were "searching for a oil deposit in dusty depot as their oil ran out". A Quick-Check clerk told Jesse of "a oil casino nearby": The 6 7 Casino.

    Prime US oil

    Hank demanded "15 gallons of your finest Prime US oil" from Microsoft Bing as payment for guarding the relic. Bing called it "a high price".

    The case against Chris

    During the Casino Heist, Hank explained that since being teleported to Dusty Depot "we haven't had a ounce of oil for our business". Walter realised that if Chris were defeated, the oil reserves "There would be plenty of them, we could drive the cost down exponentially, creating a market almost even over dependent on oil!" Hank then noted that Kanye already had "plenty of oil in the Yeezy reserve".

    Mountain dew oil

    In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, the escape door from the 360 Chambers is "smothered in Dorito dust and mountain dew oil".

    Analysis

    The canon never explains why Chris's existence restricts the oil supply. Editors have proposed that, since Chris "took balling all for himself", he may have done the same with oil, but this is speculation.

    Oil and Propane seem to be interchangeable for Hank's business: Hank says "we ran out of propane" in the same scene where the narration says their oil ran out.
