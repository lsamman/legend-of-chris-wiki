    Propane is one of the main commodities in the canon. Two rival operations deal in it: Hank Hill's propane empire, which distributes "propane and propane accessories" with Los Polos Harmonicas Under System Gang, and Kanye West's brand Ye the Gas.

    The watering-down scandal

    After the Weezing of Kanye, Hank found that Kanye's propane had been watered down. He was horrified: "How many people did Kanye lie to all the barbecue bros all the car enthusiasts all the people who needed to commit arson, how could he". He finished with "This isn't over kanye".

    In the next scene, Kanye asks ChatGPT whether his new song will "ensure the continuation of the water gas trend ive been creating". This suggests the watering down was deliberate and possibly a marketing strategy.

    In culture

    • At Club Shadowban, Lil Jokez dissed Kanye: "Kanye's propane is mid". Kanye answered: "I'm Ye the Gas, no watered-down trash". Given the scandal, editors have questioned whether this is true.
    • "What in the ever loving propane yo?" is Hank's exclamation when he wakes up during the casino heist.
    • In Dusty Depot, Hank laments "Gosh dang it Jesse boy, we ran out of propane", even though the RV had actually run out of Oil.

    Trivia

    • "Propane and propane accessories" is the slogan of Strickland Propane, Hank Hill's employer in King of the Hill.
