    The O'Block Coup

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events

    The O'Block Coup
    LocationO'Block palace
    BelligerentsPol Phi Doros vs. MF Vroom

    Events

    The O'Block Coup was the seizure of O'Block and its palace by Osama Bin Bom'in and his fraternity brothers. It ended the rule of MF Vroom over the Projects. The only account of it comes from Son S. Sonionring in Chapter 4+.1+.1….

    Contents
    1. Account
    2. Aftermath
    3. Continuity errors
    4. Trivia

    Account

    “Once O’block had a nation around it the frat bros rose up, One in particular… Osama Bin Bomb’in. He was a frat member of the Pol Phi Doros with Koolaid College. One day they showed up on Vrooms porch and demanded that the O’block join the EU. When MF’Vroom declined because the Projects are in the americas not Europe they stormed his palace and gained complete control.”

    Son was not present: “That day I was at my sisters wedding, If I were there everything would be different pogchamp.”

    Aftermath

    • MF Vroom was exiled, “to forever get yelled at in COD lobbies” (see Call of Duty Lobbies).
    • Son went into hiding among the spaceport janitorial staff.
    • O'Block entered the O Block 2 era and was scaled up to an EU-level nation even though it never joined the EU.
    • Osama built the TON robots.

    Continuity errors

    In Chapter 2, MF Vroom is exiled at I-38 West by Osama while working as a guide for Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire, with no palace or frat involved. Son's account mentions a stormed palace and nothing about guiding. The fan consensus is that both are true, since it was a busy day.

    Trivia

    • The coup was carried out by Pol Phi Doros of Koolaid College. MF Vroom was in Alpha Mogger Beta at Darkworld State.
