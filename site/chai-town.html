    Chai Town

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Home of RFK's favorite (probably gang-affiliated) corner store.

    Chai Town
    TypeStreets / neighborhood
    Notable businessRFK's favorite corner store
    Gang activity"several gangs" (alleged)
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Locations

    Chai Town is a district whose streets RFK walked after leaving the Galactic Senate. It is home to his "favorite corner store that probably was involved with several gangs on the streets of Chai Town", where he picked up a bottle of G Fuel.

    Chai Town is also where Plankton (no last name) first approached RFK, making it the birthplace of the anti-Chris alliance. It is unclear whether Chai Town is on Planet Patrick, though since RFK walked there from the senate, most editors assume so.

    Trivia

    • The store's gang involvement is described only as "probably". The wiki does not accuse the corner store of anything.
    • Not to be confused with the Corner Store in Ohio, which sells potato chips for 2.85 a gallon. The canon has at least two corner stores.
    • The name is likely a pun on "Chinatown".
