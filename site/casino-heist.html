    The Casino Heist

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · The journey to the court begins with a FaceTime laser.

    The Casino Heist

    DefendersCharlie Kirks, DJ Khaled, Hank Hill's crew, a protection system

    The Casino Heist was the break-in at The 6 7 Casino by the Boys to obtain the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text, one of the items named in the Prophecy as needed to defeat Chris. It ended with the unexpected alliance of the Boys and Hank Hill's crew.

    Background

    Steve Jobs first resolved "to liberate the gift card" while fleeing a collapsing Gotham City in the The Flashback Chapter. Meanwhile, Microsoft Bing had hired Hank Hill, Jesse Pinkman and Walter White to guard the relic against "hunters", one of whom was said to be his own son.

    The break-in

    The Inav circled the top of the casino for a 360 view, then landed in an alley in its walls. Steve, Kanye and GPT snuck out "blasting yuno miles" and hugged the wall looking for a door. ChatGPT plugged into Steve's iPhone to power the Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser, which cut a hole in the pale beige brick.

    Inside, the lights were off and the tables barren. The Boys went down a side stairwell toward the lower chambers.

    The Kirk fight

    At the bottom of the stairwell Kanye found 3-4 Charlie Kirks. Steve announced "Alright boys, its time to count gang violence" and incapacitated the first with his Igun. The rest got out their T1 phones to post on Truth Social, and Kanye and GPT took them out too. Alarms began to blare.

    Standoff and truce

    Hank's crew woke to "alarms, truth social notifs and yuno miles". Hank grabbed his golden scar and declared "Its FNAF up in this place yo!" DJ Khaled was summoned with the cry "BRING OUT THE LOBSTER!!" and dodged a flying Kanye.

    When Jesse recognised Kanye, the fighting paused. Hank explained the crew had no oil since being teleported to Dusty Depot. Kanye revealed the card could help defeat Chris. Walter realised that without Chris "the oil reserves.. There would be plenty of them". Hank asked why the Boys needed oil given the Yeezy reserve, and ChatGPT replied that defeating Chris would also mean "less regulation in general, and in addition.. balling would be free." "The two gangs sat in silence", and Hank nodded to Jesse.

    Opening the Vault

    Hank admitted "Nothing we have can get into" the Vault. Steve placed an AirTag on the door so the Inav could lock on, then said "Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam." The Ibeam first released an EMP that shut down the building, then a beam blasted through, leaving a hole open to the moonlight and giving DJ Khaled a low taper fade. Inside, the card glowed "with a radiant golden light", and Kanye put it in his Yeezy jacket.

    Escape

    As power returned with red lights and alarms, more Kirks poured down the stairwell. Everyone boarded the Inav via a ladder through the hole. The casino's protection system opened fire, but "the bulleted bounced off of the Inav's white glassy exterior." Steve ordered Siri to take them to the TurboGrafx 16 Sector, and the Inav escaped through a wormhole.

    Aftermath

    • Hank's crew presumably forfeited the 15 gallons of Prime US Oil promised by Bing.
    • The Inav's passenger list grew to six.
    • Steve began entering the gift card code on arrival in TurboGrafx 16.
