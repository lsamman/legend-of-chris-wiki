    Darkworld State University

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations

    Darkworld State University
    TypeUniversity
    Notable alumniMF Vroom (Business), Spamton G. Spamton
    FraternitiesAlpha Mogger Beta
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Locations

    Darkworld State University is the university where MF Vroom earned his degree in business. It is also where he met his frat bestie Spamton G. Spamton:

    “Someone I met while I was getting my degree in business from Darkworld State University, he was my bestie from my days in a fraternity”

    The university's one known fraternity is Alpha Mogger Beta (or “something that sounded like that”). The frat ran a XP candy packaging operation that paid in kromer.

    Significance

    This passage is the only account of MF Vroom's education. Before it, readers knew him as a long-lost relative of the Projects royal family, an exile in the COD lobbies, and a car wearing a robe. The business degree may explain how he and Son later brought O'Block “to its glory”. This is a fan theory.

    Behind the scenes

    • The “Dark World” is the setting of Deltarune, the same game Spamton comes from.
    • Darkworld State is one of two universities in the canon. The other is Koolaid College, Osama's alma mater, which makes Osama and MF Vroom rivals from different schools as well as everything else.
