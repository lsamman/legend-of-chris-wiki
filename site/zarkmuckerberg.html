    Zarkmuckerberg

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Retired at 3. Irrelevant forever.

    Zarkmuckerberg
    CareerEnded at age 3
    Cause of retirementWill to live cancer
    Known to Mark ZuckerbergNo
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Characters

    Zarkmuckerberg is mentioned exactly once in the canon, at the end of the first section of Chapter 2, where he retires at the age of three “because of will to live cancer.” What he retired from is not stated.

    The book immediately adds that Mark Zuckerberg “did not know about this and the event was completely irrelevant to his life story,” making Zarkmuckerberg possibly the only figure in the canon formally confirmed to be irrelevant. Whether the two are related is unknown, though the names are clearly spoonerisms of each other.

    Trivia

    • His entry is followed by seven full stops, the longest ellipsis in Chapter 2.
    • Editors have repeatedly proposed deleting this page on grounds of irrelevance. The book itself is the main source for that irrelevance, and the page has survived every vote.
