    Factions & Organizations

    The Flip Phone Covenant is an organisation (or possibly a network) with which the iPhone 3G must sync in order to defeat Chris, according to Siri 3G at the iPhone 3G Keynote.

    In the story

    After the Casino Heist, Steve Jobs took the Inav to the TurboGrafx 16 sector: "We gotta sync with the flip phone covenant, and then we start with the social media apps.."

    The problem: "the current flip phone covenant uses 5g, and if we connect the iPhone to that.." This would wake Elon Mush's dormant twin (see 5G). Steve's plan is to bypass the G and roll back to 3G at the last moment. ChatGPT warned that this means dealing with Elon at the same time as Chris.

    Notes

    • The word "current" implies that there have been earlier covenants, presumably using older networks.
    • When Steve lifted the iPhone in Club Shadowban, "Apps began downloading themselves onto flip phones across the cosmos", which may have been the covenant's first contact.
    • A Covenant using 5G is, by Siri's own logic, already updated, which arguably breaks the "never update" rule.
    • Flip phones also appear in Chapter Triangle as the Nokia I-4500.
