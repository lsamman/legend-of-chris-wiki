    The Cringe Universe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · A universe of nazis. LeBron did not approve.

    The Cringe Universe is the name this wiki uses for the universe that Elon Mush accidentally created while building the Multiverse. It is described as a universe that was entirely nazis. (The book's exact wording is an offensive joke and is not reproduced here.) The name comes from LeBron James's assessment of it: “nah bro thats cringe.”

    History

    LeBron sent the Baller Army to “dab on the haters.” In the resulting attack, “over 10 casualties were found and every nazi was dabbed on.” Afterward, “in that universe was the great oppression where the america universe took over” (see The Great Oppression).

    Even after the Snap, its legacy continues: the Shadow Realm is a place where light does not exist and the nazi ants still exist.
