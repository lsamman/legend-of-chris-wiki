    Characters

    Mark Zuckerberg (also Mark Zucc or Zuck) is one of the four original sky-ballers alongside Chris, co-founder of Apple Podcasts, the author of the Snap that consigned nearly the whole Multiverse to the Shadow Realm, and co-commander of the Department of Defense. He is also, per Chapter Triangle, extremely chill.

    The Snap

    After the Multiverse Civil War ended with the bad ones winning, Zuckerberg “snapped his fingers and all the universes except for his fav were sent to the shadow realm, a realm where light doesnt exist.” His favourite universe is never identified.

    War for Byzantine Mexico

    In Chapter 2, Ron DeSantis resorts to Zuckerberg and Elon Musk to take back the Byzantine Empire of Mexico. When Medea interrupts the war, Zuckerberg, “outraged at this summoned jason from the same play,” which led directly to the Donkey Kong Racing Duel.

    Personality

    RFK considers recruiting him, reasoning that “his baller finger just was chill like that and lowkey did not care about anything and wanted you to chill on the porch with him and try some new cuban cigars be bought from eastern rome which he got from his cousin harry.” RFK ultimately decides “nah bro screw that” and goes for MF Vroom instead.

    It is unclear whether the “baller finger” is the same finger used in the Snap. Most editors assume yes.

    Department of Defense

    In Chapter the Fourth, the Department of Defense is described as “a army divided by Jeff bezosi and Mark zucc.” It is deployed against Hank Hill in the Kanye System.

    Later appearances

    • FS Martin Genius shows Kanye “an ancient gif of Zuck crying in a Chili's” and hands over the Zuck Tears (Do Not Install) USB stick, which may have sent Club Shadowban to the Shadow Realm. The Snap's author is now apparently also its fuel.
    • At the iPhone 3G Keynote, Steve Jobs wears “a black turtleneck woven from Mark Zuckerberg's abandoned dreams.”
    • The Prophecy names him among those who will fall with Chris.

    Trivia

    • The Snap is a reference to Thanos's finger snap in Avengers: Infinity War.
    • Zuckerberg did not know about Zarkmuckerberg's retirement, and the event was “completely irrelevant to his life story.”
