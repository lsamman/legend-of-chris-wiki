    Chapters

    chapter the maybe seventh one if you can still count idk tho, or Chapter the Maybe Seventh, is the seventh chapter of The Legend of Chris. It contains the Casino Heist, in which Steve Jobs, Kanye West and ChatGPT break into the casino to steal the Verizon contract, only to find Hank Hill's gang guarding it. The two gangs join forces against Chris.

    Synopsis

    Arrival

    The Inav exits the wormhole at a casino "taller than the wideness of the tall." Jobs welcomes "the gangity gang" to Sector 1/99, the Gambling Addiction Sector, former home of Elon Mush, Frodo Baggins and D. Piddy, and reveals the Verizon contract is inside; recovering it begins "the journey to the court."

    The break-in

    The Boys ("not from the the hit amazon prime show") sneak out blasting Yuno Miles. ChatGPT explains Yuno became immortal after the Gawk-ination of 2039 and made the first verse-wide tour; Kanye replies "The fuck does that mean Kobe Bryant." Jobs, sourcing gadgets locally since Tim Apple retired, cuts in with the Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser. Downstairs Kanye finds 3-4 Charlie Kirks. Jobs takes one down with 37 shots from his Igun; the others pull out T1 phones and post on Truth Social before Kanye and ChatGPT take them out. Alarms blare.

    Two gangs

    Hank, Jesse and Walter wake. Walter charges with "67 ioann rizz," but someone shouts "BRING OUT THE LOBSTER!!" and DJ Khaled leaps out with a DJ set. Kanye lunges and misses. Jesse recognizes Ye, and the fighting stops. Hank explains they have been oil-broke since teleporting into Dusty Depot; Walter realizes Chris's defeat would free the oil reserves, and ChatGPT adds that "balling would be free." Hank asks how they'd open the vault.

    The Ibeam

    Jobs places an AirTag on the vault and says "Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam." An EMP darkens the floor, DJ Khaled receives a "huge massive low taper fade," and the beam blasts the door. Jobs compares it to the iPod Touch, which "cooked eggs while playing geometry dash." Kanye stores the glowing contract in his Yeezy jacket, and everyone escapes in the Inav as bullets bounce off its glass.

    TurboGrafx 16

    Siri teleports them to the TurboGrafx 16 Sector to sync with the Flip Phone Covenant. The Covenant uses 5G, which would summon Elon Mush, so Jobs plans to roll back to 3G only when "right in Chris's face." ChatGPT warns they'd then face Elon fresh from the Shadow Realm. "Its not like we have a choice GPT."

    Title & numbering

    The title openly admits the authors have lost count ("if you can still count idk tho"). It is, in fact, the seventh chapter, making it the only chapter whose number is correct but whose title doubts it.

    Continuity notes

    • Steve Jobs lists Elon Mush as a former resident of Sector 1/99 despite his death of NFL poisoning in Chris Origins. Mush's "dormant twin" and possible Shadow Realm return are also discussed.
    • Frodo, who "left the face of the universe," also apparently lived here.
    • A hand is placed on "Mr.whites shoulder" with the words "Hank let the security guard deal with this"; who is speaking to whom remains unclear.
    • ChatGPT tells Hank the gang has oil "in the Yeezy reserve."

    Trivia

    • "Bring out the lobster" references a viral DJ Khaled clip.
    • FNAF is Five Nights at Freddy's, a horror game about a night security guard.
    • The TurboGrafx-16 is a 1980s video game console.
