    Vladimir Putin

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Almost in the center. Coffee enjoyer.

    Vladimir Putin
    LocationConstantinople (almost the center)
    Known associatesMr. Bean (coffee)
    ViewsMental illness is a myth
    StatusUnknown
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Characters

    Vladimir Putin appears during the Fall of Constantinople. He is "almost in the center" of the city, which is exactly where the plan needed its target to be. The plan's target, however, was Barack Obama.

    Role

    Mr. Bean spent part of the mission with Putin, who was "lecturing me about how Mental illness in a myth created my Mormon progressives in various governments in the universes". The relationship seems to have warmed: Bean later reports "we're having coffee just waiting on you". When McQueen was ready, Bean said a polite goodbye and promised to see Putin later.

    Whether Putin was still near the center when the city fell is not recorded. Bean's "we'll see you later" has been called the most ominous line in the chapter.

    Continuity errors

    Putin's presence at the center, where the plan said Obamna would be, is one of the best-known continuity problems in the canon. See Barack Obama.
