    The DQ–McDonald's MySpace Beef is a feud between DQ and McDonald's ("Mac Donald's") that took place on MySpace. It began after Moscow hosted the September 2002 McDonald's Wine Brewing contest, when "DQ starting beef on MySpace with Mac Donald's".

    Contents
    1. Escalation
    2. Status
    3. Trivia

    Escalation

    The beef was rated "C tier" by the narrator, which in most traditions would make it ignorable. But Osama Bin Bom'in, Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire, as Keepers of the MySpace Relics, felt responsible for the platform. Their solution was to bomb Moscow, the host city, "to shut McDonald's up and let DQ win already."

    This makes the relic keepers neutral moderators who openly picked a side.

    Status

    Unresolved. The book never confirms whether DQ won.

    Trivia

    • This is the earliest recorded online beef in the canon, though Lil Jokez's SoundCloud diss tracks later rival it.
    • The beef is called "C tier". The wiki has been unable to find the A or B tier beefs.
