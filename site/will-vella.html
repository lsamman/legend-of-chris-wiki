    Will Vella is the colorful figure Vinny B. James meets in the Dunkin in Wyoming. Wearing a United Kingdom flag as a cape, Will is furious that there are no England-style donuts and announces a plan to colonize every coffee shop.

    At the Dunkin

    Will demands England-style donuts, and the Starwalker manager answers, "This is America." Will says, "You're getting colonized." Will vows that when Dunkin is taken over, every coffee shop will be colonized, and shouts "DO NOT QUESTION THE BAGEL MAN!!!!!" at the manager's question ("But aren't you no-"). Will's goal is to "create my Dunkin Cult" through Vellatosis, with an "army of Vella's".

    The deal

    Vinny offers to help find the secret of Vellatosis, which he calls "a game theory", if Will helps defeat Claude, King of AI. Will agrees: "Sounds baller." Will then sits at the table with Aesop Rock and Del (The Dunkin Alliance).

    On the road

    After the crash, Will produces the iPhone 3G and the Scott the Woz tutorial, and reports the time as "2:24am UNC standard time". At Buc-ee's HQ Will "sheds a tear because Will is British". Kilroy reveals that the UHA already knows Will, and Will's plan to find the MacBook Charger. On the road trip, "even though Will was British, Will still felt free because America runs on Dunkin."

    Vellatosis

    Beaten after six hours in Louisiana, Will stands up and asks Claude, King of AI: "Before you kill us. How does one achieve Vellatosis?" The U.K. flag flies off Will's back. Claude explains, and Will splits into 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 Vellas "and so forth" (The Day of the Bagel Man). The Bagel Man puts on a white cloak with the Dunkin Donuts logo, leads the Vellas against Claude and the data centers, and flies off in the Dunkin Cult Cruiser (The Departure of the Dunkin Cult). "They will return again."

    Trivia

    • The narration never uses "he" or "she" for Will. The cloak is "their cloak".
