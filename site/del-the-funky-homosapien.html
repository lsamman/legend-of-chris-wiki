    Del the Funky Homosapien ("Visually Del from Gorillaz") is a rapper from the real world who appears at the Dunkin in Chapter 3: The Age of Claude with Aesop Rock. He is the one who translates Aesop's lines for Vinny.

    Contents
    1. Appearances
    2. The rest of Chapter 3
    3. Trivia
    4. On the spin-off wiki

    Appearances

    The pair are writing a diss track about Wordle. Del's lyrics include, "Yeah, Yeah, you know what time it is, Me and Aesop dunking inside Dunkin on this piece of shit game," and "Wordle is the name and it doesn't have enough shame." He also asks what Vinny B. James has to do with them, and agrees to help once he hears that Claude, King of AI likes Wordle.

    The rest of Chapter 3

    Del stole the Mystery Machine "for literally no reason", smoked weed on the drive and crashed it (The Mystery Machine Crash): "How can you blame me, Vin? That weed was absolute." He missed the teleportation tutorial the first time, so it was played again. He fights in the Battle of Louisiana and is back in Wyoming by Chapter 4: The Burger King Dark World.

    Trivia

    • Del is a real person. The character here is a fictional cameo in a fan story.
