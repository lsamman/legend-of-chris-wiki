    Pog / Poggers / Pogchamp

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore

    Pog / Poggers / Pogchamp
    TypeInterjection / standard of quality
    VariantsPog, poggers, pogchamp, dope pog
    UsersRFK, ChatGPT, Plankton, Son, Triple T
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Concepts & Lore

    Pog, along with poggers and pogchamp, is the canon's main measure of approval. Something either is pog or, more often, is not pog.

    Contents
    1. Recorded uses
    2. Analysis
    3. Trivia

    Recorded uses

    • “why is RFK how is RFK, many are not pog like RFK” — RFK, about himself, Chapter Triangle.
    • “What?! That is not poggers at all dude” — said in reply to RFK's disturbance in the Fabric in the Protestant Universe, Dunder Mifflan.
    • “Honestly this is not pogchamp lets be real” — ChatGPT, looking at the abandoned Club Shadowban, The Flashback Chapter.
    • “This turn will align us with the projects national spaceport, pretty dope pog if you ask me” — Plankton, Chapter 4+.1+.1….
    • “MF Vroom.. it is not very poggers for you to come back after you were explicitly sent into exile” — Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Chapter 4+.1+.1….
    • “If I were there everything would be different pogchamp.” — Son, on the coup, Chapter 4+.1+.1….

    Analysis

    Four of the six uses are negative. “Pretty dope pog” is the only unreserved positive, and it is about a turn. Son's “pogchamp” comes at the end of a sentence where it has no grammatical role.

    Trivia

    • “PogChamp” is a Twitch emote of excitement. “Pog” and “poggers” come from it.
