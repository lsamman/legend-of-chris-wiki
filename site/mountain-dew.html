    Mountain Dew

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Super human, or super dead.

    Mountain Dew
    TypeOrganic substance (soft drink)
    VarietiesRegular, Baja Blast
    PropertiesCan make you "super human, or super dead"
    Found inPipes of the 360 Chambers; rain at COD HQ; the Quick-Check
    Related venturesErger Wings
    First appearanceThe Flashback Chapter
    Items & Technology

    Mountain Dew (also "Dew", "mountain dew") is a green liquid that shows up throughout the canon as a drink, a weather event, an infrastructure fluid and a business plan. One character calls it "An organic substance that has the power to make you super human, or super dead", which makes it one of the most dangerous items in the multiverse.

    Contents
    1. Appearances
    2. Properties
    3. Trivia

    Appearances

    Steve Jobs' keynote

    At the end of the iPhone 3G Keynote, "The stage collapses into a pool of Mountain Dew Baja Blast" and Steve Jobs ascends into a hologram.

    The wormhole

    The Inav's trip to the casino goes through "a tunnel of Doritos and mountain dew ads, xbox live cod clip comps and skrilla".

    Dusty Depot

    Jesse Pinkman comes back from the Quick-Check through the Port-a-Potty Portal "with 2 mountain dews and a pack of zyns". Walter White then shares his retirement dream: "I would like to open up a mountain dew refinery and restaurant here, we'll make it a chain.. call it erger wings.."

    The 360 Chambers

    The walls of the 360 Chambers are "filled with translucent pipes filled with a green liquid", which is identified as Mountain Dew. The exit door is "smothered in Dorito dust and mountain dew oil", and outside it is "absolutely raining pepsi and dew".

    Properties

    • Biology: classified as "organic".
    • Effects: either super human or super dead, with no known middle outcome.
    • Refining: Walter White wants to refine it, so it may occur naturally in crude form, possibly alongside Oil.
    • Oil: it produces "mountain dew oil", a residue.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: Mountain Dew is a PepsiCo soft drink, and Baja Blast is a flavor originally sold only at Taco Bell. Mountain Dew and Doritos together are the stereotypical gamer diet, which explains why they are all over Call of Duty HQ.
    • The rain at COD HQ is a mix of Pepsi and Dew, both PepsiCo products. Editors suspect corporate sponsorship of the weather.
