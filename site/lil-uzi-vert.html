    Lil Uzi Vert

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · A concert with consequences.

    Lil Uzi Vert
    Also spelledlilt uzi vert
    OccupationRapper (real-world)
    Canon roleConcert venue of tragedy
    RelatedRFK
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Characters

    Lil Uzi Vert is a real-world rapper who is mentioned exactly once in the canon, spelled "lilt uzi vert". After defeating Bon Jovi, RFK stands on the senate floor "remembering his adopted father and mother that died earlier that day at a lilt uzi vert concert."

    Lil Uzi Vert does not appear in person and is not blamed for anything. The tragedy is part of RFK's "cool sad backstory", and is notable for happening on the same day as the Siege of the Galactic Senate, making it a very busy day for RFK.

    Trivia

    • RFK has adopted parents, and Plankton is an orphan. Their partnership is sometimes called the Orphan Alliance by fans.
