    The Reformed Travis Scott Sector

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Last known McDouble-purchase location of Chris.

    TypeSector of space
    ContainsThe Kanye System

    Notable sightingChris, buying a McDouble
    First mentionedChapter Triangle
    Locations

    The Reformed Travis Scott Sector is a region of space where Plankton's sources have picked up "traces" of Chris. According to Plankton, "it will be hard to find exactly which planet in this system it is buying a mcdouble from however with the help of some former friends it should be possible."

    Security

    In Chapter the Fourth, the Department of Defense, an army "divided by Jeff bezosi and Mark zucc", is said to have been "created to contain damn level threats in the reformed Travis Scott sector". The DOD had recently been sent to the Kanye System, which is inside the sector, to contain Hank Hill and his Propane empire.

    This means a Chris sighting and the Hank Hill propane crisis happened in the same sector, though the canon does not say they are connected.

    Trivia

    • The sector being "Reformed" suggests there was once an unreformed Travis Scott Sector. Its history is not recorded.
    • Real-world: Travis Scott is a rapper, and the McDouble is a McDonald's burger. Travis Scott famously had a McDonald's meal promotion in 2020, so the McDouble sighting is probably not a coincidence.
    • Plankton calls it a "sector" and a "system" in the same sentence.
