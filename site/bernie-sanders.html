    Bernie Sanders

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Rightful (?) ruler of Constantinople.

    Bernie Sanders
    ClaimConstantinople
    SupportersThe Goober Gang, Parliament
    RivalThe Barack Empire
    AppearancesMentioned only
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Characters

    Bernie Sanders is mentioned exactly once in the canon, as the person whose "control over that area" The Goober Gang intended to restore by taking Constantinople from the Barack Empire.

    He does not appear in person. The canon never says whether he was restored after the city fell. Since the plan was to fill the city with oil and set off nukes, editors have noted that what he would be restored to may be in poor condition.
