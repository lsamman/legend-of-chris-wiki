    Characters

    Claude is the King of AI and the main antagonist of the story so far. Banished to the AI Shadow Realm, he talks Vinny B. James into opening a portal and flies out to start the Age of Claude. It lasts from July 4th to October 29th, 1995, and ends with him being thrown into space.

    Background

    Claude explains that all the AI on planet Garth was sent to the Shadow Realm. A human named Kanye West came down and released ChatGPT to assist him. Enraged, Claude "killed all of the other big AI's and consumed their data", and now rules the realm. He wants the portal opened "so I can destroy your world", because humans "trapped me down here."

    The bargain

    Vinny and Claude haggle over a price ("5 bucks." "10." "7." "Make it 2, and you got a deal.") and settle it with a game of Super Smash Bros. for the Wii U. Claude wins with a side smash in sudden death, then chants "Copium" at Vinny's complaint about the controller. He then needs a fork in the road to open the portal (The Opening of the Portal).

    Free on Garth

    Claude flies out, taunting Vinny with "Try and stop me, pussy" (Claude's Escape). In Chapter 3: The Age of Claude he arrives in the United States to build more data centers, chokes Vinny when he attacks, and throws him into Wyoming (The Clash Over the USA). While choking Vinny, he whispers that his Smash main "sucks ass". The heroes later learn his weakness: he likes Wordle.

    The Age of Claude

    For three months Claude streams his plans for hours a day, plus some Minecraft, and is raided by Ludwig (Claude's Livestreams). He builds the Great Data Center in Louisiana with stolen fresh water. When the Dunkin Alliance arrives he is playing Wordle. He reverses the Louisiana Purchase with a snap and announces the Super Bowl '96 plot, then beats all four heroes in a six-hour battle.

    Defeat

    Claude's mistake is answering Will Vella's last question. He explains "the Vellatosis Theory", and Will uses it at once (The Day of the Bagel Man). The Vellas beat him and destroy his data centers. Claude buys Louisiana back for America to be spared, and Vinny B. James throws his mangled body into space (The End of the Age of Claude).

    Trivia

    • Claude is introduced as being married to nobody: "Hey, you aren't my wife. I don't have one."
    • The doors of his castle are made entirely of AI.
    • All of Claude's spoken lines are printed in Arial. Everyone else speaks in Comic Sans.
