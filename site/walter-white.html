    Walter White

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Dreamer of Erger Wings.

    Walter White

    Characters

    Walter White, usually called Mr. White, is the other half of "the wrath of Jesse and white". He co-pilots the crack van with Jesse Pinkman and is a member of Hank Hill's crew.

    Contents
    1. Biography
    2. Continuity errors
    3. Trivia

    Biography

    After rescuing Hank, White asked about "getting the ingredients from Kanye" and pointed out that "Kanye only came out during the Super Bowl". This led to the house break-in of the Weezing of Kanye.

    In Dusty Depot he sat in a lawn chair "looking up into the trees" and talked about his retirement dream, Erger Wings. He was first through the portal, and once inside the 6 7 Casino he gave the order: "Indeed Jesse, quick to the roulette table!"

    During the heist, Mr. White ran toward the gunfire "with determination in his eyes" and "67 ioann rizz in his eyes" before a hand on his shoulder stopped him. He was the first to see why defeating Chris mattered: "Hank.. if Chris was defeated then the oil reserves.."

    Continuity errors

    The hand on Mr. White's shoulder comes with the line "Hank let the security guard deal with this", even though the hand was on White, not Hank.

    Trivia

    • Walter White is the protagonist of Breaking Bad, a chemistry teacher turned drug manufacturer. His wish for a "refinery" fits the character.
    • "6 7" is a meme that also names the casino.
