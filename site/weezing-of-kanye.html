    The Weezing of Kanye

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · This isn't over, Kanye.

    The Weezing of Kanye

    TargetKanye West

    ChapterChapter the Fourth
    Events

    The Weezing of Kanye was a raid on Kanye West's house by Hank Hill and his crew to get "the ingredients from Kanye".

    Contents
    1. Background
    2. The event
    3. Aftermath

    Background

    Hank's first plan used social media, but it hit a snag: "our twitter account doesn't have 100 followers yet and we aren't verified yet". Since contacting Kanye online wasn't possible, Hank decided to "find him and weeze him". White pointed out that "Kanye only came out during the Super Bowl", so Hank replied "We're gonna have to break into his house". White said: "ok".

    The event

    The whole event is told in one sentence: "And so they broke into his house and weezed him." What weezing is has never been defined, and editors have chosen not to guess.

    Afterward, the crew found that "the propane was watered down". Hank mourned the barbecue bros, car enthusiasts and arsonists who had been deceived, and swore: "This isn't over kanye"…

    Aftermath

    Kanye seems unaffected, since he is promoting Ye the Gas in the very next scene. When Kanye and Hank meet again during the casino heist, Kanye says "Hank I know we've had our differences". This is generally taken to refer to the weezing.
