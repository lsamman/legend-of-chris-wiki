    Chapters

    Chapter Triangle is the third chapter of The Legend of Chris. It resolves the Siege of the Galactic Senate in a single sentence and launches the book's two main quests: RFK and Plankton's mission to recruit MF Vroom for the New Crips, and Lightning McQueen's recruitment into a plot to seize Constantinople.

    It is also the chapter that first reveals Chris's full name and title: Chris: All Balled Up Deluxe Edition.

    Synopsis

    RFK and Plankton

    "Bon Jovi Died at the hands of RFK." RFK ponders his existence ("why is RFK how is RFK, many are not pog like RFK") and remembers his adoptive parents, who died that day at a Lil Uzi Vert concert. He leaves the senate, buys a G Fuel from a gang-affiliated corner store in Chai Town, and is approached by Plankton, who knows of his membership in the Lost Society of Ballers.

    Plankton speaks of a man "so infamous that almost no one dares to utter his name": Chris: All Balled Up Deluxe Edition. RFK has not heard the name "since operation desert storm aired on cartoon network." Plankton explains that after Chris's war crimes in the senate his body was cloned and thrown into a pit thought to have no end, but his soul stayed in the original because the All-Knowing Overseers declared it "the less wack one." Since the housing market crash of 2007/2008 he has been lost, with traces in the Reformed Travis Scott Sector.

    Only one who has met the Tech Bros and downloaded the sacred apps onto a Nokia I-4500 flip phone with a 3 year contract plan can fight him. After briefly considering Mark Zuckerberg (and his cousin Harry, who smuggles meth for Roman emperors), RFK decides to enlist MF Vroom, who is held at Call of Duty HQ deep in Dunder Mifflin. "Aight Bet", says Plankton.

    McQueen goes to England

    Sick of George Constanta, McQueen flies to England, having received a pager instructing him to hoard smoke detectors for Parliament. At the smoke and vape shop, Mr. Bean briefs him: take Constantinople from the Barack Empire and restore Bernie Sanders. The plan to defeat Barack Obama, holder of the 7 Chaos Emeralds, is to plant nukes, flood the city with Oil, lure "Obamna" to the center, and escape through a wormhole that may or may not exist. Agent 007 emerges from a vape-filled telephone booth, approves of McQueen's "work in Moscow," and the trio leave in the Tesla-sponsored motorcade from E.C.S.M Airport.

    Title & numbering

    The title is printed on two lines, "Chapter / Triangle". As the third chapter, it is generally accepted that the triangle stands for the number three, making this the first chapter to be numbered geometrically. It is the last chapter whose title fits on two lines.

    Continuity notes

    • McQueen is said to be "in the Soviet Union" at the start of his storyline, though he moved in with George Constanta after the Attack on Moscow, which is in Italy.
    • The target is Barack Obama here; by Chapter the Fourth the man in the center of Constantinople is Vladimir Putin.
    • England is described as having reached "a new level of Roman."

    Trivia

    • This chapter coins the term New Crips, of which MF Vroom is to be "the first member."
    • Mr. Bean wears "a set of brand new Kanye west yeezes," the first mention of Kanye West in the canon.
    • The 7 Chaos Emeralds come from the Sonic the Hedgehog series.
