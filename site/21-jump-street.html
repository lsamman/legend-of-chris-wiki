    21 Jump Street

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Site of the only cucumber homicide in the canon.

    21 Jump Street
    TypeStreet
    Notable eventDeath of Larry the Cucumber
    See also8aller Jump Street
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Locations

    21 Jump Street is where Larry the Cucumber "got killed", as reported in a parenthetical in the Dunder Mifflan chapter. Nothing else is known about the street, the killing, or which universe it happened in.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: 21 Jump Street is a TV police drama (1987–1991) that was remade as comedy films in 2012.
    • It is one of two Jump Streets in the canon. The other is 8aller Jump Street in Constantinople, where Mr. Bean and 007 wait to be picked up. Whether the 13-number gap is meaningful is not known.
