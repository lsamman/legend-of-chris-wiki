    Wormholes

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · Theoretically could open. Usually does.

    Wormholes
    TypeInterdimensional transit
    Known usersThe Goober Gang, Steve Jobs, the Inav
    Typical decorDoritos and Mountain Dew ads, COD clip comps, skrilla
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Concepts & Lore

    Wormholes are the main way characters travel between places, sectors and universes in the canon. Unlike hyperspace (used by the crack van and the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser), wormholes tend to open at dramatically convenient moments.

    Known wormholes

    The Constantinople wormhole

    Mr. Bean's escape plan used "a wormhole that theroreticly could open there but we have Basiclly no clue if It'll actually happen", with only 6.8 seconds to get out. It did open, "just north of Dababy's last known location", and The Goober Gang went into it and has not been heard from since.

    The Keynote wormhole

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, light off Steve Jobs's glasses bounced "into a wormhole" and blinded an NFT collector in Universe Prime.

    The Multiverse wormhole

    When 808s and Apocalypses dropped, "across all multiverses, a wormhole opened at once". Out of it flew the iPhone 3G with Steve Jobs standing on top of it.

    Inav tunnels

    The Inav travels through wormholes at Siri's command. Inside, the Inav "was surrounded in a tunnel of Doritos and mountain dew ads, xbox live cod clip comps and skrilla" (ChatGPT: "Woah.. this is baller man im deadass"). The Inav also left the casino through one on the way to the TurboGrafx 16 Sector, "with a rumble and a high frequency siren".

    Trivia

    • Wormholes are different from portals. The canon also has the Meme Portal and the Port-a-Potty Portal.
    • The Inav "burst out of the wormhole" at the casino, and "a giant rift opened in the sky" before that, so rifts may be the exits of wormholes.
