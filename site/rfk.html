    RFK (Robert F. Kennedy Jr.)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Dark Knight. Last senator. Drinker of G Fuel. Licker of rain.

    RFK (Robert F. Kennedy Jr.)
    Also calledRFK Jr., Ronald F Kennedy Jr. (by MF Vroom)
    AffiliationLost Society of Ballers, New Crips
    PartnerPlankton
    WeaponsLightsaber, Hantavirus vaccine, 4 Red Bulls
    DrinkG Fuel
    FamilyAdopted parents (deceased)
    StatusAlive
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Characters

    RFK is one of the main protagonists of The Legend of Chris. Introduced as "a Dark Knight" and the last remaining member of the Galactic Senate, he defeats Bon Jovi, is recruited by Plankton to stop Chris, and decides that the way to do it is to rescue MF Vroom. He shares a name with the American political figure Robert F. Kennedy Jr.; this article covers only what the canon says.

    Biography

    The Galactic Senate

    In Chapter 2, after Bon Jovi pushes out "all of the 80 year old senators", "one remained a Dark Knight named RFK. RFK used ancient battle tactics of the Jedi". He declares, "It's over Bon Jovi I have 4 Red Bulls and a lightsaber, no one can mess with me I'm cool", which leads to the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate. Chapter Triangle opens with "Bon Jovi Died at the hands of RFK."

    A cool sad backstory

    RFK then "pondered his existence" on the senate floor, "remembering his adopted father and mother that died earlier that day at a lilt uzi vert concert." He left the senate and bought G Fuel from "his favorite corner store that probably was involved with several gangs on the streets of Chai Town."

    Meeting Plankton

    Plankton found him and asked, "Are you RFK, part of the lost society of ballers?" After learning about Chris, RFK says, "I haven't heard that name since operation desert storm aired on cartoon network". He briefly considers Mark Zuckerberg as an ally, in a long tangent about cigars and Zuckerberg's cousin Harry, before deciding, "nah bro screw that we're gonna enlist MF Vroom."

    Dunder Mifflin

    RFK and Plankton reach Dunder Mifflin in their Mercedes Benz System Cruiser ("Dear god.. its huge there's so much.. paper.."). At Call of Duty HQ, RFK announces, "I felt a disturbance in the Fabric in the Protestant universe" (see The Protestant Universe), and greets the battle inside with "What's up my meninges!"

    Freeing MF Vroom

    In the 360 chambers, "RFK drew his hantavirus vaccine" when ambushed by what turned out to be MF Vroom. Outside in the rain, he warns that they might catch various things "if we stay out in the cold moist rain" while he "licks the rain off of his arm aggressively."

    The Projects

    On the flight home, he explains the mission to MF Vroom. During the car chase through the Projects, he asks whether there is roadkill, as he needs a raccoon part "for my next dish", and Son asks why he wants "to cook up some new Kennedy food" in the middle of a police chase. At the end of the book, he sums up the TON 618 situation: "W-well shoot".

    Personality

    RFK refers to himself in the third person in moments of reflection, and is easily impressed ("Cool", "Baller, so theres only one then?"). In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, he begins to stutter ("t-t-t-this is really cool guys", "N-n-now mister Vroom", "Who was that-t?", "I-it is extremely important"). The cause is unknown; some fans blame the rain.

    Equipment

    • 4 Red Bulls and a lightsaber, his arsenal against Bon Jovi.
    • A hantavirus vaccine, drawn as a weapon in the 360 chambers.
    • A modded nerf gun, retrieved from the cruiser at the spaceport.
    • G Fuel, which he drinks on the way to his destiny.

    Trivia

    • RFK and Plankton are both effectively orphans: RFK lost his adopted parents, and Plankton has "no last name" because he is one.
    • MF Vroom calls him "Ronald F Kennedy Jr.", and he does not correct him.
    • He is a "Dark Knight" in a canon that also contains Drake Batman. No connection has been established.
    • His "disturbance in the Fabric" line is later echoed by Steve Jobs's "disturbance in the iCloud".
