    8aller Jump Street

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Not to be confused with 21 Jump Street.

    8aller Jump Street
    LocationConstantinople
    UseExtraction point
    Notable visitorsMr. Bean, Agent 007, McQueen
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Locations

    8aller Jump Street is the pickup point in Constantinople where Mr. Bean and Agent 007 waited for Lightning McQueen during the Fall of Constantinople ("Ok McQueen we're at 8aller jump street").

    Most scholars read "8aller" as "Baller" spelled with an 8. The street shares a naming convention with 21 Jump Street, where Larry the Cucumber was killed in a later chapter. Whether the two streets are connected is a subject of fan debate.
