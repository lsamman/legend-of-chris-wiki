    The Pager

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Hoard smoke detectors. Don't ask why.

    The Pager
    RecipientLightning McQueen
    SourceA stranger on the street
    MessageHoard smoke detectors for Parliament
    Related itemsBags of old pagers (nuke containers)
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Items & Technology

    The Pager is a device that Lightning McQueen "recivied... from a stranger on the street". It "instructed him to hoard smoke detectors for parliament because of 'special reasons'". No smoke detectors are hoarded anywhere in the canon, and the special reasons are never revealed.

    Pagers in Constantinople

    Pagers come back in the Fall of Constantinople. Mr. Bean and 007 "deployed the first 2 nukes inside various bags of old pagers." Whether these pagers include the original one, or were bought in bulk, is unknown.

    Trivia

    • Pagers are one of several outdated devices that matter in the canon, alongside the Nokia I-4500 flip phone and the iPhone 3G.
