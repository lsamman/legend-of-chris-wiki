    The TurboGrafx 16 Sector

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Sixteen bits of destiny.

    The TurboGrafx 16 Sector
    TypeSector
    InhabitantsThe Flip Phone Covenant
    Reached byThe Inav (teleport via wormhole)
    Purpose of visitSync the iPhone 3G with the Covenant
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Locations

    The TurboGrafx 16 Sector (spelled "Turbo Graphx 16" in the text) is where the Inav went after the Casino Heist. Steve Jobs ordered: "Hey Siri... Take us to.. The Turbo Graphx 16 sector." Siri replied "Teleporting sir," and the Inav entered it "with a rumble and a high frequency siren."

    Notes

    • A crew member, usually taken to be Kanye, asked "Why 16 Steve? I haven't been there in years." Steve explained that they had to sync with the Flip Phone Covenant, which now uses 5G.
    • The TurboGrafx-16 was a real 1980s video game console from NEC and Hudson Soft, which fits the canon's preference for legacy technology.
    • The sector is the last known location of Steve, Kanye and ChatGPT at the end of the canon.
