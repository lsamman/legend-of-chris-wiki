    DaBaby

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Vaporized, suspended, a landmark.

    DaBaby
    LocationConstantinople
    ConditionVaporized, in a suspended animation cell
    UseNavigational landmark
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Characters

    DaBaby is a rapper who appears in the canon only as a landmark. When McQueen asked where the wormhole was, the answer was "just north of Dababy's last known location". The Goober Gang then "flew by the seen of vaporized dababy in a suspended animation cell and entered the wormhole".

    How DaBaby came to be both vaporized and in suspended animation is never explained. Editors have pointed out that being vaporized would normally make suspended animation pointless.
