    The Pit With No End

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Thought to have no end. Unconfirmed.

    The Pit With No End
    TypePit (bottomless, reportedly)
    ContentsChris's cloned body (or bodies)
    DepthThought to be infinite
    First mentionedChapter Triangle
    Locations

    The Pit With No End is the place where Chris's body was thrown after his war crimes in the Galactic Senate. According to Plankton, the body "was thought to be clones and thrown into a pit thought to have no end to it."

    The pit is mentioned exactly once, and the wording is hedged twice: the body was only thought to be clones, and the pit is only thought to have no end. No one in the canon has checked either claim.

    Significance

    Because Chris's soul stayed in his first body by ruling of the All-Knowing Overseers, the pit apparently holds only the "more wack" body or bodies. Editors therefore list it as soulless.

    Fan theory: If the pit does have an end, a Chris clone is at the bottom of it. This has never been suggested in the canon and is included only because someone was going to say it.
