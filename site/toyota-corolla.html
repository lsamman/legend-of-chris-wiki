    The Toyota Corolla Question

    The Toyota Corolla Question
    Raised byOsama, Steve, Quagmire
    AnswerLightning McQueen
    Related vehiclesCamry, 2005 Honda Accord, 2008 Honda Accord, Prius
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Concepts & Lore

    The Toyota Corolla Question is the central problem of the Attack on Moscow summit: "they discussed which Toyota Corolla should be responsible for the intital bombing run." The answer: "The Toyota Corolla to do the initial bombing run was to be lightning McQueen".

    Contents

    Is McQueen a Corolla?

    In the real world, Lightning McQueen from Pixar's Cars is a custom stock car, not a Toyota. The canon is firm, though, and the wiki follows the canon. The question of how many Toyota Corollas Osama had to choose from is not answered.

    Later vehicle confusion

    The canon's car problems continue:

    • In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, Plankton yells at someone aboard the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser: "Bro lowkey why r u built like a 2008 Honda accord Zyn weed addition". The subject is presumably MF Vroom, a car.
    • Later, the heroes escape in Son's 2005 Honda Accord.
    • TON 618 then scans the city "looking for the Camry". The heroes are in a Honda Accord.

    So the canon's major vehicles have been described as a Toyota Corolla, a Camry, a 2005 Honda Accord and a 2008 Honda Accord, and no two of these are the same car. Fans call it the Toyota Corolla Question because it has never been answered.

    Fan theories

    • TON 618 is just bad at cars: the Terrible Offensive Nuhuh may have been built without a car-recognition module.
    • All cars are McQueen: not taken seriously.
