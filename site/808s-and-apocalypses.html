    ***808s and Apocalypses*** is the mixtape of Kanye West and ChatGPT, dropped in Club Shadowban to counter Lil Jokez's Laugh.exe. It is the only mixtape in the canon confirmed to have cosmological consequences.

    Contents
    1. History
    2. The drop
    3. Trivia

    History

    Kanye declared: "ChatGPT, it's time to drop our mixtape." ChatGPT replied: "Yes sir. Initializing 808s and Apocalypses."

    Known verses, performed at the Battle of Club Shadowban:

    I'm Ye the Gas, no watered-down trash, My bars melt steel beams, while you still DoorDash.
    ChatGPT online, rhymes divine, I code your whole verse in assembly line.

    The drop

    After the battle there was silence, then: "The mixtape… has dropped." Suddenly "across all multiverses, a wormhole opened at once", out of which came the iPhone 3G carrying Steve Jobs, who said "Ye. GPT. I heard the drop."

    This fulfils the first instruction of Siri 3G at the iPhone 3G Keynote: "Begin the Mixtape." Kanye and GPT appear to have begun it without knowing about the instruction.

    Trivia

    • The title parodies Kanye West's real 2008 album 808s & Heartbreak.
    • "Ye the Gas" refers to Kanye's propane brand.
