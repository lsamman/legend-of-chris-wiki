    Elon Musk

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Tech bro, conqueror of two universes, half of Samwise.

    Elon Musk
    AffiliationThe Tech Bros
    Co-foundedApple Podcasts
    RelativesElon Mush (secret cousin)
    FateMerged with LeBron James into Samwise
    Notable warsMultiverse Civil War; War for Byzantine Mexico
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Characters

    Elon Musk is one of the original tech bros who balled into the sky with Chris, a co-founder of Apple Podcasts, a conqueror of “over 2 universes,” and one half of Samwise. He should not be confused with his secret cousin Elon Mush, although the book frequently makes this difficult.

    Contents
    1. In the Cycle of Chris
    2. Chapter 2
    3. Later references
    4. Continuity errors

    In the Cycle of Chris

    Musk joins Chris, LeBron James and Mark Zuckerberg in the sky to learn the secret teachings of social media. Later, when the America universe “yerned for the minor miner universe because it was funny,” it was “together with elon musk” that “they took over over 2 universes after which a civil war broke out and then the bad ones won.” (See The Multiverse Civil War.)

    After the Great Famine and the death of his cousin, “Elon musk then merged with lebron james to become samwise,” who in turn created Chris. See The Merging.

    Chapter 2

    Musk returns, apparently un-merged, in Chapter 2. When Joeius Bidenias forces Ron DeSantis to make Julian a native bird, Ron “said no and resorted to Mark zuckerberg and elon musk to take back the byzantine empire of mexico.” The war was long and hard.

    Later references

    • Club Shadowban features “a rotating giant Twitter “X” suspended by Elon's tears.” The text does not specify which Elon. Most editors assume Musk, given his association with Twitter / X.
    • Lightning McQueen's allies depart in a Tesla sponsored motorcade.
    • In Chapter the Maybe Seventh, ChatGPT warns that “we have to deal with elon when we deal with chris, if hes coming straight from the shadow realm.” Context strongly suggests this is Mush, not Musk.
    • The Prophecy lists “elon musk” among those who will fall alongside Chris.

    Continuity errors

    • Musk merges into Samwise in the Chapter Before Chapter 2 and then fights a war in Chapter 2 as himself. One fan theory holds that Chapter 2 takes place before the merging, since the Cycle repeats forever anyway.
    • Musk and LeBron are prophesied to fall as separate people.
