    Knights of 4chan

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · A chivalric order of the imageboard.

    Knights of 4chan
    Known memberLightning McQueen
    Platform4chan (real-world imageboard)
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Factions & Organizations

    The Knights of 4chan is a knightly order mentioned exactly once in the canon, in Lightning McQueen's introduction: "The Toyota Corolla to do the initial bombing run was to be lightning McQueen a chickfilla loyalist and a knight of 4chan."

    Nothing else is known about the order: not its oaths, its leader, or how a car is knighted. 4chan is a real-world anonymous imageboard, so the order probably has no known members besides McQueen, which is the point.

    Trivia

    • McQueen holds two titles at once, also being one of the Chick-fil-A Loyalists. Scholars debate which comes first on a Sunday.
