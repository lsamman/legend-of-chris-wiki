    Sector 1/99 (The Gambling Addiction Sector)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Home of many great minds.

    Sector 1/99 (The Gambling Addiction Sector)
    Also known asThe Gambling Addiction Sector
    Notable landmarkThe 6 7 Casino
    Notable former residentsElon Mush, Frodo Baggins, D. Piddy
    First mentionedChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Locations

    Sector 1/99, also called the Gambling Addiction Sector, is the region of the multiverse containing The 6 7 Casino. Steve Jobs welcomed the gangity gang to it when the Inav emerged from a wormhole.

    Steve describes it as having been "the homes of many great minds": Elon Mush, Frodo Baggins and D. Piddy. He adds that "over the years as balling has become compromised" it has declined, but that he has found "something the most remarkable" there: the Verizon contract.

    The fraction "1/99" has never been explained. Fans note that two of its three great minds (Elon Mush, who died of NFL poisoning, and Frodo, who ate the universe) are origin-story figures, which places the sector's history in the deep past.
