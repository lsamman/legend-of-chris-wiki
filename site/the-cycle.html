    The Cycle of Chris (Origin Litany)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · It happened. Then it happened again. Eight times.

    The Cycle of Chris is the origin litany that makes up almost all of the Chapter Before Chapter 2, roughly the first 300 lines of the book. It tells the history of Chris and the Multiverse from Chris's birth to Chris's birth, and then tells it again. In-universe, editors treat it as an eternal recurrence: history literally repeats.

    Sequence of events

    Each recitation goes through the following:

    • Chris is a baby, is baller, dies, balls from the grave, becomes the ball, and rises into the sky with the tech bros.
    • Apple Podcasts is founded and the word reaches the middle aged men.
    • Elon Mush mooshes the chalkboards, creates Settings and the Multiverse, and accidentally creates the Cringe Universe.
    • The Dabbing of the Haters, then the Great Oppression, then the Multiverse Civil War.
    • Zuckerberg's Snap.
    • Frodo takes Andromeda and carries out the Cooking of the Universe.
    • The Great Famine, and the death of Mush by NFL poisoning.
    • The Merging into Samwise, who creates Chris, “born in newark.”

    Then it starts over at “Chris was a baby.”

    Textual variants

    The recitations are almost, but not quite, identical:

    • “nfl poisoning” appears four times and “nfl poisioning” four times.
    • “Spider-man Across the spider verse” appears four times and “spiderverse” four times.
    • Some recitations run straight on from “born in newark.” into “Chris was a baby” without a space.
    • The final recitation ends with “born in newark. 9999999999999,” after which Chapter 2 begins. Theories about the meaning of the thirteen nines include a loop counter, a page number, and someone falling asleep on the keyboard.

    The Chapter 2 leak

    After the fourth recitation, instead of restarting, the text continues into “Then he was stabbed to death by Ron destantis” and runs through most of the opening of Chapter 2, from the Stabbing to the Soviet Union of North Korea. Then, mid-sentence, it resets with “Why do we believe what we believe…” and the Cycle continues as if nothing happened. Some fans regard this as the only instance of Chapter 2 appearing inside the chapter that comes before it.

    Interpretation

    The Cycle explains the paradoxes of Chris's birth: Samwise can create a Chris who already knew Samwise's components, because the loop has no first iteration. It also explains why the Prophecy is needed, since without outside intervention the Cycle would simply keep going.
