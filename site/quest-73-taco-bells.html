    The Quest for the 73 Taco Bell Locations

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events · Ongoing.

    The Quest for the 73 Taco Bell Locations
    QuesterRon DeSantis
    ObjectiveFind 73 Taco Bell locations
    StatusUnresolved
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Events

    The Quest for the 73 Taco Bell Locations is the quest Ron De Santis began right after stabbing Chris: “Ron De Santis then started his quest to find the 73 Taco bell locations.”

    Why there are 73, why they had to be found, and whether any were found is never stated. Ron gets distracted almost at once by Julian and the War for Byzantine Mexico. Mexico is plausibly where one would look for tacos, so some editors argue the war was part of the quest.

    Trivia

    • Taco Bell turns up again indirectly when Kanye describes Gotham as “a city that looks like Hot Topic threw up on a Taco Bell.”
