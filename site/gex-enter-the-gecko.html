    Gex: Enter the Gecko

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · The storm of box art.

    Gex: Enter the Gecko
    TypeVideo game
    ConsoleNintendo 64
    FoundThe TurboGrafx 16 Sector, in great quantity
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    ***Gex: Enter the Gecko*** is a Nintendo 64 game whose covers fill the horizon of the TurboGrafx 16 universe: an “endless stream of ripped out covers” and “the dope amount of box art”. Steve Jobs reaches out of the Inav and pulls one in.

    Explanation

    Steve explains: “Since the premier of Borderline forever from Scott the woz. in this universe the visible horizon of this place has been filled with copy’s of Gex: Enter the gecko, in addition if you’ll notice in your vision there’s a blue border in the edge of your sight.” That blue border is the Border.

    Trivia

    • Gex: Enter the Gecko is a real N64 game.
    • Steve's explanation also mentions “Borderline forever from Scott the woz”, which the book treats as the event that filled the horizon.
