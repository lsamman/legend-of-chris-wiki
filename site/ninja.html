    Ninja (Tyler Blevins)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters

    Ninja (Tyler Blevins)
    Real nameTyler Blevins
    Known forPossibly joining the lobby
    Associated withFortnite
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Characters

    Ninja, “aka Tyler Blevins”, is mentioned once in the canon, when the blue glow of DPF Facility 69420 appears on the horizon:

    “Hey uh guys, did ninja aka Tyler Blevins join this Fortnite lobby.”

    He did not. The glow came from TON 618, which then roared. The question still sets off Plankton's Fortnite Island Wars instincts, and he chants “Fornite battle pass, I just-”.

    Trivia

    • Ninja is a real streamer best known for Fortnite and for his blue hair. Fans think the faint blue glow is why he came to mind. This is a fan theory.
    • The line comes right after the narration about Ioannus Paranukus looking out the window, so it is usually credited to him.
