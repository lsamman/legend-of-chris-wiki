    Chicken Hour

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore

    Chicken Hour
    TypeTime of day
    EffectGuards not looking out
    Observed inThe Projects
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Concepts & Lore

    Chicken hour is a time of day in the Projects during which guards are apparently not on the lookout. As the gang gets close to O'Block, someone says:

    “We’re almo7st there, its chicken hour so I doubt the guards will be looking out for us”

    What happens during chicken hour is never explained. Most fans assume it is a meal break. The timing matches the end of the chapter, “as the skies turned orange”.

    Trivia

    • “almo7st” is a typo with a 7 in it, which fans have connected to the canon's 67 motif. This is a fan theory.
    • Earlier, Lightning McQueen holds up a Popeyes in what may be an observance of chicken hour. This is a fan theory.
