    The 6 7 Casino

    The 6 7 Casino is an oil casino in Sector 1/99, the Gambling Addiction Sector. It is home to the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text, an ancient relic that generates the Field of Gambling allowing its patrons to gamble at all. It is the setting of the Boys' Casino Heist.

    Description

    Exterior

    Seen from the Inav at evening, "the casino was taller than the wideness of the tall, and it was a sight to behold". Kanye West described it as "bussin' for real on Geoffrey". Its walls are made of "pale beige brick", which is soft enough to be cut by the Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser.

    Interior

    When Hank Hill's crew arrived through the port-a-potty, they found "a vast casino lined with slot machines, gold decor", and statues of three public figures. The casino contains "elegant hallways", long enough that reaching the roulette room left the crew "panting from the marathon".

    Lower chambers

    A side staircase called the Stairway to Heaven leads down to the lower chambers and the Vault, a "giant freaking door" behind which the relic is kept.

    Security

    • Charlie Kirks, trained warriors posted in the underground hallways.
    • DJ Khaled, who appears to serve as a security guard.
    • Hank Hill, Jesse and Walter, hired by Microsoft Bing for one night in exchange for 15 gallons of Prime US Oil.
    • Alarms, and a "protection system" that fires on intruders. Its bullets bounced off the Inav.
    • Staff are apparently issued golden scars.

    History

    The casino's relic was "recovered from the ruins of the LA Walmart". During the night of the heist, the Vault was opened by the Ibeam, leaving "a large hole" in the building through which moonlight came in. The fate of the casino after the relic was removed is unknown, but since the Field of Gambling only exists around a building containing the relic, fans assume gambling there has stopped.

    Trivia

    • The name refers to the "6 7" meme. The number 67 recurs throughout the canon: "67 ioann rizz", a "67gb micro sd card", and the typo "almo7st".
    • It is called an "oil casino" by the Quick-Check clerk, but no oil-based gambling is ever shown.
    • ChatGPT claims to have seen casinos for "a millennia", raising questions about its age (see Bing's Son).
