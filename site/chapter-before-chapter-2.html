    Chapters

    The Chapter Before Chapter 2, subtitled Chris Origins, is the opening chapter of The Legend of Chris. It is the cosmological foundation of the entire canon, establishing the birth, death, balling and ascension of Chris, the creation of the The Multiverse, and the long chain of catastrophes that ends with Chris being born in Newark.

    The chapter is notable for consisting almost entirely of a single paragraph, known to scholars as the Origin Litany, repeated approximately nine times in a row. Whether this is a printing error, a liturgical device, or a deliberate depiction of the eternal recurrence of Chris remains one of the most hotly debated questions in the fandom.

    Synopsis

    The question

    The chapter opens with the philosophical inquiry Why Do We Believe What We Believe, which is immediately answered: "Its a matter of Chris."

    The life and afterlife of Chris

    "Chris was a baby he was baller and then he died and then he balled from the grave and then he became the ball." Chris then balled himself into the sky together with LeBron James, Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg to learn the secret teachings of Twitter / X, MySpace, Instagram, Snapchat and Facebook. Together they founded Apple Podcasts and spread the word of Chris to all the middle aged men to roam the earth.

    The deeds of Elon Mush

    Elon Musk's secret cousin Elon Mush "mooshed" all the chalkboards on earth and used their power to create the anti-social media platform Settings, "a place for computer nerds to tweak their settings on all devices until the ends of all times." Mush then created the multiverse solely to recreate Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, and accidentally created the Cringe Universe, a universe of nazis.

    The multiversal wars

    LeBron James, judging this "cringe", dispatched the Baller Army of the United States of A America to dab on the haters ("over 10 casualties"). The Great Oppression followed, in which the America Universe took over the Cringe Universe, then yearned for the Minor Miner Universe "because it was funny." A civil war broke out, "the bad ones won," and Zuckerberg snapped his fingers, sending every universe except his favorite to the Shadow Realm, where light does not exist and nazi ants still exist.

    Frodo and the famine

    Frodo began a takeover of the Andromeda Galaxy and used Master Chef to cook the universe into "a sirloin steak with lobster tail," ate it, and left, "never to be baller again." A great famine followed, and Elon Mush died of NFL poisoning. Elon Musk then merged with LeBron James to become Samwise, who created Chris, who was born in Newark.

    And then it happens again.

    Title & numbering

    The title is printed vertically, one word per line ("The / Chapter / Before / Chapter 2"), followed by the centered subtitle "Chris Origins". The chapter therefore defines itself entirely in relation to a chapter that has not happened yet. It is never called "Chapter 1", and no Chapter 1 exists anywhere in the book, making this the closest thing the canon has to one. The chapter ends with the string "9999999999999", which some fans interpret as a page number, a cat walking on the keyboard, or the number of times the litany should be recited.

    Continuity notes

    • The litany is repeated roughly nine times with small variations: "Spider-verse" vs. "spiderverse", "doesnt" vs. "doesn't", and "poisioning" vs. "poisoning".
    • In the fourth repetition (around line 150), the text skips ahead and runs straight into the opening of Chapter 2 (the The Stabbing of Chris, Ron DeSantis, Truth Social) before snapping back to "Why do we believe" mid-sentence. Chapter 2 therefore technically begins before the Chapter Before Chapter 2 ends.
    • Elon Mush dies of NFL poisoning in every repetition, and is resurrected by the next one.

    Trivia

    • The Origin Litany accounts for nearly a quarter of the book's total length.
    • The Cycle of Chris is sometimes cited as evidence that Chris's existence is a loop, with every birth in Newark resetting the multiverse.
    • Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is a 2023 animated film about a multiverse of Spider-People. In the canon it is the sole reason the multiverse exists.
