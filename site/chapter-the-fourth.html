    Chapters

    Chapter the fourth one the one after the one after the second one which is before the third one, usually shortened to Chapter the Fourth, is the fourth chapter of The Legend of Chris. It introduces Hank Hill's propane empire and its feud with Kanye West, concludes the Fall of Constantinople, and delivers RFK and Plankton to the doorstep of Dunder Mifflin.

    Synopsis

    Hank Hill on the Twelve Towers

    The chapter opens with "Damn son where'd you find those?" as Hank Hill stands atop the Twelve Towers in the Kanye System, surrounded by the Department of Defense, an army "divided by" Jeff Bezosi and Mark Zucc. His Propane empire works with the Los Polos Harmonicas Under System Gang. The DOD claims to hold Snoopy McSpookintosh at COD headquarters, but Hank warns of "the wrath of Jesse and white." The drug van from Breaking Bad exits hyperspace, rains blue dust on the DOD, and they flee "in fear of being a Goober."

    The Weezing of Kanye

    Hank, Walter White and Jesse Pinkman need ingredients from Kanye but their Twitter / X account lacks 100 followers. Since Kanye supposedly only comes out during the Super Bowl, they break into his house and weeze him, only to find the propane watered down. "This isn't over kanye"…

    The Fall of Constantinople

    Mr. Bean and 007 have hidden two nukes "inside various bags of old pagers," while McQueen plants the last two at the abandoned Walmarts. 007 has "lost his torso," and Putin, approaching the center, lectures Mr. Bean on mental illness. McQueen is confronted by the local mafia, declines "to be part of society," and finds the second Walmart is now a Popeyes, where he orders an enormous meal at gunpoint with his Glock 19. He collects the others at 8aller Jump Street, passes a vaporized DaBaby, and escapes through the wormhole. Constantinople falls "at 14:74pm Unc standard time." The trio is formally named the Goober Gang.

    Ye the Gas

    In the United Streets of Ohio, Kanye, sipping Prime, presents the corner store selling potato chips at 2.85 a gallon, and asks ChatGPT about promoting his new propane brand, Ye the Gas.

    Arrival at Dunder Mifflan

    After a month in their underpowered Mercedes Benz System Cruiser, boosted by Galaxy Gas from Litl Wayne of the Litl Empire, RFK and Plankton reach Dunder Mifflin, a galaxy encased in one office building and guarded by Google Smart Guards. They slip through the Paper Belt and behind the wires of the giant LED sign.

    Title & numbering

    The title is printed as a narrow eleven-line column. It describes the chapter as "the one after the one after the second one," which correctly makes it the fourth, and also "before the third one," which makes it impossible. Scholars have proposed that the third chapter being named "Triangle" rather than a number frees this chapter to come before it.

    Continuity notes

    • The plan in Chapter Triangle was to lure Barack Obama into the center of Constantinople. Here it is Vladimir Putin, with whom Mr. Bean ends up having coffee. Obama is not mentioned.
    • Snoopy McSpookintosh is "in custody" and is never mentioned again.
    • The van is called "the drug van", "the crack van" and "the bus" within a few lines.

    Trivia

    • "Propane and propane accessories" is Hank Hill's catchphrase from King of the Hill.
    • Jesse Pinkman, Walter White and Gus Fring are from Breaking Bad; Gus is mentioned only as someone Hank has a meeting with.
    • 14:74pm is not a real time, though it is a real time in Unc Standard Time.
