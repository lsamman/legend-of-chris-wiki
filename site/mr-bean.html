    Mr. Bean

    Mr. Bean (written "Mr.bean" and "Mr.Bean") is a Parliament operative, the partner in crime of Agent 007, and a founding member of The Goober Gang. He is the strategist behind the plan to take Constantinople from the Barack Empire.

    Contents
    1. Appearance
    2. Biography
    3. Trivia

    Appearance

    Mr. Bean is described as wearing a "gold suit normal pants, a round tall hat and a set of brand new Kanye west yeezes." He is one of the best-dressed characters in the canon.

    Biography

    The briefing

    Mr. Bean met Lightning McQueen outside the Smoke and Vape Shop in England. While 007 vaped, Bean explained the mission: take Constantinople, restore Bernie Sanders, and deal with Barack Obama, who holds the 7 Chaos Emeralds. His plan was to plant nukes, break the city's oil lines to flood it, "lure Obamna into the center and light the nukes", and escape within 6.8 seconds through a wormhole that "theroreticly could open there but we have Basiclly no clue if It'll actually happen." He then announced that the Tesla sponsored motorcade was ready at E.C.S.M Airport.

    The operation

    In the Fall of Constantinople, Bean planted two nukes in bags of old pagers and reported 007's missing torso over the radio. He also spent part of the operation being lectured by Vladimir Putin "about how Mental illness in a myth created my Mormon progressives". Later he reported he had "just finished lunch" and was "having coffee" with Putin. He excused himself politely ("Ok sorry Mr.putin we gotta go to a job we'll see you later bye!") and waited for pickup at 8aller Jump Street.

    Trivia

    • Mr. Bean is a famously near-silent British comedy character. In the canon he is the most talkative member of his team.
    • The hashtag "#IaintnoSigma" refers to the "sigma male" meme.
    • Bean's plan targets Obama, but on the day it was Putin who was "almost in the center". See Barack Obama for this continuity error.
