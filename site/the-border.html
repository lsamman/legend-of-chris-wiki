    The Border

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · A blue border. A giant bezel. A boss fight.

    The Border
    TypePhenomenon / location
    LocationThe edge of vision in the TurboGrafx 16 universe
    ColourBlue, then dark grey, then black, then blue again
    InhabitantSpamton NEO
    Blamed onChris
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Locations

    The Border is a blue frame “in the edge of your sight” in the TurboGrafx 16 universe, a result of the visible horizon being filled with Gex: Enter the Gecko covers. Steve Jobs notices it first; Kanye “became visibly tweaked out” at the idea that “im surrounded by some big blue border”.

    Contents
    1. The change
    2. Inside
    3. Trivia

    The change

    Scott reports that it is changing colour, then turning black, then becoming “A giant Bezel.” As the gang runs for Scott's house, “the border got darker and darker, not yet black but a very, very, dark grey.” It is later seen as “the giant blue border suspended in space”, and two roadsters dock to it.

    Inside

    The interior is darkness, then a clank, “a bit crunch, and the sound of pulleys rapidly approaching”. The inhabitant is Spamton NEO. When he is removed, “the color started returning to the blue border to the tune of the Scott the woz motif.” See The Border Fight.

    Trivia

    • Editors read the Border as a screen bezel. Scott's worry about Wii Fit is the book's only explanation.
    • Chris is blamed for it by Scott: “By Chris! It’s becoming.. A giant Bezel.”
