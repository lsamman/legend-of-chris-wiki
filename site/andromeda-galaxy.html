    Andromeda Galaxy

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Taken by Frodo.

    Andromeda Galaxy
    Conquered byFrodo Baggins
    EventGalactic takeover
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Locations

    The Andromeda Galaxy is the site of Frodo's conquest. After the Snap, “frodo appeared deep in the universe and started a galactic take over of the andromeda galaxy.” The narrative then moves directly to Frodo cooking the whole universe with Master Chef. Whether Andromeda was cooked with everything else, or was simply the kitchen, is unknown.

    In the real world, Andromeda is the nearest large galaxy to the Milky Way, about 2.5 million light-years away. In this canon it was an appetizer.
