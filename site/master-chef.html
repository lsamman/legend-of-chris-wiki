    Master Chef

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Kitchen appliance of cosmic scale.

    Master Chef
    TypeCooking implement (exact nature unknown)
    WielderFrodo Baggins
    OutputOne universe, prepared as surf and turf
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Items & Technology

    Master Chef is the means by which Frodo cooked the universe: “he used master chef to cook the universe into a sirloin steak with lobster tail.” The book never says whether Master Chef is a device, a person, a technique, or the television show.

    Whatever it is, it worked on the first try. The result was eaten in full, which caused the Great Famine.

    Trivia

    • MasterChef is a real-world cooking competition franchise. No contestant has yet attempted a universe.
    • See The Cooking of the Universe for the full event.
