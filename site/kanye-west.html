    Kanye West, also called Ye, is a propane mogul, rapper and one of the canon's main protagonists. Along with his AI partner ChatGPT and later Steve Jobs, he leads the effort to collect the artifacts needed to defeat Chris.

    Biography

    Weezed

    Kanye first comes up as the target of Hank Hill's crew, who say he "only came out during the Super Bowl". They broke into his house and weezed him, and found his propane watered down. See The Weezing of Kanye.

    The propane business

    In the United Streets of Ohio, Kanye is pitched a corner store that sells "potato chips for 2.85 a gallon". He sips "his super baller prime energy drink" and plans "a song to promote my new propane brand, ye the gas". He asks ChatGPT whether it will "ensure the continuation of the water gas trend ive been creating".

    Gotham

    Kanye and ChatGPT "rolled into Gotham city" in the Prius of Destiny ("Yo GPT, you ever been to a city that looks like Hot Topic threw up on a Taco Bell?"). There they met Ye Olde Bin and FS Martin Genius, who gave them the Zuck Tears USB. Drake Batman then asked for help against Lil Jokez. At Club Shadowban, Kanye and ChatGPT won the rap battle by dropping 808s and Apocalypses. This brought Steve Jobs down on the iPhone 3G, and Kanye shouted "Steve Jawbs?!"

    The Inav and the casino

    Aboard the Inav, Kanye asked Siri for a diet dr.pepper ("dayum this is ice cold bruh"). At Sector 1/99 he reacted to the casino with "Woah bruh this place be bussin' for real on Geoffrey", and to Yuno Miles's backstory with "The fuck does that mean Kobe Bryant". During the heist he was first to find the Charlie Kirks, used an Igun to stop them, and lunged at DJ Khaled, who dodged him. He then persuaded his old enemy Hank Hill to switch sides. After the Ibeam opened the vault, Kanye "grabbed the gift card and put it into his Yeezy jacket".

    Personality

    Kanye is confident, easily impressed by luxury, and quick to speak. He is loyal to ChatGPT and treats Steve Jobs with awe. The text describes his response to Lil Jokez's diss simply: "Kanye's eyes glowed."

    The TurboGrafx 16 universe

    Kanye “became visibly tweaked out” when told about the blue border (“Its like im surrounded by some big blue border n-”), and defends ChatGPT on Throats For Thought: “I will not have racism against my AI sideki-”. In the Border Fight he drops bars on Spamton NEO. Afterwards he is the one to say “We finna pull up on those robots brotha”, on learning that Snapchat: Ghost Protocol is in Freddy Fazbear's Pizza.

    When asked who he is, he answers Jerry Attricks: “It’s Ye.”

    Continuity errors

    • Hank's crew believe Kanye "only came out during the Super Bowl", yet he appears at a corner store, in Gotham, in a nightclub, and in a casino basement. None of these are the Super Bowl.
    • Hank says Kanye's propane was watered down. Kanye calls himself "no watered-down trash" and is promoting "the water gas trend" at the same time.

    New Jersey

    In Chapter Number New Jersey Kanye wakes sleep deprived from a dream about Travis Scott, gets weed infused coffee, and calls the screaming voice “Strange..”. He fights Yeat on top of a Lambo Tonka and wins with one sentence, “in reality jesus is king”, and a beam of light from God (The Smiting of Yeat). He then leads the gang into Freddy Fazbear's Pizza.

    Trivia

    • Mr. Bean wears "a set of brand new Kanye west yeezes", so Kanye's fashion line exists across the multiverse.
    • ChatGPT mentions that Kanye has "plenty of oil in the Yeezy reserve". Exactly why he needs to defeat Chris is therefore unclear, though ChatGPT gives reasons involving regulation and free balling.

    In The Epic of Vinny

    Kanye West is only mentioned in The Epic of Vinny so far. Claude, King of AI says a human named Kanye West "came down here, and released ChatGPT, to assist Kanye West on his journeys", and that this enraged him.
