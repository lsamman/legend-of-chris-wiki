    Osama Bin Bom'in (spelled at least four different ways in the canon) is the ruler of the Projects and one of the main antagonists of The Legend of Chris. A former fraternity brother who seized power in a palace coup, he coordinated the Attack on Moscow, exiled MF Vroom to the Call of Duty lobbies, and commands an arsenal of giant robots. He is rumored to have "connections with Chris".

    Rise to power

    According to Son S. Sonionring, MF Vroom and Son built O'Block up "to its glory". But "once O'block had a nation around it the frat bros rose up, One in particular… Osama Bin Bomb'in." Osama, a member of Pol Phi Doros at Koolaid College, showed up on Vroom's porch with his brothers and demanded that O'Block join the EU. When MF Vroom declined "because the Projects are in the americas not Europe", they "stormed his palace and gained complete control". See The O'Block Coup.

    Continuity note

    This account comes from Chapter 4+.1+.1…. In Chapter 2, however, MF Vroom is introduced as a "long lost relative of the project's royal family" whom Osama exiles on sight, which suggests either that Vroom was deposed twice or that the narrator had not yet decided. The book itself admits, "We forgot to mention this in the prologue".

    The Moscow affair

    In Chapter 2, Osama receives Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire at I-38 West, the heart of the Projects. Upon their arrival he sends their guide MF Vroom into exile "to forever get yelled at in COD lobbies." He and his guests then settle which Toyota Corolla will lead the bombing run on Moscow, all to settle the DQ–McDonald's MySpace beef in DQ's favor. As one of the Keepers of the MySpace Relics, he takes this beef personally.

    While leaving the Projects, Osama was ambushed by QXC and Jinxy. "Osama not knowing who they we're ignored the mustard gas being deployed in this area and left without injury." Fan wikis widely agree this is the most effective use of ignoring something in recorded history.

    The O Block 2 era

    "In the begining of the O'block 2 era, when It was being scaled to be a EU level nation, Osama Bin' Bombin created monolithically sized defensive bio-robotic weapons robots", the TON robots. The most infamous of these is TON 618 ("Terrible Offensive Nuhuh"), kept in DPF Facility 69420 and described as "the projects flagship defense".

    When MF Vroom returns in Chapter 4+.1+.1…, Osama's ambassador Tung Tung Tung Sahur attempts to arrest him at the national spaceport. Son reports that "Osama knows we're here", and Osama responds by unleashing TON 618 on the city. MF Vroom theorizes that this is because of the Prophecy: "If he knows our end goal already and he has connections with Chris.. That has to be why."

    Trivia

    • His name is a pun. The wiki does not elaborate.
    • Spellings recorded: "bin bom'in", "Bin Bom'in", "Bin' bombin", "Bin Bomb'in" and "Bin' Bombin". This wiki uses the first spelling the canon gives.
    • He is the only character in the canon who is both a keeper of the MySpace relics and a fraternity brother. Whether Pol Phi Doros has a MySpace page is unknown.
    • Osama is never seen on-page after Chapter 2; his later actions are told only through other characters and giant robots.
