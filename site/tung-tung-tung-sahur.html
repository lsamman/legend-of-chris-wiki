    Tung Tung Tung Sahur

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Ambassador. Wooden. Briefly a beta male.

    Characters

    Tung Tung Tung Sahur, also called Triple T, is the ambassador of Osama Bin Bom'in for the Projects. He has a wooden body. He meets the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser at the Projects National Spaceport, and the narrator calls this “a big deal”.

    Contents
    1. Attempted arrest
    2. Status
    3. Behind the scenes

    Attempted arrest

    Triple T tells MF Vroom:

    “MF Vroom.. it is not very poggers for you to come back after you were explicitly sent into exile, we’re gonna have to arrest you”

    While Plankton and RFK run back to the ship for their modded nerf guns, a dark figure comes out of the janitorial staff. Triple T insists “Sir this is official Alpha male business” and then “Sir cursing is strictly prohibited” (in response to “GG fricken easy”). The figure turns out to be Son S. Sonionring. Triple T's last word is a whimpered “.. Son?”, and then a musket pellet goes “right through his wooden body and incapacitated him.”

    Status

    Triple T is not dead, or at least will not stay that way. Son explains:

    “That’ll only have him being a beta male until his death pose starts mogging, then he’ll be back”

    His return therefore depends on how much his death pose mogs. When the crew leaves he is still on the floor. Plankton calls the situation “that fat L issue”, now sorted.

    Behind the scenes

    • Tung Tung Tung Sahur is a wooden, bat-carrying character from the “Italian brainrot” internet meme trend, which is why his body is wooden.
    • He holds an ambassador's title while living in the country he is ambassador for. How this works is not explained.
