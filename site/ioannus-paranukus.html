    Ioannus Paranukus (introduced as Ioann-ius Nukian) is a Jedi master from the Projects who joins RFK and Plankton at Call of Duty HQ to free MF Vroom, whom he calls his king. He speaks in an archaic style, has a scarred face, fights with a white Lightsaber, and has more spellings of his name than any other character in the canon.

    Biography

    Introduction at COD HQ

    Ioannus enters by smashing through the extremely openable OpenAble Door. "As lighting struck around them a flash of light illuminated his face, showing a scarred face filled with alpha energy." He asks whether they have come to break MF Vroom's "chains of COD".

    Plankton answers with a speech about "chains that bind us, and some chains that blind us", and asks: "do you bid us harm or are you a desirable crip". Ioannus is offended: "Art thou a fool?, for I am not native to these lands, for I am of the projects, I wish to free my king!"

    He doubts the party ("Pah with a party like yours I Do not see how you intend on freeing him") and warns them about the Xbox Live Dungeons. Plankton answers "Nah we'll be fine", Ioannus says "aight bet", and "the party of 3" goes in.

    The battle and the 360 Chambers

    Inside the HQ, "Ioann-ius jumped into the battle, his lightsaber lit up a deep white", and "disappeared into the deep red mist of the bloodshed of the battle". In Chapter 4+.1… he is running with RFK and Plankton into the 360 Chambers anyway. There he draws his lightsaber at a shadow (MF Vroom), notices the newly appeared Dorito-dusted door, and later remarks, "brooding in his Russian flag cloak": "On Bitch Ass 1000 for real for real".

    Return to the Projects

    Ioannus rides in the back of Son's 2005 Honda Accord with Plankton during the Spaceport Breakout. Through the window he is the first to spot the blue glow of DPF Facility 69420, home of TON 618. He is last seen with the gang as they head for the palace to retrieve Facebook (Pre-Cringe).

    Abilities

    • Lightsaber combat, including "light sabers slicing" in the spaceport breakout, where the gang's "body count was going crazy".
    • Alpha energy, visible in his scars.
    • Rizz. The phrase "67 ioann rizz" later turns up in the casino, used to describe Mr. White's determination: he "ran to the hallway with 67 ioann rizz in his eyes". This suggests Ioannus's rizz is famous enough to be used as a unit of measure across the multiverse.

    Continuity errors

    • Name: Ioann-ius Nukian, Ioann-ius, Ioann, Ioannus paranukus, Ioann paranukus, Ioannus Paranukus and Ioannius Paranukus. The wiki uses "Ioannus Paranukus" because it is the most common.
    • Location: he vanishes into the battle while "Rfk and Plankton ran down the adjacent hallway" alone, but he is with them when they arrive at the 360 Chambers.
    • Nationality: he says he is "of the projects", but he wears a Russian flag cloak.

    Trivia

    • His job title pairs "Jedi master" with "dealer of justice", and he immediately clarifies that he sells justice by the gram.
    • He is the only character in the canon who uses "thou" and "art".
