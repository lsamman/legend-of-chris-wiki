    The Prius of Destiny
    TypeCar
    OwnerKanye West
    Last seenParked next to Ye Olde Bin, Gotham City
    StatusUnknown
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Items & Technology

    The Prius of Destiny is Kanye West's car, presumably the vehicle in which he and ChatGPT "rolled into Gotham city". It is mentioned exactly once: "Kanye parked the Prius of Destiny next to a sentient trash can named 'Ye Olde Bin'."

    Fate

    The Prius is never mentioned again. Kanye leaves Gotham aboard the Inav while the city disappears, meaning the Prius of Destiny was likely left behind. Its destiny remains unfulfilled.

    Trivia

    • The Prius is made by Toyota, making it a distant cousin of the canon's many Toyota Corollas and Camrys (see The Toyota Corolla Question).
    • Fan theory: the Prius was sent to the Shadow Realm along with Club Shadowban. Unconfirmed.
