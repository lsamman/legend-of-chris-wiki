    Son's 2005 Honda Accord

    Items & Technology

    Son's 2005 Honda Accord (his “whip”) is the getaway car of the Spaceport Breakout. With Police Kirks chasing them out of the Projects National Spaceport, Son shouts “Quick hop in my whip cuh!”, and the gang gets in to the sound of “Skrrrt” as Son does “a completely unnecessary burn out.”

    Contents
    1. Seating
    2. Armament
    3. Continuity errors

    Seating

    According to the book: Plankton and Ioannus Paranukus in the back, Son driving, and RFK in shotgun. MF Vroom is not listed. Since MF Vroom is himself a car (“a fuckig car wearing a robe”), some fans think he drove alongside. This is a fan theory.

    Armament

    The Accord has a hidden weapon. Son reaches into the glove box, there is “something like a click”, and:

    “suddenly a machine gun appeared out of the trunk. As son stepped on the throttle the machine gun started firing. Little pellets of solidified purple juice shot at the cars and popped all of the tires”

    The narrator stresses that “it was magnificent how many tires were popped it was truly amazing you should’ve been there.” The car stereo plays “I Can't Put Down the Cup” the whole time.

    Continuity errors

    • When TON 618 starts searching the city, it looks “for the Camry”. The gang is in a Honda Accord.
    • Earlier in the same chapter, Plankton asks the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser “why r u built like a 2008 Honda accord Zyn weed addition”. That makes two Accords from two different model years in one chapter.
    • See also the Toyota Corolla Question, since Lightning McQueen is canonically a Corolla.
