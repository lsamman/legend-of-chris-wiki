    The Ball

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · What Chris became.

    The Ball
    FormerlyChris
    DestinationThe sky
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Concepts & Lore

    The Ball is the form Chris took after balling from the grave: “then he became the ball and balled himself into the sky together with lebron james and elon musk and mark zuckerberg.”

    The Ball is the source of all Balling, or possibly the result of it; theologians disagree. As the Ball, Chris is both the one who balls and the thing that is balled.

    Not to be confused with

    • The roulette ball in the 6 7 Casino (“And the ball rolled.. and rolled.. and rolled…”), which was stopped by Microsoft Bing. See The Great Roulette Bet. Some fans read this as a sign of Chris's presence. Nothing in the text supports this.
