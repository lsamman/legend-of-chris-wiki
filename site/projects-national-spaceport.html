    The Projects National Spaceport

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations

    The Projects National Spaceport
    LocationThe Projects, The United State of America
    SecurityBreached
    Staff of noteSon S. Sonionring (janitorial, undercover)
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Locations

    The Projects National Spaceport is the main spaceport of the Projects and the place where the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser lands in Chapter 4+.1+.1…. Plankton lines the ship up with it from Adirondack Park with a mach 3 turn, and the crew lands after “a hour or two of practicing mogging”.

    Events

    At the bottom of the ramp the crew is met by Tung Tung Tung Sahur, ambassador of Osama Bin Bom'in, who tries to arrest MF Vroom for coming back from exile. Son S. Sonionring, who has been hiding among the janitorial staff, shoots him with a modded colonial musket.

    The gang then goes “straight through spaceport security, muskets firing, light sabers slicing, their body count was going crazy” and escapes through the front doors with Police Kirks chasing them. See The Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase.

    Trivia

    • Outside the spaceport the streets are lined with “Brick buildings and sketchy gas stations”.
    • The spaceport's security did not catch an armed musketeer working on its own janitorial staff. Fans have pointed out the hiring problem.
