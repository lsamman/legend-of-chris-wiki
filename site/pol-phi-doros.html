    Pol Phi Doros

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations

    Pol Phi Doros
    TypeFraternity
    CollegeKoolaid College

    Known forThe O'Block Coup
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Factions & Organizations

    Pol Phi Doros is the fraternity Osama Bin Bom'in belonged to at Koolaid College. According to Son, once O'Block “had a nation around it the frat bros rose up, One in particular… Osama”. The frat then showed up on MF Vroom's porch and later stormed his palace in the O'Block Coup.

    Trivia

    • It is the canon's second fraternity, after Alpha Mogger Beta (MF Vroom's). The two frats are on opposite sides of the Projects' politics.
    • The name looks like Greek letters but only partly is. “Phi” is real. Fans have suggested “Doros” comes from Doritos, which are everywhere in the canon. This is a fan theory.
