    Characters

    John Cena is invoked once, by Plankton, as the ship's stairs lower at Call of Duty HQ: "Insert and his name is john cena here".

    The line looks like a placeholder that was never filled in. Whether Cena is present, was meant to be inserted, or is the stairs is not stated, and he does nothing else.

    Behind the scenes

    • Real-world: John Cena is a professional wrestler and actor. "And his name is John Cena!" is the announcer's line from his entrance, which became a widespread meme. Another meme says "you can't see him", which would explain why nobody in the scene reacts.
    • The line is immediately followed by a parenthetical about Larry the Cucumber.
