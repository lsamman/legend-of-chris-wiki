    Chris: All Balled Up Deluxe Edition

    Chris, known in his full and most feared form as Chris: All Balled Up Deluxe Edition, is the title character of The Legend of Chris and the central figure of its entire cosmology. He is at once a baby, a baller, a corpse, the ball itself, a founding member of Apple Podcasts, a war criminal, and the being whose defeat on the court is foretold by the Prophecy. According to the opening incantation, everything that is believed is ultimately “a matter of Chris.”

    Did you know…

    • The iPhone 3G Keynote — Apple's real headquarters are in Cupertino, California. The real iPhone 3G was announced at WWDC in June 2008.
    • Peter Griffin — The ringtone interrupts “the middle of their speech” about playing Wemby, Curry, even Bronny, so nobody gets to finish the explanation.
    • The New Crips — The phrase "new parks of nikes" is probably a typo for "new pair of Nikes". The wiki keeps it as written.
    • Barack Obama — "Obamna" is a well-known internet misspelling meme.
    • The 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text — At the time of writing, no 3 year Verizon plan was known to generate gambling fields in the real world.
    • Zarkmuckerberg — His entry is followed by seven full stops, the longest ellipsis in Chapter 2.
    • Charlie Kirks — Named after the American political commentator Charlie Kirk.
