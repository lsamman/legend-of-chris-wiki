    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Owner of a giant box made of Wii games. Enemy of the Border.

    Characters

    Scott the Woz is the owner of a ship made of Wii games and the host of the best-known rescue mission in the TurboGrafx 16 Sector. He is named after a real YouTuber, and the book describes only what he does in it: he arrives mid-argument on the set of Throats For Thought to report that the sky is changing colour, flies the heroes to the source in his Wii cube, and ends the fight with Spamton NEO by feeding a Sega Genesis a very specific video game.

    Arrival

    Scott bursts into the studio “out of breath and in a state of panic” with the line “Hey all, Scott here”. He tells Steve Jobs that “The border, its changing color!” and then that “It’s turning black!”, and finally that it is becoming “A giant Bezel.” He explains his stake in it plainly: “I usually buy apple products but Chris has turned that against me! HOW WILL I PLAY WII FIT!”

    It is the first time in the canon that Chris is blamed for a graphical user interface.

    The Wii cube

    When the heroes ask why he needs their help, Scott answers “Giant orb spaceship” and is told “Fair enough.” He leads them past the target and the GameStop to his house, where the ship waits in the backyard: “A giant box made out of Wii games.” He apologises that “It’s a bit rusty since we fought the green bay packers but it should do the job”, then calls his gang on an iPhone duo. See The Wozniaks and The Wii Cube.

    The Border fight

    Inside the Border, Scott is struck by a “flying Kromer brick freshly bought from Costco in bulk”, while ChatGPT films the fight for TikTok. Thinking fast, he pulls a Sega Genesis from the scene and slams a copy of Dick Vitale's Awesome Baby into it. The game boots, Spamton NEO is ripped from his strings, and the Border's colour returns “to the tune of the Scott the woz motif.” See The Border Fight.

    Aftermath

    Scott thanks Steve for the backup (“you did absolutely nothing”, and Steve accepts this as praise), phones Officer Steelwool to find Snapchat: Ghost Protocol, and later agrees to host Walter White and Jesse Pinkman's propane operation in his basement, on the condition that Walter plays Wii Sports with Moh on Thursdays. See The Basement Deal.

    Trivia

    • Scott the Woz is a real YouTuber. His Wiki article describes only the character in the book; that character shares his name and, evidently, his feelings about Wii games.
    • His “motif” is how the Border's colour is restored, which implies the Border responds to musical cues. Nobody else in the canon has had a motif.
    • He lives in Ohio, which is where the corner store also is. Fans have not yet drawn the map.
