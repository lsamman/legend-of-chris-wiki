    The Great Fortnite Island Wars

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Events

    The Great Fortnite Island Wars
    TypeWar
    LocationFortnite island (presumably)
    Notable veteransPlankton
    LegacyBattle-pass chanting by instinct
    Mentioned inChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Events

    The Great Fortnite Island Wars were a conflict in which Plankton fought as a veteran. They come up only once, when the possible arrival of Ninja sets off his old training:

    “Then out of instinct from the great Fortnite island wars of which plankton was a veteran of he chanted “Fornite battle pass, I just-“”

    He is cut off by TON 618's roar, so the rest of the chant has been lost.

    Analysis

    The book does not give the wars' dates, sides or outcome. Plankton's chant suggests the battle pass played a part, possibly a ritual one. That he still reacts by instinct suggests the wars left a lasting mark, and fans read it as war trauma. This is a fan theory.

    The wars add to Plankton's already crowded backstory, which includes being an orphan, being a strategist, carrying a metal folding chair, and appearing in “the hit show SpongeBob square pants”.

    Trivia

    • The chant echoes a well-known meme song about the Fortnite battle pass.
    • The book spells it “Fornite” here.
