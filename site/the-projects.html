    The Projects is a city-turned-nation in The Legend of Chris, introduced as "the famed city of 'the projects'". Ruled by Osama Bin Bom'in after he took it from MF Vroom, it is home to a gang army, a national spaceport, a national archive and a fleet of giant robots. Because its archives hold Facebook (Pre-Cringe), it is one of the most strategically important places in the fight against Chris.

    Geography

    The Projects lies in The United State of America (singular). MF Vroom stresses this point: he refused to join the EU "because the Projects are in the americas not Europe".

    Its heart is the city of I-38 West. Visitors arriving at the Projects National Spaceport approach through the treetops of Adirondack Park, during "a slow long turn, as fast as you could turn at mach 3". At street level, "Brick buildings and sketchy gas stations lined the roads." The city is overlooked by "giant defensive processing facilities that towered over the projects", one of which, DPF Facility 69420, "looked like something out of neon genesis evongelian".

    History

    Early history

    According to Son S. Sonionring, he and MF Vroom "worked together to bring the O'block to its glory". MF Vroom is also described as a "long lost relative of the project's royal family" (see The Projects Royal Family).

    The O'Block Coup

    Once O'Block "had a nation around it the frat bros rose up", led by Osama. When Vroom declined to join the EU, they "stormed his palace and gained complete control". See The O'Block Coup.

    Under Osama

    In Chapter 2, Osama uses the Projects as the base for planning the Attack on Moscow with Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire, and exiles MF Vroom. During "the O'block 2 era, when It was being scaled to be a EU level nation", Osama built the TON robots, including the flagship TON 618, "the projects flagship defense like Israel's iron dome but as one big robotic creature".

    The return

    In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, MF Vroom, RFK, Plankton, Ioannus Paranukus and Son land at the spaceport, flee Police Kirks in Son's 2005 Honda Accord, and head for O Block 2 while TON 618 wakes up. See The Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase.

    The gang army

    RFK's reason for recruiting MF Vroom is the Projects' gang army, "more powerful than gang wars fueled by a new parks of nikes". Restoring Vroom would put this army on the side of the New Crips.

    National archives

    "We have Facebook (Pre-Cringe) in the national archives.. Its been one of our prize possessions since the nations inception, we're not quite sure how it got here, but under a build building, in the rubble we found it on a 67gb micro sd card." Facebook (Pre-Cringe) is one of the apps required by the Prophecy to beat Chris. See The Projects National Archives and The 67GB MicroSD Card. (Moments later, someone calls it "the ssd", which it is not.)

    Trivia

    • Ioann-ius Nukian, the Jedi master met at Call of Duty HQ, is "of the projects".
    • The book ends with the heroes approaching the palace at chicken hour, when "I doubt the guards will be looking out for us".
    • Osama's ambassador for the country is Tung Tung Tung Sahur, who has a wooden body.
