    The Modded Colonial Musket

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    The Modded Colonial Musket
    OwnerSon S. Sonionring
    TypeColonial musket (modded)
    AmmunitionPellets
    Notable killTung Tung Tung Sahur (temporary)
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    The Modded Colonial Musket is the weapon of Son S. Sonionring. He takes it out at the Projects National Spaceport and cocks it before shooting Tung Tung Tung Sahur:

    “The musket pellet went right through his wooden body and incapacitated him.”

    The modifications are not described. Whatever they are, a single pellet goes straight through a wooden ambassador.

    Usage

    During the breakout the narration mentions “muskets firing”, in the plural. This suggests either more than one musket or one musket being fired very quickly. Muskets are the second most common firearm in the canon's Projects arc, behind RFK and Plankton's modded nerf guns, which they run back to the ship to fetch and then never use.

    Trivia

    • It is one of several old-fashioned weapons in the canon, along with lightsabers and Plankton's metal folding chair.
    • “Colonial” may be a nod to the colonial history of the United State of America. This is a fan theory.
