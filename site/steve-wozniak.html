    Steve Wozniak

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · The other Steve.

    Steve Wozniak
    AffiliationApple
    Known projectThe Inav
    PartnerSteve Jobs
    Appears in personNo
    First mentionThe Flashback Chapter
    Characters

    Steve Wozniak is mentioned once, by Steve Jobs: "the Inav was a project I started with Wozniak in the mid 80's, its why we hit our slump." He co-started the Inav, the multiverse navigator commissioned by the Department of Awesomeness.

    Notes

    • The real Steve Wozniak co-founded Apple with Jobs in 1976 and designed the Apple I and II.
    • Since Jobs continued developing the Inav until 2008 and it "only answers to me", Wozniak seems to have had no lasting access to the project. His current whereabouts are unknown.
