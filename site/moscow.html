    Moscow (Italian City)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · The Italian city of Moscow. Wine country, apparently.

    Moscow is described in The Legend of Chris as "the Italian city of Moscow", inhabited by "the italians of russia". It was the target of the Attack on Moscow, carried out by Lightning McQueen on behalf of Osama Bin Bom'in.

    Contents
    1. Controversy
    2. Aftermath
    3. Continuity errors

    Controversy

    "This city was recently wrapped in controversy for hosting the September 2002 McDonalds Wine Brewing contest." The contest led to DQ starting beef on MySpace with McDonald's. Because the Keepers of the MySpace Relics wanted the beef over, they "wanted to level Moscow to shut McDonald's up and let DQ win already."

    Aftermath

    After the attack, McQueen stayed nearby with George Constanta. His "work in Moscow" later becomes his reputation: Agent 007 tells him, "Ive heard stories about your work in Moscow I think your a Dope pick for the mission".

    Continuity errors

    • In Chapter 2 Moscow is an Italian city. In Chapter Triangle, McQueen leaves it "in the Soviet Union". Some editors say both are true, and the Soviet Union simply includes Italy now.
    • In the real world, Moscow is the capital of Russia and is not famous for McDonald's wine.
