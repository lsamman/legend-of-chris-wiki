    Chapters

    Chapter 2 is the second chapter of The Legend of Chris, and the first to break out of the Origin Litany. It moves from cosmology to geopolitics, introducing the book's three great early storylines: the aftermath of Chris's murder, the plot to bomb Moscow, and the Siege of the Galactic Senate. It also debuts many of the canon's central heroes, including RFK, MF Vroom and Lightning McQueen.

    It is also home to the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate, which occupies more than forty lines.

    Synopsis

    The DeSantis affair

    Chris is immediately "stabbed to death by Ron destantis claiming he was gay because he was a homo-sapien." Ron DeSantis then begins his quest to find the 73 Taco Bell locations, destined for glory by Trumpy the Red, a third party advocating Truth Social. A Redditor leaves a 0.5 score on IMDb, which is review-bombed by Joeius Bidenias, Roman emperor of Canada (Roman). Joe orders Ron to make Julian the native bird of the Island of Logic and Retoric; Ron refuses and turns to Zuckerberg and Musk to take back the Byzantine Empire of Mexico. During the war, the ethereal green dot exists for 0.0836 seconds.

    The war is interrupted when Medea emerges from her tesseract dimension ship. Zuckerberg summons Jason, who duels her in Donkey Kong Racing. Jason loses, but makes Medea search for Mic Q anyway, because "ha women". She is never seen again. Meanwhile Zarkmuckerberg retires at age 3, an event "completely irrelevant" to Mark Zuckerberg's life story.

    The Moscow plot

    Steve from Minecraft and Quagmire travel to the Projects to meet Osama Bin Bom'in, guided by MF Vroom, lost relative of the Projects Royal Family. At I-38 West, Osama exiles Vroom "to forever get yelled at in COD lobbies." The three, as Keepers of the MySpace Relics, resolve to level the Italian city of Moscow over the C-tier beef between DQ and McDonald's. Lightning McQueen, a Chick-fil-A loyalist and Knight of 4chan, is selected as the bombing Toyota Corolla, with Tyler from Uber as extraction. Osama is ambushed by QXC and Jinxy and simply ignores the mustard gas. McQueen ends up "blasted" and moves in with George Constanta.

    The Siege of the Senate

    Bon Jovi seizes the Galactic Senate on Planet Patrick in the Uniontown System, expelling the 80-year-old senators. Only RFK, a Dark Knight trained in the ways of the Jedi, remains, armed with four Red Bulls and a Lightsaber. Bon Jovi answers: "Nuh Uh." The debate never concludes within the chapter.

    Title & numbering

    "Chapter 2" is printed plainly, making it the only chapter in the book whose title is a normal chapter title. This lulls readers into a false sense of security that the next chapter immediately destroys. Since there is no Chapter 1, Chapter 2 is numbered correctly purely by accident: it comes after the Chapter Before Chapter 2, exactly as advertised.

    Continuity notes

    • The opening of this chapter already appeared, word for word, inside the fourth repetition of the Origin Litany.
    • MF Vroom is declared "never seen again". He is seen again in Chapter 4+.1+.1….
    • Lightning McQueen is described as a Toyota Corolla here. Later chapters bring in a Camry and a Honda Accord; see The Toyota Corolla Question.
    • Moscow is identified as an Italian city, and Osama's attack targets "the italians of russia."

    Trivia

    • The Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh exchange runs from line 369 to the very last line of the chapter (412), which ends mid-argument.
    • Medea and Jason come from Euripides' Greek tragedy Medea, here called "the hit play medea."
    • Quagmire is from Family Guy; Steve is the default player character of Minecraft.
