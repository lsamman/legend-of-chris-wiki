    Concepts & Lore

    Balling (also baller, balled) is the central metaphysical concept of the canon. It is something one can be (“Chris was a baby he was baller”), something one can do (“balled from the grave”), and a resource that can be hoarded. According to MF Vroom, Chris has done exactly that: “all that changed when chris took balling all for himself!”

    Contents
    1. As a force
    2. The liberation of balling
    3. Usage

    As a force

    Balling supports the structure of reality. In Gotham City, Steve Jobs explains why the city is disappearing: “beat drops can't exist without balling, it a natural evolution of hip-hop vibing.. even young Sheldon predicted this.” Frodo lost the ability permanently after eating the universe (“never to be baller again”).

    The liberation of balling

    The heroes' stated goal is to free it. Plankton and RFK set out as “Everyone's last hope of universalizing Balling.” ChatGPT tells Hank Hill that if Chris is defeated, “balling would be free.” Steve Jobs calls the quest “the journey to the court… The journey back to balling out for real for real…”

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, Jobs adds that, without autocorrect, “Even “baller” must be typed with intention,” and that the iPhone 3G “is how we ball.”

    Usage

    “Baller” is also the canon's standard term of approval: “Baller, so theres only one then?” (RFK), “this is baller man im deadass” (ChatGPT). Related terms include “the lost society of ballers,” “wacko ballers,” and the 8aller Jump Street spelling.
