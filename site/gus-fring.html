    Gus Fring

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Has a meeting. Allegedly.

    Gus Fring
    Known associatesHank Hill (meeting pending)
    Possible affiliationLos Polos Harmonicas (theory)
    AppearancesMentioned only
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Characters

    Gus Fring is mentioned exactly once. As Hank Hill climbed aboard the crack van to escape the DOD, he said: "I knew you'd be here, now I have meeting with Gus fring".

    The meeting never happens on-page, and Hank does not bring it up again. In Breaking Bad, Gus Fring runs the Los Pollos Hermanos chicken chain. Fans believe he is linked to Los Polos Harmonicas Under System Gang. This is a theory.
