    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Not as big as Dunder Mifflin, but still pretty chonky.

    Call of Duty HQ (also "COD HQ", "COD headquarters", "the Cod HQ") is a fortress "located deep into the heart of Dunder Mifflin", in aisle 3 of the vending machine section. It is where MF Vroom ended up after his exile to "forever get yelled at in COD lobbies", and where the DOD holds Snoopy McSpookintosh.

    Exterior

    "The hq was huge, not as big as Dunder Mifflin but still pretty chonky." RFK and Plankton landed on a landing platform in "a very dark heavy storm, only lights lit the way to the building, the winds and rain ran through the air as if they were one, the drops hit the ship as if they were bullets". Waves crash beneath the platform, so the HQ is apparently on an ocean, inside a vending machine, inside an office building, inside a galaxy.

    As the ship's stairs lowered, Plankton said "Insert and his name is john cena here" (see John Cena), and RFK "felt a disturbance in the Fabric in the Protestant universe". Then Ioann-ius Nukian smashed through the OpenAble Door.

    Interior

    Getting in requires a fingerprint scanner ("please put finger on scanner"), which Plankton ignores, walking "through the door with the fam because he answers to no one". RFK greets the room with "What's up my meninges!", and what they find is "madness":

    • "Pokemon everyone"
    • "gamers being yelled at for not capping red"
    • "xbox 360s red ringing in the middle of Cod tournaments"

    "The horrors were just insane no cap fr fr." Ioannus jumps into the battle with his white Lightsaber and disappears into the red mist, while RFK and Plankton run for the 360 Chambers. Ioannus also warns them about the Xbox Live Dungeons.

    In the 360 Chambers they find MF Vroom, who had "escaped the COD lobbies" and had been lost in the hallways for days. The group leaves through a door smothered in Dorito dust, runs along the outside of the base in a downpour of Pepsi and Dew, and takes off in the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser.

    Known prisoners

    • MF Vroom, exiled by Osama Bin Bom'in and rescued by the New Crips.
    • Snoopy McSpookintosh, said by the DOD to be "in our custody in the COD headquarters". The rescue party never mentions him, and as far as anyone knows he is still there.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: "capping red" means capturing an objective in a team game. "Red ringing" is the Xbox 360's infamous Red Ring of Death hardware failure.
    • The HQ's ecosystem includes an ocean, a storm, Pokémon, and pipes full of Mountain Dew.
