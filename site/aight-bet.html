    "Aight Bet"

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore

    "Aight Bet"
    TypeCatchphrase
    Most frequent userPlankton (2)
    Other usersMcQueen, Ioann-ius, ChatGPT
    First useChapter Triangle
    Latest useChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Concepts & Lore

    “Aight Bet” is the canon's standard way of agreeing to something, and arguably its motto. It is how missions start, how orders are taken, and how the most recent chapter ends.

    Contents
    1. Recorded uses
    2. Analysis
    3. Continuity

    Recorded uses

    • “Aight Bet” — Plankton, agreeing to RFK's plan to enlist MF Vroom, Chapter Triangle. “and off they went to search for the first member of the New Crips”.
    • “Aight bet” — Lightning McQueen, when told “Please pull up” at the Popeyes drive-through, Chapter the Fourth. He then takes out his Glock 19.
    • “aight bet” — Ioann-ius, accepting RFK and Plankton's confidence about the Xbox Live dungeons, Dunder Mifflan.
    • “Aight bet boss” — ChatGPT, taking the job of guarding the Verizon contract for Microsoft Bing, “with a wojack ahh face”, The Flashback Chapter.
    • “Aight bet” — Plankton again, as the last spoken line of Chapter 4+.1+.1…: “Plankton looked at RFK, RFK looked at Son, Son looked at MF Vroom, MF Vroom looked at Ioannius Paranukus. “Aight bet” Said plankton, and as the skies turned orange... their course was set.”

    Analysis

    Plankton says it both when the New Crips are formed and at the end of the latest chapter, so the main storyline begins and ends with it. Fans call these the two “Bets”.

    Continuity

    • Who says the Ioann-ius “aight bet” is unclear, since it follows straight on from “Nah we’ll be fine”. The wiki credits it to Ioann-ius.
    • ChatGPT agreed to guard the Verizon contract, but he was actually with Steve Jobs breaking in to steal it. The Aight Bet Boss problem is still unresolved.
    • Related words appear elsewhere: “Aight let me know when to pick you up” (McQueen), “aight now im going to have to make a song” (Kanye) and “Aight lets go”.
