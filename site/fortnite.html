    Fortnite

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    Fortnite
    TypeVideo game / currency / war zone
    Denominations$10 card, 19 dollar card
    VeteransPlankton
    CelebritiesNinja (possibly)
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Items & Technology

    Fortnite shows up across the canon more than almost any other video game except Call of Duty. It turns up as currency, as a way of celebrating, as a deadline, and as a war.

    Contents
    1. Appearances
    2. Continuity errors
    3. Spellings

    Appearances

    • $10 Fortnite cards. When Lightning McQueen arrives in England, he sees “glimmering lights and advertisements for free $10 Fortnite cards in the sky”.
    • The archives. In the lobby file cabinet of Dunder Mifflin, the documents include “WoW, Fortnite, The Bible of battle passes”. See The Bible of Battle Passes.
    • Fortnite emotes. After Kanye and ChatGPT's verses in the Battle of Club Shadowban, “The crowd exploded in Fortnite emotes.”
    • “Return when Fortnite ends.” At the end of the iPhone 3G Keynote, Steve Jobs rises into a hologram with this label.
    • The gift card. On the way to the casino, Steve announces it is “time to liberate the gift card.. 19 dollar fornite card, code: who wants it”. Siri later says “buy your battle passes”.
    • The lobby. In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, someone wonders whether Ninja aka Tyler Blevins has joined “this Fortnite lobby”.
    • The war. Plankton is a veteran of the Great Fortnite Island Wars and chants “Fornite battle pass, I just-”.

    Continuity errors

    • The value of a Fortnite card goes from $10 in England to 19 dollars in the flashback era, which is either inflation or a different card.
    • Steve calls the target of the heist a “19 dollar fornite card”, but the gift card actually recovered is the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon. Fans still argue about whether these are the same card.
    • “Return when Fortnite ends” suggests Fortnite will end one day. The canon has not said when.

    Spellings

    Fortnite, fornite, Fornite.
