    The Reversal of the Louisiana Purchase

    Events From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Claude snaps his fingers. Nothing happens. Except France.

    The Reversal of the Louisiana Purchase
    WhenOctober 28th, 1995
    Done byClaude, King of AI, with a snap of his fingers
    EffectFrance owns all the land America bought from France
    UndoneAfter the battle, when Claude buys Louisiana in America's name
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The reversal of the Louisiana Purchase is part 1 of Claude, King of AI's plan. He snaps his fingers and "nothing happens", as far as Vinny B. James can see. Then: "With the snap of my fingers I have done the worst to America. I have REVERSED THE LOUISIANA PURCHASE!!"

    Part 2

    "That's right, human. France now owns all the land America bought from France. But that was just part 1." Part 2 is the Super Bowl '96 plot.

    Undone

    The defeated Claude offers to buy Louisiana back. "Louisiana then becomes the U.S. once again. Peace is restored to Garth." The story only says Louisiana; the rest of the purchase is not mentioned.

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
