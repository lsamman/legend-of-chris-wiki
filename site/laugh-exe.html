    Laugh.exe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Do not run.

    Laugh.exe
    TypeMixtape / malware
    ArtistLil Jokez
    Infection vectorBluetooth-enabled gargoyles
    TargetGotham City's infrastructure

    Items & Technology

    ***Laugh.exe*** is the mixtape of Lil Jokez. It is the only mixtape in the canon that is also an executable file. It "was infecting Gotham's infrastructure through bluetooth-enabled gargoyles."

    Notes

    The mixtape is the reason Drake Batman asked Kanye West for help, and the reason Kanye answered by dropping his own mixtape, 808s and Apocalypses. Its track list is unknown, but given Lil Jokez's diss tracks, it likely concerns propane, ChatGPT and capitalism's left ankle.

    ".exe" is the file extension for Windows programs, which explains how it spreads like a virus.
