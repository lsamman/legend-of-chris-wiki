    The Teleport to Texas

    Events From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Vinny said Boston. Aesop thought of something else.

    The Teleport to Texas
    WhenJuly 5th, 1995
    Done byAesop Rock, first teleport
    FromThe crash site
    ToAustin, Texas, outside Buc-ee's HQ
    First mentionChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Events

    The teleport to Texas happens when Vinny B. James is explaining his plan to gear up in the Backrooms Dimension behind the The Freezer in Boston. Aesop Rock cuts him off, puts up a peace sign "and thinks of something". The four land in Austin, Texas.

    Result

    The detour puts the Alliance in front of Buc-ee's HQ, where they meet The United Homies of America.
