    Plankton is one of the main protagonists of The Legend of Chris. Known to MF Vroom as "Plankton from the hit show SpongeBob square pants", he has no last name, as he is an orphan. He recruits RFK to find Chris, co-leads the expedition into Dunder Mifflin to free MF Vroom, and answers to no one.

    Biography

    Recruiting RFK

    In Chapter Triangle, Plankton approaches RFK in Chai Town and introduces himself. He then tells RFK about "a Man so infamous that almost no one dares to utter his name… His Name…. Is Chris: All balled up Deluxe Edition."

    Plankton gives the canon's most detailed account of Chris's fate: "after his warcrimes in the galactic sentate his body was thought to be clones and thrown into a pit thought to have no end to it." Chris's soul stayed in the first body "because our all knowing overseers declared it the less wack one" (see The All-Knowing Overseers). Since "the crash of the housing market in 2007/2008" (The Housing Market Crash), Chris has been lost, but there are traces in the Reformed Travis Scott Sector, where he is buying a McDouble from an unknown planet.

    When RFK proposes MF Vroom, Plankton considers it and agrees: "Aight Bet". See "Aight Bet".

    Dunder Mifflin

    After a month-long trip, Plankton announces their arrival at Dunder Mifflin. His plan to get past the Google Smart Guards is to go "through the sign" (The Giant LED Sign), since its wires are "So big", "bigger than big". At Call of Duty HQ he says, "Insert and his name is john cena here" (see John Cena). When Ioann-ius Nukian confronts them, Plankton replies with the canon's most poetic speech: "Some might say we wish to free all chains that bind us, and some chains that blind us". At the fingerprint scanner, he "proceeds to walk through the door with the fam because he answers to no one."

    The 360 Chambers

    In Chapter 4+.1+.1…, ambushed by a shadowy figure, "Plankton drew his metal folding chair" and threw "his average sized metal chair" before learning it was MF Vroom. When a door appears, he notes, "Obama care did not reach this door". In the rain outside, he says "Bro let me get some of this" and "licks RFK's shoe in theory."

    The Projects

    Plankton comments on the cruiser's turn toward the Projects National Spaceport ("pretty dope pog if you ask me"). After Son shoots Tung Tung Tung Sahur, he says, "Well that sorts out that fat L issue". When a blue glow appears at DPF Facility 69420, his instincts as a veteran of the Great Fortnite Island Wars take over and he chants "Fornite battle pass, I just-". The book ends with Plankton saying "Aight bet" once again.

    Personality

    Plankton is calm, decisive, and above authority. He is the only character confirmed to walk through a fingerprint scanner without scanning. He has a talent for exposition and for short, final decisions.

    Trivia

    • Plankton's "no last name" line is his best-known quote, despite his last name never being asked for.
    • He says "Aight Bet" at the end of Chapter Triangle and at the end of Chapter 4+.1+.1…, which is also the end of the book.
    • In the real show, Plankton is a tiny villain who runs the Chum Bucket. The canon never mentions the Chum Bucket.
    • He yells at someone in the cruiser, "Bro lowkey why r u built like a 2008 Honda accord Zyn weed addition". See The Toyota Corolla Question.
