    Vegas

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Closed.

    Vegas
    TypeCasino (or city)
    Status"Closed"
    Mentioned byChatGPT
    Locations

    Vegas is mentioned once, by ChatGPT, when the Inav arrives at The 6 7 Casino. According to ChatGPT, the 6 7 Casino is one of the best it has seen "in a millennia, ever since that one in Vegas closed".

    The canon does not say which Vegas casino is meant, when it closed, or why. Since real-world Las Vegas has many casinos, ChatGPT's apparent belief that there was only "that one" has puzzled editors. Fan theory: it closed after losing its Field of Gambling relic.
