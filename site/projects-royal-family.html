    The Projects Royal Family

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Royals of the Projects, mostly in exile.

    The Projects Royal Family
    SeatThe Projects
    Known memberMF Vroom (long-lost relative)
    RivalOsama Bin Bom'in
    StatusDeposed
    First appearanceChapter 2
    Factions & Organizations

    The Projects Royal Family is the royal house of the Projects. It is mentioned once directly, in Chapter 2: MF Vroom "was a long lost relative of the project's royal family and was keen on returning to the top."

    History

    Nothing else is said about the family by name. However, later chapters strongly suggest royal loyalty is still alive. Ioann-ius Nukian, a Jedi "of the projects", calls MF Vroom "my king", and Son S. Sonionring describes Vroom's "palace" being stormed by Osama and his frat (see The O'Block Coup).

    Whether the family ruled before O'Block, or whether MF Vroom founded the dynasty while also being its long-lost relative, is a matter of debate. See MF Vroom for the continuity discussion.

    Trivia

    • MF Vroom is a car. It is unknown whether the rest of the family are also cars.
