    Litl Wayne

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Benefactor of the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser.

    Litl Wayne
    AffiliationThe Litl Empire
    Known forGifting Galaxy Gas
    Relationship to protagonistsGracious benefactor
    StatusUnknown
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Characters

    Litl Wayne "of the litl empire" is mentioned once, as the person who "graciously gifted" Galaxy Gas to RFK and Plankton. The gift gave their Mercedes Benz System Cruiser 40% more engine performance and made the trip to Dunder Mifflin possible.

    How he knows RFK and Plankton is not explained. Plankton earlier says Chris can be found "with the help of some former friends", and Litl Wayne may be one of them.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: a play on the rapper Lil Wayne. The "Litl" spelling is consistent and is used for his empire too. Compare "lilt uzi vert" in Chapter Triangle.
    • He is one of the few people in the canon who gives the heroes something without asking for anything back. Compare Hank Hill, who asks Bing for 15 gallons of oil.
