    Galaxy Gas

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · +40% engine performance. A gift.

    Galaxy Gas
    TypePropellant additive
    SourceLitl Wayne of the Litl Empire
    Used inMercedes Benz System Cruiser
    Effect40% more engine performance
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Items & Technology

    Galaxy Gas is the additive that made the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser capable of intergalactic travel. Mixed into the "liquid deuterium and gaseous methane propellant", it gave the engines "40% more performance". It was "graciously gifted to them by litl Wayne of the litl empire".

    Without it, RFK and Plankton could not have reached Dunder Mifflin, and MF Vroom would still be lost in the COD lobbies.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: Galaxy Gas is a brand of flavored nitrous oxide that became an internet controversy and meme in 2024. Nitrous oxide is also used as an engine performance booster in cars, which is presumably how it works here.
    • It is one of several gases in the canon, along with Propane and Ye the Gas.
