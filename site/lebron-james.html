    LeBron James

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Balled into the sky. Cannot abide cringe.

    Characters

    LeBron James is one of the four beings who balled into the sky with Chris to learn the secret teachings of social media, and the moral conscience of the early Multiverse, insofar as it had one.

    Contents
    1. History
    2. The Prophecy
    3. Trivia

    History

    Together with Chris, Elon Musk and Mark Zuckerberg, LeBron helped found Apple Podcasts and spread the word of Chris to the middle aged men.

    When Elon Mush accidentally created a universe of nazis, LeBron “saw this he was like nah bro thats cringe so he sent the baller army of the united states of a america to dab on the haters.” See The Dabbing of the Haters.

    After the Great Famine, “Elon musk then merged with lebron james to become samwise.” Samwise went on to create Chris, making LeBron one of Chris's two parents-by-fusion as well as one of his original sky companions.

    The Prophecy

    Despite having merged, LeBron is named separately in the Prophecy: “lebron james and elon musk and mark zuckerberg and Chris will fall.” Whether the Prophecy predates the merging or simply did not get the memo is unresolved.

    Trivia

    • LeBron is the only founding tech bro with no confirmed tech accomplishments beyond co-founding Apple Podcasts.
    • MF Vroom mentions playing Bronny (see Wemby, Curry & Bronny), LeBron's real-life son, though LeBron himself is not mentioned there.
    • He appears in all eight recitations of the Cycle of Chris.
