    MySpace

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Ancient platform. Relic. Battlefield of beef.

    MySpace
    TypeSocial media platform / sacred relic
    KeepersOsama, Steve, and Quagmire
    Sacred versionMySpace v1.3.3
    Runs oniPhone 3G
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Items & Technology

    MySpace is one of the five secret teachings that Chris learned in the sky, and the platform with the richest history in the canon. It hosted a fast-food war, has its own order of relic keepers, and is part of the Prophecy.

    Contents
    1. The DQ–McDonald's beef
    2. MySpace v1.3.3
    3. Trivia

    The DQ–McDonald's beef

    After Moscow hosted the September 2002 McDonalds Wine Brewing contest, “DQ starting beef on MySpace with Mac Donald's.” Osama, Steve and Quagmire, “being keepers of the MySpace relics they wasnt cool with this C tier beef,” and decided to level Moscow “to shut McDonald's up and let DQ win already.” See The DQ–McDonald's MySpace Beef and Keepers of the MySpace Relics.

    MySpace v1.3.3

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, “A single MySpace server cries in the back.” Steve Jobs names “MySpace v1.3.3” first among the Sacred Apps that only the iPhone 3G can run. The Prophecy also lists it first.

    Trivia

    • MySpace was the leading real-world social network in the mid-2000s. In this canon it has become an ancient religion with relics, which several editors find historically accurate.
