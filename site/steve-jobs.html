    Steve Jobs

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Turtleneck of algorithms and starlight. One more thing.

    Steve Jobs
    AliasesSteve Jawbs, Steves jobs
    AffiliationApple; The Boys
    VehicleThe Inav
    DeviceiPhone 3G
    WeaponsIgun, Ibeam, FaceTime laser
    Catchphrase"One more thing"
    StatusAlive (formerly a hologram)
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Characters

    Steve Jobs is a technologist, prophet and heist leader who arrives in the canon on top of a flying iPhone 3G. He is the mastermind behind the plan to defeat Chris using legacy technology, and the only person the Inav answers to.

    He joins Kanye West and ChatGPT after the Battle of Club Shadowban and leads them through the Casino Heist to recover the 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon.

    Arrival at Club Shadowban

    When 808s and Apocalypses dropped, a wormhole opened across all multiverses and a glowing sideways iPhone 3G flew out of it. "Standing atop it, wearing a turtleneck spun from algorithms and starlight, was Steve Jobs himself." His voice "echoed with .wav clarity."

    Ye. GPT. I heard the drop. It's time for the final collab.

    He lifted the iPhone "like Mufasa presenting Simba", causing apps to download themselves onto flip phones across the cosmos, and "The Shadow Realm wept." He closed with "One more thing... Let's end this."

    The Keynote (flashback)

    The The Flashback Chapter shows Steve's earlier iPhone 3G Keynote in Reality 42-B, wearing "a black turtleneck woven from Mark Zuckerberg's abandoned dreams." There he unveiled the iPhone 3G as the answer to Chris, consulted Siri 3G, and then "ascends into a hologram labeled 'Return when Fortnite ends.'" Fortnite is not known to have ended, so his return is either early or a sign that something has happened to Fortnite.

    Gotham and the Inav

    Back in the present, Kanye shouted "Steve Jawbs?!" Steve explained: "I felt a disturbance in the iCloud I had to come here right away." Examining the empty club, he blamed the USB drive for sending the room to the Shadow Realm and summoned the Inav, which he had started with Wozniak in the mid 80's for the Department of Awesomeness. On boarding he "parked his zyn in the zyn storage locker" (Zyn).

    When Gotham began disappearing, Steve offered his theory: "beat drops can't exist without balling... even young Sheldon predicted this how could Chris be so blind" (Young Sheldon). He then ordered Siri to take them to the casino: "its time to liberate the gift card."

    The Casino Heist

    At Sector 1/99, Steve briefed the crew that the Verizon contract "Resides here" and that getting it would start "the journey back to balling out for real for real." He led the way along the casino walls and produced his iPhone "from his Ipouch in his Ibag from his Ipocket." He noted that "Ever since Tim apple retired ive had to source all my gadgets locally.. this new kid can't be trusted.. but ill explain that later" (Tim Apple). He has not explained it later.

    With ChatGPT plugged in, he cut into the wall with the Ultra Mega Uber Cool FaceTime Laser. Facing the Charlie Kirks guarding the lower chambers, he announced "its time to count gang violence" and incapacitated the first with his Igun. After the truce with Hank Hill's crew, he placed an AirTag on the vault door and said "Hey Siri.. fire the Ibeam." As the Ibeam fired, he remarked: "Yess.. perfection, just like the iPod touch.. that thing cooked eggs while playing geometry dash" (The iPod Touch).

    The TurboGrafx problem

    Aboard the Inav, sitting in "his futuristic pilots seat on the second floor main deck", Steve laid out the next step: travel to the TurboGrafx 16 sector to "sync with the flip phone covenant" (The Flip Phone Covenant). The catch is that the covenant now uses 5G, which would awaken Elon Mush's dormant twin. His plan is to "bypass the G" and roll back to 3G "right when we need to activate it." When GPT objected, Steve said: "Its not like we have a choice GPT, its just how it'll have to go…."

    Trivia

    • The real Steve Jobs co-founded Apple and died in 2011. "One more thing" was his famous keynote catchphrase, and the iPhone 3G was released in 2008, the same year he stopped developing the Inav.
    • He is the second character to sense "a disturbance" in something, after RFK felt one in the Protestant Universe.

    Continuity errors

    • Kanye "shouted" Steve's name and then "was speechless".
    • The chapter containing Steve's flashback is titled as being "all a flashback", but only the keynote section is one.
    • Steve says he has to source gadgets locally since Tim Apple retired, but the Igun, Ibeam, AirTag and FaceTime laser are all clearly Apple products.
