    The iPhone 3G is Steve Jobs's phone and the central technology of the plan to defeat Chris. It is sideways, it is cracked, and it plays U2 on loop. According to Steve, "This is not a phone. This is an anti-deity protocol."

    Description

    At the iPhone 3G Keynote, the iPhone 3G descended through a portal as "a familiar glowing rectangle." "It's sideways. It's cracked. It plays U2 on loop. Everyone weeps." Steve introduced it to the "Ladies, gentlemen, wacko ballers" in attendance as predating TikTok, having survived the downfall of Vine, and connecting only to God's router.

    Specifications

    The keynote lists the following reasons "Why the iPhone 3G Matters":

    • 3G Speed: "Because 4G is for the weak, and 5G awakens Elon Mush's dormant twin." See 5G and Elon Mush.
    • No Front Camera: "To avoid self-reflection. Literally."
    • Sacred App Compatibility: It is "the only phone capable of running" MySpace v1.3.3, Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition, Facebook (Pre-Cringe) and Twitter Bluebird Variant (with no limits). See The Sacred Apps.
    • No Auto-Correct: "You are forced to mean what you say. Even 'baller' must be typed with intention."

    It also comes with Siri 3G, who advises: "never update".

    History

    The phone first appears in the present day when 808s and Apocalypses dropped: "Out of it flew a glowing sideways iPhone 3G, pulsing with raw nostalgia," with Steve standing on top of it. Lifted "like Mufasa presenting Simba", it caused apps to download themselves onto flip phones across the cosmos.

    Afterwards Steve uses it as an all-purpose tool. He used it to examine Club Shadowban, to summon the Inav via Siri, as the emitter for the FaceTime laser (with ChatGPT plugged in), and to fire the Ibeam. He keeps it in his Ipouch, in his Ibag, in his Ipocket. In the TurboGrafx 16 sector he began "entering the gift card code" from the Verizon contract into it.

    The 5G problem

    The Flip Phone Covenant now uses 5G, and connecting the iPhone to it risks waking Elon Mush's twin. Steve's workaround is to "bypass the G by loading a social media first" and roll back to 3G "Right when we're in Chris's face."

    Trivia

    • The real iPhone 3G (2008) did lack a front camera. One arrived with the iPhone 4 in 2010, which makes this one of the canon's most accurate statements.
    • "Plays U2 on loop" refers to Apple automatically adding a U2 album to every iTunes library in 2014.
    • The Prophecy does not name the iPhone. It only requires "a phone capable of containing" the Sacred Apps, Apple Podcasts 2, Settings (Full access) and the Verizon contract.

    Continuity errors

    • In Chapter Triangle, Plankton says the only person able to battle Chris is one who has downloaded the sacred apps onto a "nokia I-4500 flip phone with android wireless technology." The canon has therefore named two different chosen phones, one by Nokia and one by Apple.
    • Steve calls it "the only phone capable" of running the apps, yet in the same scene apps download themselves onto flip phones across the cosmos.
