    Concepts & Lore

    Unc Standard Time (UST) is the time standard used to record the Fall of Constantinople: "Constantinople fell at 14:74pm Unc standard time".

    UST seems to allow hours past 12 together with "pm", and minutes up to at least 74. "Unc" is slang for an older man (short for "uncle"), so some editors believe UST runs on an older man's sense of time. It is the canon's only named time zone.
