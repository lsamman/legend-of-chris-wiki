    Yeat

    Characters From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · The real twizzy, in his own opinion. God disagreed.

    Yeat
    PartnerQuandale Dingle
    WeaponInfinity blades
    VehicleA custom Lambo Tonka
    WantsThe iPhone 3G
    StatusHas a hole through him, and so does the Earth
    First appearanceChapter Number New Jersey
    Characters

    Yeat is one of the two attackers who jump the gang in Salty Springs in Chapter Number New Jersey. With Quandale Dingle he demands the iPhone 3G. He ends the chapter with a hole going completely through him.

    Contents
    1. The ambush
    2. The fight with Kanye
    3. Trivia

    The ambush

    “The one and only Yeat and Quandale Dingle jumped the gang with infinity blades.” Yeat's contribution to the threat is “Thas right twizzy”. A custom lambo tonka drives up behind them, and they say: “Give us the iphone and we'll be on our way.” See the Salty Springs Ambush.

    The fight with Kanye

    After Plankton spends all his power on Quandale, Kanye West takes Yeat. They fight “fist to fist”; Yeat leaps onto his Tonka and Kanye follows. On top of the Tonka, Kanye blocks his fist and tells him his problem: “you think your the real twizzy when in reality jesus is king”. A beam of light from the great beyond then makes “a hole going completely through yeat and the earth”. See the Smiting of Yeat.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: Yeat is a rapper. “Twizzy” is his word.
