    NFTs are digital assets that appear twice in the canon, both in The Flashback Chapter.

    Contents
    1. Hank's mysterious apes
    2. The NFT collector
    3. Trivia

    Hank's mysterious apes

    When Jesse Pinkman asked "anyone got some nft's on their phone?" so the crew could gamble at the oil casino, Hank Hill revealed he had "some mysterious apes on my bitcoin Wallet". He returned from the RV with what he called "a nice token right here". The crew then bet "Several NFTs on red!" at the Great Roulette Bet, whose result was never revealed.

    The NFT collector

    During Steve Jobs's iPhone 3G Keynote, light refracting off his glasses bounced into a wormhole and blinded "an NFT collector in Universe Prime". Fan theory: some readers believe this collector was Hank, which would explain why he describes his own NFTs only as "mysterious".

    Trivia

    • "Mysterious apes" refers to the real-world fad of ape-themed NFT collections.
    • It is unclear how a roulette table accepts NFTs, but within the Field of Gambling anything is presumably possible.
