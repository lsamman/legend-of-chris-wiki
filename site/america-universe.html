    The America Universe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Conqueror of universes. Enjoyer of funny ones.

    The America Universe
    ConquestsThe Cringe Universe; “over 2 universes”
    CovetedThe Minor Miner Universe
    AlliesElon Musk
    OutcomeCivil war (the bad ones won)
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Locations

    The America Universe is the imperial power of the early Multiverse. It took over the Cringe Universe in the Great Oppression, and then: “But the america yerned for the minor miner universe because it was funny so together with elon musk they took over over 2 universes after which a civil war broke out and then the bad ones won.”

    Analysis

    The America Universe is the only power in the canon whose foreign policy is driven by finding things funny. Editors note that “yerned” (yearned) is spelled the same way in all eight recitations. It is unclear whether the America Universe is connected to the United State of America where the Projects are, or to the “united states of a america” of the Baller Army.
