    The Multiverse Civil War
    CauseAmerica and Elon Musk taking over “over 2 universes”
    VictorThe bad ones
    AftermathZuckerberg's Snap
    Events

    The Multiverse Civil War broke out after the America Universe and Elon Musk “took over over 2 universes.” The entire account of the war reads: “a civil war broke out and then the bad ones won.”

    The sides, the reasons, and who the bad ones were are not recorded. The war ended when Mark Zuckerberg made the result irrelevant by sending every universe but his favourite to the Shadow Realm.
