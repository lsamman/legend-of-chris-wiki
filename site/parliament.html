    Parliament

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Special reasons.

    Parliament
    LocationEngland
    Known agentsAgent 007, Mr. Bean
    InterestsSmoke detectors (for "special reasons")
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Factions & Organizations

    Parliament is the governing body that Lightning McQueen had "a contact" from, which led him to fly to England. Its field agents appear to be Agent 007 and Mr. Bean.

    Before the trip, McQueen received a pager from a stranger instructing him "to hoard smoke detectors for parliament because of 'special reasons'". What Parliament wants with the smoke detectors is never explained. Editors have noted that its agents meet in a vape shop, so the smoke detectors may be a defensive measure.

    Trivia

    • It is never said which country's parliament this is. England is assumed because that is where McQueen went.
