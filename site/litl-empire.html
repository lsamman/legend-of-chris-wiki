    The Litl Empire

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Small name. Generous gas policy.

    The Litl Empire
    TypeEmpire
    RulerLitl Wayne
    Known exportsGalaxy Gas (as a gift)
    AlliesRFK and Plankton (apparently)
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Factions & Organizations

    The Litl Empire is the realm of Litl Wayne, who gave RFK and Plankton the Galaxy Gas that powers their Mercedes Benz System Cruiser. It appears in exactly one clause: "litl Wayne of the litl empire".

    Nothing is known about its size, location or government. Given the name, editors assume it is small.

    Trivia

    • It is one of many empires in the canon, along with the Barack Empire, the Byzantine Empire of Mexico, Eastern Rome, and Hank Hill's propane empire. It is the only one known to have done anything nice.
