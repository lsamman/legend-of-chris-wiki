    Kromer

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    Kromer
    TypeCurrency
    Used byAlpha Mogger Beta, Spamton
    Spelling[kromer]
    First mentionChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    Kromer, always written in brackets as [kromer], is the currency Alpha Mogger Beta earned from its XP candy operation:

    “we made so much [kromer] from that man it was the high life”

    It joins a canon economy that also runs on dollar-denominated Fortnite cards and NFTs.

    Behind the scenes

    • In Deltarune, Kromer is the currency of Spamton G. Spamton, and it is written in brackets because that is how he talks. In the canon MF Vroom puts it in brackets as well, so it seems Spamton's style has rubbed off on him.
    • The exchange rate between kromer and Projects money is unknown.
