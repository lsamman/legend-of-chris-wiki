    The OpenAble Door™

    The OpenAble Door™ is a door at Call of Duty HQ, described in full as "the extremely openable (trademark pending) OpenAble Door". It is mentioned only once, when Ioann-ius Nukian makes his entrance: "A man smashed through the extremely openable (trademark pending) OpenAble Door".

    The door was extremely openable, and Ioannus smashed through it anyway. Scholars disagree on whether this shows his commitment to drama or a design flaw.

    Trivia

    • Since the trademark is still pending, the ™ in this article's title may be premature.
    • Other notable doors in the canon include the Dorito-dust door of the 360 Chambers ("Obama care did not reach this door"), the Port-a-Potty Portal, and the vault door that the Ibeam went through.
