    Dunder Mifflin (Galaxy)

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · A galaxy. In an office building. Mostly paper.

    Dunder Mifflin (Galaxy)
    TypeGalaxy (fully enclosed in an office building)
    Alternate spellingsDunder Mifflan, dunder mifflin sector
    Entry pointsThousands
    SecurityGoogle Smart Guards
    Notable featuresThe Paper Belt, the Giant LED Sign, the Lobby File Cabinet, the Vending Machine Labyrinth, Call of Duty HQ
    First mentionedChapter Triangle
    Locations

    Dunder Mifflin (also spelled Dunder Mifflan) is a galaxy that "had been completely encapsulated in a giant dunder mifflan office building". It contains Call of Duty HQ, where MF Vroom is held, and is the destination of RFK and Plankton's month-long trip in the Mercedes Benz System Cruiser. A chapter is named after it.

    Description

    On arrival, Plankton announces "Alright RFK we're here.. Dunder Mifflan.." RFK replies "Dear god.. its huge there's so much.. paper.." Plankton: "Precisely.."

    The building has "thousands of entry points, all of which were guarded heavily, by what? By Google Smart guards of course." Its outer features include:

    • The Paper Belt, a ring the ship passes through on approach.
    • The Giant LED Sign, whose wires are so big that a ship can slip through them without the guards noticing.

    Inside are:

    • The lobby, home of the File Cabinet, which holds documents on Call of Duty, WoW, Fortnite and The Bible of battle passes.
    • The vending machine section, entered "through the crack in the door of the machine", which leads to the Labyrinth.
    • Aisle 3, location of Call of Duty HQ, which is "not as big as Dunder Mifflin but still pretty chonky".

    Infiltration

    With the Smart Guards "everywhere", Plankton chose to go in through the sign:

    "How big are they?" "So big" "like big big" "bigger than big" "baller"

    The ship "slowly passed through The paper belt and then slipped behind the giant LED sign, as the ship hugged the wires is slowly entered.." It then flew straight into the lobby and browsed the file cabinet, which suggests the lobby is big enough for a spaceship.

    Departure

    After rescuing MF Vroom, the crew "flew back out of the dunder mifflin sector" toward the United State of America. In one sentence, Dunder Mifflin is called a galaxy, an office building and a sector.

    Trivia

    • Real-world: Dunder Mifflin is the paper company in the TV sitcom The Office. The giant amount of paper is consistent with that.
    • The spelling switches between "Mifflin" (planning and departure) and "Mifflan" (arrival and the chapter title). The wiki uses "Mifflin" for the galaxy and "Mifflan" for the chapter.
    • No employees of Dunder Mifflin are ever seen.
