    The Stairway to Heaven

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · It goes down, actually.

    The Stairway to Heaven
    LocationThe 6 7 Casino
    TypeSide staircase
    Leads toThe Vault and the relic
    Named byMicrosoft Bing
    Locations

    The Stairway to Heaven is a side staircase off the main casino room of The 6 7 Casino, leading down to the Vault containing the Verizon contract. Microsoft Bing led Hank's crew to it, and they "did the crip walk slowly down the stairs" with their casino-issued scars and red bulls.

    Despite its name, the stairway goes down. Wiki editors have noted that, given the relic is the source of all gambling in the casino, this may be the point. The name references the famous Led Zeppelin song.

    It is likely the same "side door" stairwell that the Boys "quickly scurried down" during the Casino Heist, and through which the Charlie Kirks later poured in.
