    Locations

    O'Block, also written O Block and in its later form called O Block 2, is the grand apartment complex at the centre of the Projects, and in a sense the nation itself. It is the home of Osama Bin Bom'in, who took it over in the O'Block Coup. At the end of Chapter 4+.1+.1… it is where the heroes are heading.

    History

    The golden age

    According to Son S. Sonionring, he and MF Vroom “worked together to bring the O’block to its glory. But our success was short lived, Once O’block had a nation around it the frat bros rose up”. MF Vroom lived there in a palace with a porch.

    The coup

    Osama and his Pol Phi Doros frat came to MF Vroom's porch and demanded that O'Block join the EU. MF Vroom refused because “the Projects are in the americas not Europe”, so they stormed the palace and took complete control. See The O'Block Coup.

    The O'Block 2 era

    Under Osama, O'Block entered “the O’block 2 era, when It was being scaled to be a EU level nation”. In this period Osama built the TON robots, including TON 618.

    Features

    • The grand apartment complex where Osama lives.
    • The palace, where “The ssd would still be”. This is the 67GB MicroSD card holding Facebook (Pre-Cringe).
    • The Projects National Archives, which, according to MF Vroom and Son, also holds Facebook (Pre-Cringe). Whether the archives are inside the palace is unclear.
    • Towering defensive processing facilities, including DPF Facility 69420.

    Current status

    At the end of Chapter 4+.1+.1…, the heroes have decided not to retake the Projects outright (“the TON’s are going to level the city with us chuds in it”). Instead they plan to sneak in during chicken hour, take Facebook, and “dip before we start tweaking twin.” The last line of the canon so far is “Computah locate Facebook…”

    Behind the scenes

    • O'Block is the nickname of a real housing block on Chicago's South Side, made famous by drill music.
    • O Block 2 is never referred to as a sequel in the in-universe sense. Nobody mentions an O Block 1 except through the “O’block” of the golden age.
