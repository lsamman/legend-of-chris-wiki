    The All-Knowing Overseers

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Arbiters of wackness.

    The All-Knowing Overseers
    TypeOverseers (all-knowing)
    Known rulingChris's first body is "the less wack one"
    Mentioned byPlankton
    First mentionedChapter Triangle
    Factions & Organizations

    The All-Knowing Overseers are a body of unknown membership whose only recorded act is deciding where Chris's soul lives. After Chris's body was cloned (see Chris's War Crimes & the Cloning), Plankton explains that "his soul remained in the first one because our all knowing overseers declared it the less wack one."

    Plankton says "our all knowing overseers", which suggests he answers to them, or at least claims them. The overseers are never mentioned again. Despite being all-knowing, they apparently lost track of Chris after the housing market crash of 2007/2008.

    Trivia

    • Wackness is the only metric the overseers are known to use.
    • Fan theory: The overseers are the same group as the "all knowing" tech bros. Nothing supports this.
