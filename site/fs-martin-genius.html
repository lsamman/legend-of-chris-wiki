    FS Martin Genius is a mysterious informant who intercepted Kanye West and ChatGPT in the alley outside Club Shadowban and supplied them with the Zuck Tears USB.

    Contents
    1. Appearance
    2. Role in the story
    3. Trivia

    Appearance

    FS Martin Genius is described as "a hunched figure with a cracked Bluetooth headset and a trench coat made of Reddit tabs." His voice was "glitching in 144p", making him the only character in the canon whose voice has a video resolution.

    Role in the story

    He opened with "Yo. You Ye?" and, after Kanye asked "Who's askin'?", introduced himself as an App Whisperer who once beta tested Snapchat in the nether dimension. ChatGPT "gasped in XML" and recognised him as the one "who made Vine loop in reverse in Timeline R" (Timeline R, Vine). He answered: "Guilty."

    He then "flicked a cracked Motorola Razor" and showed them "an ancient gif of Zuck crying in a Chili's", before handing Kanye a USB stick labelled 'Zuck Tears (Do Not Install)'. His instructions: "Run it on stage. Jokez's autotune can't survive legacy formats."

    "And with that, FS Martin Genius vaped into a drain." He has not been seen since.

    Trivia

    • The Motorola Razr was a hugely popular flip phone in the mid-2000s, which may make FS Martin Genius an associate of the Flip Phone Covenant (fan theory).
    • The "Zuck" in the gif is presumably Mark Zuckerberg. Chili's is a real restaurant chain.
    • Whether the USB stick he gave out actually helped is disputed: Steve Jobs later suspects it is what sent the club to the Shadow Realm.
