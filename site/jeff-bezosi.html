    Jeff Bezosi

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Co-divider of the DOD.

    Jeff Bezosi
    AffiliationDepartment of Defense
    Co-leaderMark Zucc
    First appearanceChapter the Fourth
    Characters

    Jeff Bezosi is mentioned exactly once, in Chapter the Fourth. The Department of Defense is “a army divided by Jeff bezosi and Mark zucc.”

    He takes no further action in the canon. His name is generally taken as a nod to the Amazon founder, with an extra “i” in keeping with the book's habit of Latinizing names, as with Joeius Bidenias.
