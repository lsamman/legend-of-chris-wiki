    Why Do We Believe What We Believe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Concepts & Lore · The opening incantation.

    Why Do We Believe What We Believe
    TypeIncantation / creed
    Answer“Its a matter of Chris.”
    RecitedTwice
    First appearanceChris Origins
    Concepts & Lore

    Why Do We Believe What We Believe is the creed that opens The Legend of Chris. It poses the fundamental epistemological question and answers it in five words: “Its a matter of Chris.”

    Occurrences

    The incantation appears twice. It opens the book (with a large drop-cap “W” separated from “hy” by several spaces), and it reappears in the middle of the Cycle immediately after the leaked Chapter 2 passage, as if the universe were rebooting itself. In both places it leads straight into “Chris was a baby.”

    Analysis

    The sentence contains six “believe”s and no punctuation until the end. Editors have tried to diagram it and failed.
