    Characters

    Bon Jovi is a character in The Legend of Chris who conquered the Galactic Senate on Planet Patrick in the Uniontown system, and was then defeated by RFK after the longest argument in the canon. He shares a name with the real-world rock band and its frontman; the wiki documents only the canon version.

    Biography

    The siege

    "Meanwhile Bon Jovi has taken the galactic senate on the planet of Patrick in the Uniontown system. The battle was long and hard but Bon Jovi eventually had pushed out all of the 80 year old senators." See The Siege of the Galactic Senate.

    The debate

    One senator remained: RFK, "a Dark Knight", who told him, "It's over Bon Jovi I have 4 Red Bulls and a lightsaber, no one can mess with me I'm cool". "Bon Jovi looked around and proclaimed 'Nuh Uh'", starting the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate, which lasts the rest of Chapter 2.

    Death

    Chapter Triangle opens: "Bon Jovi Died at the hands of RFK." How the debate became a death is not explained. Most scholars assume RFK won the argument.

    Trivia

    • Bon Jovi says "Nuh Uh" 202 times, more than any other line in the canon is spoken.
    • He is the only character whose last words are recorded about 200 times.
    • The Projects' giant robot TON 618 stands for "Terrible Offensive Nuhuh". No link to Bon Jovi has been confirmed, but fans have noticed.
