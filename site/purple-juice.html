    Solidified Purple Juice

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology

    Solidified Purple Juice
    TypeAmmunition
    FormPellets
    Used inSon's 2005 Honda Accord
    EffectPops tires
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Items & Technology

    Solidified purple juice is the ammunition of the trunk-mounted machine gun in Son's 2005 Honda Accord. During the O'Block chase it fires “Little pellets of solidified purple juice” at the Police Kirks, which “popped all of the tires”.

    How the juice is solidified, what flavour it is, and whether it can be turned back into juice are not covered by the canon.

    Fan theories

    • Because the juice is purple and “I Can't Put Down the Cup” is playing at the same moment, some fans link it to the lean that Drake Batman sips from his utility belt. This is a fan theory.
    • Others link it to Koolaid College, even though that is the enemy's school. This is a fan theory.
