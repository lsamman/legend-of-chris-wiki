    Yuno Miles

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · The last rapper. Immortal. Historically ass.

    Yuno Miles
    OccupationRapper
    StatusImmortal
    DistinctionLast rapper (after the Gawk-ination of 2039)
    Known forThe first verse-wide tour
    FanbaseA cult following
    First appearanceChapter the Maybe Seventh
    Characters

    Yuno Miles is a rapper whose music the Boys blast "In their earth pods pro .5's" as they sneak up on The 6 7 Casino. Kanye, apparently unfamiliar, asks "The fuq is this kid bro".

    Biography

    According to Steve Jobs, "after the gawk-ination of 2039 he was the last rapper and he gained immortality. Afterwards he got a cult following and started touring the universe, doing the very first verse-wide tour. Gangs from all over bought tickets for him, and eventually the verse-wide tour cemented him as a historically ass artist. That's why his music is still prominent even today."

    Kanye's response was "The fuck does that mean Kobe Bryant".

    Yuno Miles's music is still audible later in the chapter: Hank's crew wake "To the noise of alarms, truth social notifs and yuno miles", suggesting the Boys did not turn it down during the break-in.

    Trivia

    • Yuno Miles is a real-world rapper known online for intentionally absurd lyrics.
    • Kanye, himself a rapper, does not recognise the last rapper. Fans attribute this to the story taking place before 2039 for Kanye, though Steve speaks of 2039 in the past tense. This is either a continuity error or time travel.
    • "Historically ass" is a direct quote and should not be corrected.
