    TON 618

    Characters

    TON 618 (“Terrible Offensive Nuhuh”) is a giant bio-robotic war machine housed in DPF Facility 69420. It is the flagship of Osama Bin Bom'in's TON robots and the main defence of the Projects. Son S. Sonionring calls it the most infamous of them all. At the end of Chapter 4+.1+.1… it activates and starts searching the city for the heroes.

    Description

    Son introduces it while the gang drives toward O'Block:

    “In the begining of the O’block 2 era, when It was being scaled to be a EU level nation, Osama Bin’ Bombin created monolithically sized defensive bio-robotic weapons robots.. the most infamous of which is.. the one in that facility, facility 69420, TON six eighteen. It stands for Terrible Offensive Nuhuh. Its the projects flagship defense like Israel’s iron dome but as one big robotic creature.. yall we might be screwed, Osama does not want us here”

    TON 618 has the following known features:

    • A faint blue glow, visible from far away.
    • A “giant robotic roar” that can be heard “from horizon to horizon”.
    • Footsteps that make the ground rumble.
    • A “large red electronic eye, maybe not a eye, more like a giant spotlight” that sweeps the buildings and roads.

    Activation

    The faint glow from the facility first prompts the question of whether Ninja aka Tyler Blevins has joined the Fortnite lobby. Then the roar sounds and the doors of the DPF open. At the end of the chapter TON 618 is walking through the city “looking for the Camry”, while robotic screeches are heard “from horizon to horizon”.

    Continuity errors

    • The Camry. TON 618 is searching for “the Camry”, but the heroes are driving Son's 2005 Honda Accord. The book never says whether this is an error in the book or in TON 618's targeting. Either way the robot is looking for the wrong car. The canon's other car mix-ups are listed under the Toyota Corolla Question.
    • Offensive defence. TON 618 is described as a defensive system, yet the name stands for Terrible Offensive Nuhuh.

    Trivia

    • In real life, TON 618 is one of the largest known black holes. Within the canon the name is also used as an oath: Spamton says “for the love of [TON 618]” earlier in the same chapter.
    • “Nuhuh” recalls Bon Jovi's famous answer in the Great Nuh Uh–Yuh Huh Debate. Fans have suggested the robot was named in its honour. This is a fan theory.
    • The comparison to Israel's Iron Dome missile-defence system comes from Son.
