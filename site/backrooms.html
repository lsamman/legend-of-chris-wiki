    The Backrooms

    Locations From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · A storage room full of Double Whoppers with Cheese. Staffed by a forklift guy.

    The Backrooms
    EntranceA wall inside the The Freezer, Burger King, Boston
    ResidentsGarret the Forklift Guy, a black cat
    ContentsBoxes of Double Whoppers with Cheese, a The Forklift
    Exit usedNo-clipping, with FRIEND
    First appearanceChapter 1: Beginnings
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 1: Beginnings, Chapter 3: The Age of Claude
    Locations

    The Backrooms are the room reached through the The Freezer wall. It is "filled to the brim with boxes of Double Whoppers with Cheese", and has a forklift and a forklift guy.

    Events

    Vinny B. James and Tyrone use the The Forklift (The Forklift Incident), see a black cat (The Cat in the Box), and are carried through the Backrooms by FRIEND before FRIEND no-clips into a wall (FRIEND's Abduction).

    On the spin-off wiki

    This article is canon in the Legend Of Chris universe. It comes from the spin-off The Epic of Vinny. Read the full article on The Epic of Vinny Wiki.
