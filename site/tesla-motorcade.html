    The Tesla-Sponsored Motorcade

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Items & Technology · Ready for departure.

    The Tesla-Sponsored Motorcade
    SponsorTesla
    Departure pointE.C.S.M Airport
    PassengersThe Goober Gang (presumably)
    First appearanceChapter Triangle
    Items & Technology

    The Tesla-Sponsored Motorcade is the transportation Mr. Bean arranged for the mission to Constantinople. It was "ready for departure from E.C.S.M Airport", and it is the last thing mentioned before the gang departs.

    The motorcade is never seen again, and the gang is not shown arriving in Constantinople. Tesla is associated with Elon Musk, so the sponsorship may tie the mission to one of the canon's founding figures. The text does not say this.
