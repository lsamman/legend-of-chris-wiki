    The Laid-Off Workers

    Factions & Organizations From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Fired because of AI. Came together. Banished it.

    The Laid-Off Workers
    Members"All of the people who got laid-off or fired because of AI"
    AchievementThe Banishing of the AI
    PlanetGarth
    First mentionChapter 1: Beginnings
    Year1972
    SeriesThe Epic of Vinny (spin-off)
    Told inChapter 1: Beginnings, Chapter 2: The Weird Route
    Factions & Organizations

    The Laid-Off Workers are the people who lost their jobs to AI and, being "pretty pissed", "came together to banish it" in the Little Saint James Sea.

    Consequences

    The banishment is why Claude, King of AI hates humans ("they trapped me down here"). Vinny B. James answers, "Womp Womp. I'm glad we did." Claude's plan to destroy their world is the main conflict of Chapters 2 and 3 (The Age of Claude).

    On the spin-off wiki
