    Police Kirks

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Factions & Organizations · Kirks, but cars.

    Police Kirks
    Parent factionCharlie Kirks
    LocationThe Projects National Spaceport, the Projects
    AbilityTransform into police cars
    WeaknessSolidified purple juice
    First appearanceChapter 4+.1+.1…
    Factions & Organizations

    Police Kirks are a variant of Charlie Kirks who serve as law enforcement at the Projects National Spaceport. During the Spaceport Breakout & O'Block Chase, they pursued RFK, Plankton, Ioannus Paranukus, MF Vroom and Son out of the building.

    When the gang escaped in Son's 2005 Honda Accord, "The police kirks transformed into police cars and crashed through the front of the building and out into the road still in pursuit." Son then activated a trunk-mounted machine gun firing "Little pellets of solidified purple juice", which "popped all of the tires". The narration adds that it "was truly amazing you should've been there."

    Their transformation ability has led some fans to compare them to Transformers. It is not known whether ordinary Kirks can do this too.
