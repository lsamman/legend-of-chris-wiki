    Chapters

    chapter that is after the last one but its all a flashback, known as The Flashback Chapter, is the sixth chapter of The Legend of Chris. It opens with Steve Jobs's legendary iPhone 3G keynote, introduces the Inav, and sends Hank Hill, Jesse and Walter White to the 6 7 Casino, where they are hired to guard The 3 Year Contract Plan from Verizon for Unlimited Talk and Text.

    Despite its title, only about the first thirty lines are a flashback.

    Synopsis

    The keynote (flashback)

    At the Shadow-Filtered Theater in Cupertino, Reality 42-B, Steve Jobs, in a turtleneck "woven from Mark Zuckerberg's abandoned dreams," blinds an NFT collector in Universe Prime and announces that the answer to Chris "was never in the future." A sideways, cracked iPhone 3G descends playing U2. It connects only to God's router, avoids 5G (which "awakens Elon Mush's dormant twin"), has no front camera "to avoid self-reflection", and runs the Sacred Apps: MySpace v1.3.3, Snapchat: Ghost Protocol Edition, Facebook (Pre-Cringe) and Twitter Bluebird. Siri advises: "Begin the Mixtape. Sync with the Flip Phone Covenant. And remember… never update." The stage collapses into Mountain Dew Baja Blast.

    Back in Gotham

    "Fast forward enough centuries for humans to do surgery on a grape." Club Shadowban is deserted; Jobs concludes the USB sent it to the Shadow Realm. He summons the Inav, a project with Wozniak for the Department of Awesomeness, which was dissolved the day it asked for it. Jobs parks his Zyn in the Zyn locker; Kanye receives an ice-cold Diet Dr Pepper. As Gotham disappears ("even young Sheldon predicted this"), Jobs orders Siri to the casino "to liberate the gift card."

    The 6 7 Casino

    Hank, Jesse and Walter are stranded in Dusty Depot with a damaged RV of time, out of propane and oil. Jesse visits the Quick-Check via a port-a-potty and learns of an oil casino. Walter dreams of Erger Wings. Using Hank's NFTs ("mysterious apes"), they enter the 6 7 Casino, past statues, and bet "Several NFTs on red!" The ball is stopped by Microsoft Bing, who hires them to protect a relic his son wants to steal, one that creates a field of gambling. Hank demands 15 gallons of Prime US oil. Down the Stairway to Heaven lies the Verizon contract, recovered from the ruins of the LA Walmart. Armed with golden SCARs and Red Bulls, the gang falls asleep in beach chairs.

    Title & numbering

    The title claims both to come "after the last one" and to be "all a flashback," which would place its events before the last one. The parenthetical "(This is a Flashback)" appears beneath it as a reminder. The flashback ends roughly 35 lines in with "Fast forward enough centuries," after which the chapter continues in the present for over 100 lines.

    Continuity notes

    • ChatGPT says "Aight bet boss" to Microsoft Bing at the end of the chapter, despite being in the Inav with Kanye and Jobs, not in the casino with Hank's gang.
    • The RV is called "the rv of time" here; it was the crack van in Chapter the Fourth.
    • Hank's oil supply problem is new; in his first chapter his business was propane.

    Trivia

    • The keynote parodies Steve Jobs's real 2007 iPhone launch, including the "One more thing" catchphrase.
    • Dusty Depot is a location from Fortnite.
    • The 6 7 Casino is named after the "6 7" meme.
