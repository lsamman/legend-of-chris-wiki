    The Protestant Universe

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Locations · Its Fabric was disturbed. RFK felt it.

    The Protestant Universe
    TypeUniverse
    Part ofThe Multiverse
    Notable eventA disturbance in its Fabric
    Detected byRFK
    First appearanceDunder Mifflan
    Locations

    The Protestant Universe is one of the universes of the Multiverse. It comes up once, when RFK suddenly announces at Call of Duty HQ: "I felt a disturbance in the Fabric in the Protestant universe". Someone, probably Plankton, responds: "What?! That is not poggers at all dude".

    The disturbance comes right after the death of Larry the Cucumber on 21 Jump Street, and many editors think the two are connected.

    Trivia

    • RFK's line echoes Obi-Wan Kenobi's "I felt a great disturbance in the Force" from Star Wars. RFK is a trained user of Jedi tactics.
    • Steve Jobs later says something similar: "I felt a disturbance in the iCloud".
    • "Fabric" is capitalized, which suggests it is a proper noun.
