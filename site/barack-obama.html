    Barack Obama

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Obamna. Holder of the 7 Chaos Emeralds.

    Barack Obama

    Characters

    Barack Obama, also called Obamna, is the ruler of the Barack Empire, which holds Constantinople. He is said to hold the 7 Chaos Emeralds, which made McQueen fear that defeating him might be "impossible". He is discussed at length but never actually appears.

    Contents
    1. Role
    2. Continuity errors
    3. Trivia

    Role

    Mr. Bean's plan was to flood Constantinople with oil, "lure Obamna into the center and light the nukes". The plan does not say how Obamna would be lured.

    Continuity errors

    When the plan was carried out in the Fall of Constantinople, Obama is never mentioned. Instead Mr. Bean reports: "Putin he's almost in the center". Explanations editors have offered include:

    • Vladimir Putin was standing in for Obama.
    • Putin was a guest who wandered into the trap by accident.
    • The authors forgot.

    The third explanation is the most widely accepted.

    Trivia

    • "Obamna" is a well-known internet misspelling meme.
    • The Chaos Emeralds come from the Sonic the Hedgehog series, where collecting all seven grants great power.
