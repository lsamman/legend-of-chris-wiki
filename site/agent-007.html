    Agent 007

    From The Legend Of Chris Wiki · Characters · Licensed to vape.

    Characters

    Agent 007 is an operative associated with Parliament and the "partner in crime" of Mr. Bean. Together with Lightning McQueen he forms one third of The Goober Gang, the strike team responsible for the Fall of Constantinople. He is best known for spending the majority of his canonical screen time inside a telephone booth hitting "a fat vape".

    Biography

    The briefing

    When McQueen arrived at the Smoke and Vape Shop in England, 007 was unavailable, as he was "hitting a fat vape in the telephone booth". Mr. Bean delivered the entire mission briefing in his absence. When the booth door finally opened, smoke "bursted out", and "the smell of hand sanitizer and pumpkin spice latte nicotine vape were flowing out like a waterfall." 007 then "slowly walked out" carrying a fanny pack of hand sanitizer and chewable gum "which looked like contained some form of caffeine."

    He complimented McQueen on his work in Moscow and asked whether Bean had briefed him. Bean's nervous reply ("Uh- Yes sir") suggests that 007 outranks him.

    Constantinople

    In Constantinople, 007 and Bean deployed the first two nukes "inside various bags of old pagers". During the operation 007 "lost his torso", though Mr. Bean assured McQueen that "he could be fine after I give him a few anti-depressants". By extraction time at 8aller Jump Street, 007 "was almost out of juice on his vape", and upon being rescued he "nodded as he hit another fat vape".

    Trivia

    • 007 is the codename of James Bond, the fictional British spy. The canon does not confirm whether this is the same 007.
    • Losing a torso is treated by the text as a mental-health matter rather than a medical one.
    • 007 is never shown taking part in the pager's smoke detector hoarding scheme, which is ironic given his relationship with smoke.
